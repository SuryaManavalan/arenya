# Fronts: what the world is doing while the party isn't looking

DM-owned. Updated every **world turn** (automatically every 3 scenes and at each chapter end; see
rules/dm.md § Fronts). Each front has a goal, where it operates, a ladder of escalating steps
("portents"), and its current step. When a front advances, decide what concretely happened, where,
and **what visible sign reaches the party** (news, a rift, a strange spirit, a phone call, a body).

_Seeded 2026-10-28 by the monitor from human canon and the DM's notes. Steps and goals come from the
outline and interviews; the **current step** of each front is for the DM to set on its first world
turn._

---

## 1. Typhus Quinn and Monolith: "accelerate humanity"
- **Who:** Typhus Quinn (founder). Monolith campus (Silicon Valley); leased facility at Hunters Point.
- **Goal:** fuse frontier science with spiritual technology. Rendered spirit power drives his
  interplanetary vessels and asteroid-mining fleet. He loves *humanity* more than humans and is
  detached from the body. He isn't evil.
- **Ladder** (outline and interviews `[human]`; details `[dm-proposed]`):
  1. Grieving Adonis; pushing the drive program harder; publicly calm (Beyond Earth keynote, c12).
  2. A breakthrough test: Mill output powers a prototype drive or bench test. Something in the Bay
     *flickers* (a first small rift, misread as a gas leak or mass-hysteria cluster).
  3. **Typhus learns the rendering tears rifts.** He's terrified, and hides it from the board.
  4. **Typhus learns Lattice killed Adonis** (Pryce insulated him). It's the Book 1 turning point.
     He's furious, then cold.
  5. **He embraces it as destiny.** Global media campaign: spirits are a threat and Arenya is a danger
     (or a fraud). The rifts become "a crisis only Monolith can manage."
  6. Prototype Crown and **Adonis's sharp spirit**, recovered from the Mill: Typhus becomes spiritually
     capable.
  7. **Climax**: kaiju-scale spirits loose over the Bay. Typhus is defeated and locked up, warning that
     he was only trying to protect Earth (**must hit**).
- **Current step:** _DM to set_ · **last advanced:** _—_
