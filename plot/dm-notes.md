## Opening: b1.c15 — "The Night the Light Moved" (DM decisions, built on the human's answers)
The same night, every character is touched by one event:
- **Arenya** (asleep after her lost weekend) falls into a **shared dream**, CR2-style. She stands in
  the bulk and watches Susmithaa Aunty fight a collection team. Susmithaa is radiant and terrifying,
  and she's losing. Her aunt sees her and *pushes the light into her* before the end. Arenya wakes
  to her phone ringing: her mother, the hospital. Then she hears a cockney voice from her kitchen.
- **Abernathy** arrives with the light. He's the squire of Sahasrajyoti and must now serve a host who
  thinks he's a stress hallucination.
- **Kino**, on night patrol on Mount Tamalpais, feels the Sleeping Lady *roll over in her sleep*,
  toward the city and away from him.
- **Caleb** was **on the collection team** that killed Susmithaa. The light's discharge burns
  through his Crown implant. For the first time in fourteen months he wakes up, mid-mission,
  standing over a dead woman, with no memory of choosing to be there.
- **Ava**, asleep in her Inner Sunset apartment, dreams a sliver of the same dream: a woman made of
  lamps, falling. She wakes and writes it down like a symptom.
- **Malachi**: Susmithaa was on his watch-list (a CIA analysis of "unexplained cerebral deaths").
  He's at the hospital before the family. He's now more convinced that Arenya is involved.

Spotlight the dream for Arenya. Then cut to each of the others' mornings.

## The collection pipeline (DM decisions, built on human canon)
- **Collection teams** are meta mages working in fours, made forgettable by design (that's the
  Crown's hypnotic flattening of affect). They find **powerful hosts**, kill the host's astral body in
  the bulk, and **capture the freed spirit** in a containment lattice.
- Captured spirits go to **the Mill**, Monolith's facility (location unknown to players; dm-free),
  where they're **rendered**: ground down into astral and physical power. That power feeds Typhus's
  interplanetary drive research and the manufacture of **Vesper**, the drug that lets meta mages touch
  the bulk. Every dose is part of a spirit.
- **The victims were hosts.** Yuri Melnikov hosted a spirit of the **heavy** house (an architect;
  gravity and structure). The others were hosts of various houses. Collection is clumsy: teams
  sometimes kill hosts whose spirits are too small to keep.
- **Adonis Quinn** was a latent **sharp**-house host who stumbled on Mill power-accounting records.
  He **recognized his killer** as a Monolith colleague; hence the rage, not the fear. *Typhus does not
  know his own program killed his cousin.* Collection is run beneath him (a program director the DM
  will name), insulated from him by design. Typhus learning this is a Book 1 turning point.
- **Susmithaa was the biggest prize yet:** a deity-class light spirit. The collection **failed**,
  because the bloodline tantra passed Sahasrajyoti to Arenya at the moment of death. Now the program
  wants Arenya. This is Book 1's engine.
- Typhus learns, at some point mid-book, that the rendering is what tears the **rifts**.

## Series-level secrets (human-defined; never reveal early)
- **The books are concurrent, stacked in the bulk.** Each plane is a deeper clock nested inside the
  instants of the one below. The whole series may, in some sense, happen at once. Use this for
  structure, foreshadowing and the Book 6 ending, and never state it outright before Book 6.
- **The love of Arenya's life is Malachi, "but as God."** In *Causal*, when Arenya manifests the love
  of her life to show the creator beauty, it is Malachi. The creator and Malachi are linked; the exact
  nature is TBD with the human (whether the creator appears as Malachi, or Malachi was always a face of
  the creator). Seed it softly: Malachi knows more than he should, speaks of things "older, colder,
  less patient". (Note: "Malachi" means "my messenger".)
- **Typhus's arc (Book 1):** he's not evil, but an accelerationist who loves humanity more than humans
  and is detached from the body. He discovers mid-book that his spirit-power work is causing the rifts.
  He's terrified, then he embraces it as destiny. Everything he does is one tangled frontier program:
  spirit power sources, meta mages, rifts.
