# Six-book outline (the human's own words)

Source: the human's six-book summary, imported verbatim on 2026-10-28. These are **destinations,
not scripts** (see rules/dm.md § The outline). DM and staff only; players can't read `plot/`.

| # | Title | One line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Astral | Arenya, an investigative journalist, uncovers Typhus Quinn's spirit experiments and the rifts they cause, and defeats him. |
| 2 | Cosmic | Four years later: a divided Earth faces an alien invasion; Earth is colonized; the void angel contacts Typhus. |
| 3 | Temporal | The time masters refuse to help; Abernathy gains time powers; the gang dismantles the empire in the past. |
| 4 | Ethereal | Back in the present the empire is now an allied republic; Typhus escapes; the void cult attacks; whispers of angels. |

---

Book 1, Astral: arenya defeats typhus. Spirit houses: light, dark, heavy, sharp, flexible, sticky. Arenya is an investigative journalist and begins to investigate mysterious murders. Her aunt is the current spirit host of her family’s main spirit. She tells Arenya cryptic details about some coming war. The space ceo typhus has been experimenting on spirits to use them as power sources for inter planetary vessels. Unknowingly causing rifts between the physical and astral planes. When her aunt is the next victim of these mysterious killings. The house spirit passes to Arenya and Abernathy also arrives at her side. Mysteries of the spirits and houses are revealed to her and she continues her investigation now centered on typhus. A cia agent who has also been investigating this at first thinks she’s a suspect but eventually they begin to work together. The rifts typhus has been accidentally creating are allowing spirits to leak into the physical plane and also allowing meta mages to use spirit powers in the physical plane. Typhus uses many tactics to thwart Arenya including using his global influence to paint her and all spirits as an evil but they are successful in defeating him. As he is locked up he warns of impending doom explaining that he was just trying to protect earth.

Book 2, Cosmic: Four years later The propaganda that typhus spread still has some hold even though reports have come out that typhus was responsible for the rifts. Many people are afraid spirits and don’t believe meta mages even good ones should have that much power. Some still believe typhus was a savior. Some now believe that Arenya is a savior after recent investigative reports. The world is extremely divided and not ready to unite against a common enemy. In this confusion Arenya and gang must defend earth against alien invasion using matured spirit powers but Earth is colonized (typhus begins to be contacted by void angel)

Book 3, Temporal: Arenya and gang go to plead for time masters help but are rejected. Abernathy gains time powers and takes the gang to dismantle the empire in the past. Defeat the time masters who are in league with void angel.

Book 4, Ethereal: return to present to discover the empire, now republic, is allied with Earth. Typhus has escaped from imprisonment. And whispers of angels more powerful than the most powerful spirits begin spreading. Void cult begins attacking the republic and Arenya with new allies from the republic defeat the void cult.

Book 5, Celestial: It’s now come to light that angels are real and the most dangerous one, the void angel has found its true vessel and is only a matter of time before it fully incarnates and takes over the universe. Arenya and the gang do some Indiana Jones style temple delving in far planets to uncover the secrets of the “good angel” from whom she’s been hearing whispers in her mind. They eventually find and free the angel but Arenya realizes that if she lets the angel embody her she will be no more but she has to stop the void angel so she lets it embody her. Right before that Abernathy possesses her body and acts as a buffer allowing her spirit and ether to escape but killing himself in the process. In the final battle between the “good angel” and the void angel. The “good angel” is struck down and destroyed.

Book 6 Causal: Arenya’s ether is afloat in the astral sea. While the void angel regains total power of the republic recreating the empire and devotes all resources to creating the technology to harness the void (what destroyed his previous vessel) he finally perfects the technology and has to power to challenge even the creator. While many lesser angels already died trying to prevent this. Arenya floats disembodied and witnesses all these events powerless. Thousands of years pass and finally all the angels are defeated and the void angle is able to perfect his void harnessing technology. He begins to use it to reshape the universe in his image. But he discovers that void is uncontainable and it begins to spread and consume all of reality. Eventually everything is consumed even the void angel.

As life on the final planet in the universe is about to be extinguished. She prays and wishes that she could fight for them in order to prove that creation is worth it. Suddenly we are in the point of view of a woman on that planet she explains her life as a denizin of the final sanctuary of life in the universe and how they have a plan to stop the void angel once and for all. One of the void angels original followers from the empire who was blessed with eternal life has saught refuge here and has the knowledge to reverse the technology that he helped build in the first place to help the void angel harness the void. They build their weapon and prepare for war. Along the way she falls in love and has a family. Even in the most inhospitable and cruel conditions imaginable, love finds a way. When the void angel arrives to their sector they are defeated and the void consumes all

Everything except The disembodied ethereal singularity of Arenya who bears witness to the destruction of all reality until the infinite timeless moment is reached where all that exists is void and a singularity. She is that singularity. But yet she can sense something other than herself. “How are you here?” Says a booming voice. She is speaking to the creator. The creator explains how this was all part of his plan and that witnessing all of the suffering in his creation he believes it’s better if all is void. He would rather live in blood in the nothingness of the void. Arenya tells him that is impossible because there will never be all void. It is the voids inherent limit that she will always exist. The singularity, she is the will of creation. Arenya shows him that despite the suffering, there is beauty she manifests the love of her life and shows the creator the beauty. As they kiss, he understands and Arenya is reincarnated three days after her body was destroyed now with the full power of the “good angel” who was too arrogant. She defeats the void angel and gives up her power to seal the rifts and live a happy life with her love.
