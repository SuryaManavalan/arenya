# Interviews with the human

Transcripts or faithful condensed notes of worldbuilding conversations between the DM and the human,
one file per session: `YYYY-MM-DD-<topic>.md`. These are **primary sources**. Where they disagree with
other files, they outrank everything except a later interview or a `decree`.

Interviews happen in one of two ways:
- **In the game:** in the `human` channel (the DM awaits the human, who answers with `arenya reply`).
  The DM still writes a condensed note here afterward.
- **Live:** the human talks directly with Claude Code, which embodies the DM. The full conversation is
  condensed here, and the results are written into `world/`, `library/` (through the Lore Keeper's
  conventions) and `plot/`.
