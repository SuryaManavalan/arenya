## Round 1 — foundations

**Q1. Are the book titles a ladder of planes?** Yes. *Astral, Cosmic, Temporal, Ethereal, Celestial,
Causal* are the layers of reality, and each book opens a new one ("with new levels come new devils").
The physical world is at the bottom.

**Q2. The six spirit houses** (light, dark, heavy, sharp, flexible, sticky).
- Spirits live in the astral/spirit plane, the plane closest to the physical. They are metaphysical
  entities that **emerge from the abstract archetypal characteristics, properties and mechanics of
  physical reality**, anthropomorphized on that subtler plane.
- Sticky is also akin to **magnetism**.
- Arenya is somehow **a child of light and dark**: possibly adopted into one because of where she was
  born, versus where her family is from. The spirits of her current environment aren't the ones her
  lineage formed in, but they adopted her anyway, and she became their champion, even more than
  "the true champion" of those spirits.

**Q3. Hosting.** Doesn't need to be fully fleshed out yet; we'll figure it out over time. It's
**both bloodline and reincarnation**. These families are esoterically advanced, with high **tantra
technology** that tied their spirit lines to their bloodlines.

**Q5 (answered alongside Q3). The freezing deaths are spirit battles.** They aren't fought in the 3D
physical plane but in a higher-dimensional plane, where D&D fighting mechanics can come in. An entire
extra dimension, more complex and subtle, lives on top of reality. During a spirit battle the
combatants' whole energy and astral bodies are fully focused there, so their physical bodies have no
motion. Mathematical inspiration: not one extra dimension but **three dimensions plus a second set of
three extra dimensions**. See `world/reference/extra-dimensional-time-notes.md` ("not tied too
strongly, a similar vibe").

**Q4. Abernathy.** A disembodied entity stripped of many parts of his being: his physical body, but
also his **causal body** and other infrastructure a normal entity needs. That makes him super unique
and odd. **SECRET** (players and readers won't learn it for a very long time): he was once part of the
**Time Masters** and is in hiding. His subtle job for the family is to hide by pretending to be one of
the spirits of this lineage and domain. He acts like a **squire**. The family's main spirit is an
incomprehensibly complex entity, akin to a dragon, an angel or a Hindu deity.

**Q6. Bodies and the astral plane.** Spirit, ether and the rest are increasingly subtle bodies; a
fully incarnated entity needs all of them. The astral plane is **not a mirror** of San Francisco.
It's an emergent (or pre-emergent) world: the characteristics, properties and archetypes of the
physical world, anthropomorphized into their own plane. There are shadows and projections of
physical reality (tree spirits near trees, tinkering intelligent entities near AI research labs), but
it's far more complex and abstract than a mirror.

**Q7. Perception and meta mages.**
- Seeing a spirit, even one that has come through a rift, should feel like **psychosis**. It is so
  violent to a normal person's perception of reality that they treat it as psychosis, especially with
  large spirits. Spirits should not read like ordinary animals or creatures.
- **Meta mages** are mass-produced, so Typhus has a larger army. They have a **weaker connection** to
  the spirit realm, and they're dependent rather than truly spiritual people. They rely on a made-up
  **chemical** plus a **neural-link chip** that induces hypnosis, along with other modern new-age
  spiritual technologies (think marijuana users and hypnosis-tech users), made more magical, mystical
  and sci-fi. Individually weaker than true hosts, but in numbers they can take down true spiritual
  people.

**Q8. The table.** Malachi and Abernathy will become big characters. Saiesh will fade out. The DM
should find a few more good characters to join the party for Book 1. Main characters can change as
the books progress.

## Round 2

**Q1. Light and dark.** San Francisco's land lineage rolls up indigenous American beliefs (the
peoples who lived here, plus Olmec and Maya). The DM decides the rest and reports back. (The DM's
proposal is in its reply and in `world/mechanics.md`, tagged `[dm-proposed]`.)

**Q2. The passed-over champion.** Undecided. The DM decides, or it emerges once the game is underway.

**Q3. Clocks.** The DM's suggestion is accepted: physical time slows hugely during a spirit battle
rather than stopping, and hosts (or anyone a spirit carries) can enter a battle midway. Game
mechanics are needed for it.

**Q4. The frozen body and losing.** Stab a frozen body and "you're probably cooked." Losing a spirit
battle **doesn't always kill**. The combatant's whole life force (**prana**) is pointed into their
astral and higher bodies and away from the physical, which is like being fully embodied in the astral,
as in astral travel during sleep. Think **kundalini**: which **chakra** they're currently embodied in
shows their level. The victims died because the assassins **killed their astral body**, a crucial
part of a living being. The outcome depends on what happens in the astral battle or other astral
activity, and it isn't always death.

**Q5. Typhus.** His projects are all tied together. He's at the frontier where traditional
scientific technology feels maxed out, and he's fusing it with spiritual technology and esoteric
practice, a whole new domain with infinite threads to pull, so his hands are full. He doesn't
realize he's causing the rifts until one moment. At first he doesn't want them. He isn't evil. He's
trying to accelerate humanity. Like Elon Musk, he cares about *humanity* more than humans, and
he's even more detached from the physical body and our animal nature than most. He believes
effective accelerationism is correct. When he learns he's causing the rifts he's scared, then
decides it may be his destiny and the path humanity must embrace.

**Q6. Tone and lethality.** **Arenya, Malachi and Abernathy can't die**, or if they do, the other
players must do everything they can to revive them, because they're the main characters.

**Q7. The love of her life.** *"I think it will inevitably be Malachi, but as God."* (SECRET; see
`plot/dm-notes.md`.)

## Round 3 — the opening

**Q1. The party.** The DM chooses. → Arenya, Malachi, Abernathy, plus the DM's picks: the passed-over
champion, Ava, and a freed meta mage. Saiesh and the rest are DM-voiced NPCs.

**Q2. Susmithaa's death.** Arenya **witnesses it in a dream**, in the style of Critical Role
campaign 2's dream sequences.

**Q3. The assassins.** They collect **powerful spirits** for the new esoteric cybernetic technology,
which (very simply) **grinds powerful spirits down into usable physical and astral power**. The
easiest way to find powerful spirits, for now, is through powerful hosts. There may be more powerful
spirits tied to nature, earth, science and higher realms that aren't knowable yet.

**Q4. Malachi.** Actually **CIA**. Later, when the esoteric and magical comes into the open, he
creates the government organization that works on it (Book 2, perhaps).

**Q5. Abernathy's voice.** A weird **cockney accent that almost seems fake or forced**, a bit jarring
and annoying. **Only Arenya can perceive him.**

**Q6. Railroading.** Book endings and beginnings must be hit. Major plot points should be hit as
much as possible.

**Addendum (Abernathy).** His past before serving the lineage is **blurry even to him**. He'll start
to remember later, through events the DM throws at the party in future books, at the DM's discretion.

## During play (after b1.c17.s10): the range of spirits
The human likes the Counter and signature mechanic, and adds:
- **Spirits come in all shapes, sizes, forms, non-forms, abstract forms**, up to incomprehensible
  entities: not just human-bodied or human spirits, but **nature spirits, archetypal spirits,
  characteristic spirits**, and unimaginably abstract kinds.
- **The six houses are like attractors in the space of spirits**: domains that pull spirits of every
  kind toward light, dark, heavy, sharp, flexible or sticky.
- Introduce spirits through what people already understand (human spirits), then **steadily make them
  stranger and more interesting**. Book 1 is all about the **astral plane, where spirits live**, and
  it should **climax with massive, kaiju-like spirits**.
