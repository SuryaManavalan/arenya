# The freezing deaths

Cluster of sudden deaths in San Francisco over ~a month: victims freeze (30–60 s; 25 s on Yuri's video), expression fixed (horror, tears, recognition, rage), then collapse; officially aneurysm/cerebral failure; Adonis's was spun as overdose [c02, c03, c08]. Seven victims before Adonis; none before that month [c02]. No CCTV at the bakery besides the vendor feed; the laptop group left unseen [c02]. 23 days of nothing, then Adonis [c08].

**Truth (to be revealed):** they are spirit battles. See [[the-bulk-and-spirit-battles]] and [[collection-program]].
