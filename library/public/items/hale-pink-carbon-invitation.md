# Hale's Pink Carbon ("H")

A pink carbon copy from a two-part form, no envelope, folded in thirds, found Wed 4 Nov ~10:58 a.m. in Nana Itzel's Sunset mailbox between a PG&E bill and a pizza menu. The hand is shaky and pressed hard enough to emboss the paper. [#1292]

## Text
> *Thursday, after the low water. They want her off the hill and onto the flats. I can tell you where they'll set it. I need you to tell El Contador I helped. Colma, the 110 stop, tonight 7. Come alone or don't come.  —H*

## Details
- Offers the Thursday (5 Nov) lift site: "off the hill and onto the flats" (Tam to the Bay flats) in exchange for being vouched for to [[el-contador-rumor]]. [#1292]
- Arenya's frostbitten wrist went cold on reading; Abernathy silent, later: he "doesn't like how much I want to believe it." [#1292, #1297]
- Read by: Arenya, Malachi ("People who ask for that are usually already guilty of something worse"), Kino (smelled: pencil dust, cold stairwell, no smoke; "wrote it on his knee, standing"), Ava (photographed flat with a coin; a two-part form comes from a pad). All: fear or theater. [#1293-#1299]
- "H" = Hale is the group's working assumption; may be someone using his name. Hale, Caleb heard in the van, said "walk it off, Voss, we're burned" (s01).
- Plan: Malachi at the Colma 110 stop at 7 p.m. (Caleb in the car, not driving), Arenya/Kino/Ava at Second piling 8:56. [#1302]

## Outcome (b1.c19.s03)
- "H" came: Hale met Malachi at the 110 stop 7:05 p.m. and gave the site ([[drydock-pump-house-crisp]]). Carbon's "off the hill and onto the flats" = Hunters Point flats. See [[daniel-hale]].
