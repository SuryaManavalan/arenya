# Ledger Stakes and Truck D2-07

## Details
- **Stakes:** iron stakes in the physical mud, placed at low water, anchoring the bulk's "ledger lines" that drag the slack-water spirit NE. Pulling them frees her lines. [#1134]
- **Truck:** white box truck, CA commercial plate 8ZK-41(…), stenciled fleet unit **D2-07**; Caleb reads D2 as "Drop Two." Arrived on Harney Way at ~7:29, parked 200 yds out lights off, engine idling; workers out at slack water 7:31. [#1135, #1144, #1153, #1157]
- **Roll-up door:** white, lifts ~2 ft; hums through Caleb's scar, warming it and snapping his 11-second count onto the tide's beat ("tuned" him); warmth leaves when it lowers. His count went ragged/his own again near Daly City. Kino's ruled arm ticks in time. [#1144, #1153, #1158]
- **Workers:** two, grey steel-toe boots, logo-less hi-vis, headlamps, fiberglass survey rod with a cable to the box, tablet. One set the rod left of the second piling (spirit lost an inch; phones flickered, dash clock skipped). The other: ~40, clean-shaven, "Safeway-manager" face, waved, said the lot is port property after dusk, "keep that arm warm, son," and tapped the car roof twice, same as he tapped the tablet (a "stamp"). [#1153, #1157]
- Party has video (Ava's phone, 7:33): faces, plate, unit number. Caleb will tell Malachi all he knows about D2 at Tuesday 1 p.m. [#1151, #1156, #1157]
