# Dalal's Glasses and the Blue Jar

Dalal-ji's wrapped reading glasses (cord uncoiled, left on the lamp table at the Fremont hall; Malachi never put them on) were carried by Malachi in a knotted handkerchief. They greyed a hand's width of Nana's salt [#0882, #0883].

## Details
- Nana put them, cloth and all, into an empty lidded blue jar; Arenya wiped it and held it open. On the lid going down the glass misted and a single pencil-tick (*Tk*) sounded. Jar sits on the top shelf, a hand's width from [[susmithaa-ash-canister]], not touching. [#0884, #0887]
- Nana: "The glasses. Not him." Dalal "keeps names" and has "lost his eyes"; the man at the bottom of the hill will come for them first. [#0887, #0892]
- **Bond (Arenya's view via Abernathy):** a hair-fine grey thread runs from the jar through the wall, downhill west/south to the **110 bus stop** at the bottom of Hillcrest; taut and still. Abernathy's guess (unconfirmed): someone felt a name shut in a box; "we've been counted again." Advice: salt it, don't cut it (cutting says you can feel it). [#0888, #0890, #0900]
- **Nana's terms for the trade (Sun 1 Nov):** (1) jar set on the curb on her clay comal's wooden side, not handed over, and the runner lifts it off the wood himself; nothing crosses the salt by hand, he sets it down and walks backward; (2) the family's copy of the form never leaves the house; he must close the file on his own paper, striking the line with his own pencil and saying aloud that he's done it; (3) nobody says the dead girl's name to him. Kino adds: Nana says yes first. [#0896, #0898, #0900]
- Caleb: a runner takes the **stub**, which tells the van the canister was delivered so the lift counts and the file closes; a carried-forward form stays open and the tick beside L. Sowri picks the next person. [#0897]
