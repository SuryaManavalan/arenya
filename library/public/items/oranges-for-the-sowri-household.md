# Crate of Oranges

Left anonymously at the Colma notary office (Rodel) on Wed 4 Nov, "for the Sowri household, with no card." Rodel refused it and told Arenya by voicemail (9:15 a.m.), frightened "in a polite way." [#1292]

## Details
- Arenya told him not to accept, touch or open it; photograph from the door. His photo shows perfect fruit, every orange facing the door. [#1293, #1302]
- Echoes [[rodel-landline-message]]: Dalal "bought oranges" after selling a page to a grey suit. Link unconfirmed.
