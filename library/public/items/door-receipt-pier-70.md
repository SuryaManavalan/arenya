# Door receipt (Pier 70, Bldg 12)

A strip of warm thermal receipt paper slid under the door of Arenya's Mission studio on Sunday 8 Nov, after a single knee-height knock by the Missing Man's doorway, at about 19:10. [#1508]

## Contents
- Printed in the faint grey of a dying cartridge: *c/o Lucero, I.* A line of dots. *Door: Pier 70, Bldg 12, side. 23:40.* [#1508]
- Below it, in different ink, as if added by hand: *He will not ask twice. Neither will the chair.* [#1508]
- No signature line and no tick. [#1508]

## Notes
- It is the first place and hour the party has been given for the Sunday move. The envoy's door was 11:40 p.m. [[tide-envoy]] [#1454]; this slip says 23:40 for the same night. [#1508]
- The "c/o Lucero, I." line matches the care-of line on [[custody-receipt-proximal-11]]. [#0841]
- Nobody accepted it. Arenya answered only the name, and the doorway asked for the sender. Nothing was signed or held. [#1502, #1509]

## Open questions
- Who "I. Lucero" is. The custody receipt's care-of name is Nana Itzel's surname, and the canister sits on her shelf. Ollie, under the Crown, says "He's the one who reads it aloud," which points at a man. Unresolved. [#1509]
- "He will not ask twice" and "the chair" refer to the same Chair as the Crown's "Pryce's chair." Not yet linked.
