# Malachi's Name Lease

What became of Malachi's sold true name (see [[corinne-abate]]), per Hale.

## Details
- Hale "sold it to a lease," not a face: **Bayline Marine Services** booked it as an **asset**, paid in **water rights**; guarantor **Applied Field Research**, signed one letter, "**M.**" Hale never met M. [#1315] (Compare "M." on the Tunnel Ave lease, Bayline Materials LLC, c19.s02: likely the same signature habit; not stated.)
- Purpose: "a man who could talk to things that live under the Bay"; Malachi's name is "a key." [#1315]
- The asset is **Malachi**, not the Bay ("The Bay already belongs to itself"). If he won't talk, or says the wrong thing, the paper is worthless; **a lease breaks if the asset won't perform.** Hale can't buy it back. [#1315, #1318]
- **Intermediary:** woman in a grey suit met at the Embarcadero; pen that wrote in salt; **green sea-glass ring on her thumb**, **lanyard worn backwards**, **smelled of pool chlorine**; walked to the Ferry Building and took the **Alameda ferry**. Hale had eleven seconds of her and a cleared check. [#1315, #1318] Echoes the backwards lanyard on [[grey-nissan-7kbq4r2]] and "a woman who keeps names."

## b1.c19.s04 (Wed 4 Nov, Second piling)
- **Fleet sedan MO-0114**, white MILL OPERATIONS decal, Alameda marina hangtag; woman at wheel (green ring, backwards lanyard, chlorine per Arenya's notes) = presumably the intermediary. She wrote **SOLD** in salt on her dash for Malachi, unsurprised and "a little sorry for him," then held up a **Bayline intake sheet with one signature line circled in red, blank**, and drove toward the marina road. [#1333, #1341] Malachi's "Who is M?" went unanswered.
- The clerk, light stuttering on Malachi like a scanner on an already-rung barcode: "Permit's on file under your name, sir. You're the collateral." "The instrument is executed Thursday. You'll be asked to sign the blank. Everyone is." [#1341, #1346] (Fits Hale: lease breaks if the asset won't perform.)
- Malachi's DoE cover ("Federal, Department of Energy") used; he demanded the instrument; clerk gave "a small, tired, almost collegial nod." [#1339, #1343]
