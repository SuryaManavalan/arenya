# Pink archana slip

Small pink slip, folded once, of the kind a temple office tears off for each archana. Found by Amma inside her left brown Clark after Kino brought the shoes out. Text, blue ballpoint, neat hand: **ARCHANA — SRI VENKATESWARA — SAT 10/31 / NAME: LAKSHMI SOWRI · STAR: ROHINI / PAID ✓.**

## Details
- Dated the same day, in Amma's own name and star: an archana "paid" for her by someone else, the same device used on Susmithaa (check 2207). [#0821]
- Amma: "I did not ask for this." She refolded it on the same crease and handed it flat on her palm to Arenya "the way you'd hand over something hot." Arenya now holds it. [#0821]
- Planted while the shoes sat on the rack; the oxfords were the only unaccounted footwear. Dalal-ji's blue ballpoint matches the ink (unconfirmed).
