# Susmithaa's brass lamp

The brass window lamp Susmithaa kept lit for 31 years; it went out with oil in it when she died.

## Details
- At Seton: wrapped in a hospital blanket "like a child"; glass chimney has a hairline crack. Amma refused to surrender it to staff [#0072].
- Unlit, wick black, oil cold, yet the brass warms against Arenya's touch; Abernathy perceives it as "listening," not empty [#0073, #0082].
- Its warmth "gathers" toward the side facing Arenya's closed right fist; at 5:51 her fist loosens a quarter-inch [#0084, #0092].
- Malachi noticed it and chose not to comment [#0080].
- The lamp must not leave the family room (Arenya's order) [#0085].

## Open questions
- Relation of this lamp to the shrine lamp Ava saw (#0050) is unconfirmed.
