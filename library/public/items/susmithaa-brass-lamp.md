# Susmithaa's brass lamp

The brass window lamp Susmithaa kept lit for 31 years; it went out with oil in it when she died.

## Details
- At Seton: wrapped in a hospital blanket "like a child"; glass chimney has a hairline crack. Amma refused to surrender it to staff [#0072].
- Unlit, wick black, oil cold, yet the brass warms against Arenya's touch; Abernathy perceives it as "listening," not empty [#0073, #0082].
- Its warmth "gathers" toward the side facing Arenya's closed right fist; at 5:51 her fist loosens a quarter-inch [#0084, #0092].
- Malachi noticed it and chose not to comment [#0080].
- The lamp must not leave the family room (Arenya's order) [#0085].

## b1.c15.s06 (Sowri house)
- Lit by Appa with a kitchen match; sits on the low shelf of the puja alcove beside Susmithaa's garlanded drugstore-framed photo. [#0097, #0101]
- Flame leans flat toward Kino as he passes with no draft; trembles between Kino and Arenya; later stands upright "like a guard dog" behind Kino; stretches an inch toward the *Curtain* shelf; **splits in two for one second** when Arenya holds the receipt ("two people standing at a trailhead"). [#0101, #0105, #0115, #0137, #0140]
- Nana: "The lamp knows who... it knows who the light belongs to now," but never seen a house-flame split; meaning unknown. [#0143]
- Appears to reverse the earlier "unlit" state: it is burning. (Susmithaa's window lamp went out at her death [#0014]; Amma and Appa relit it.) [#0097]

## Open questions
- Relation of this lamp to the shrine lamp Ava saw (#0050) is unconfirmed.
