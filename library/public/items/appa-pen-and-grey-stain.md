# Appa's pen, the borrowed R, and the grey stain

How the Lattice got a "signatory on file."

## Details
- **Green Sheaffer** fountain pen: Appa's, used for Arenya's college forms; he now buttons it in his shirt pocket ("Mine"). [#0796, #0801]
- **19 March, Rodel's counter:** Rodel's chained ballpoint skipped. A polite man in a navy coat (Hale) holding a mailbox key offered his own pen cap-first. Appa wrote the **"R"** of his witness signature in that ink; Susmithaa took the pen back ("thank you, we have our own"), then steadied Appa's wrist as he finished "Sowri" in the Sheaffer. The signature on her declaration is thus half Hale's ink. Rodel phoned an hour later that the man had rented a box. [#0798]
- **Grey stain:** pencil-lead grey, even, along inside of Appa's right forefinger and thumb pad; there since March, never washes off, cold to the touch only there. Kino: "a mark, not a wound… they lent you theirs." Arenya picked up a little on her thumb from the door. [#0798, #0799, #0800, #0802]
- **Envelope:** manila, sealed, no stamp or postmark, block capitals "R. SOWRI — NEXT OF KIN"; left on the step 5 a.m. along a ruled grey-dust line. Unopened; Kino carries it under Nana's blanket with salt on top (don't carry it, burn it, or open it before Nana sees it). [#0796, #0798, #0800, #0803]
- Arenya's theory: signing would close the "custody chain" for the Lattice, tied to the Sun 1 Nov notice ([[lost-property-notice-sunday]]); the fog-writing on the gas meter read "R.S. — SUN 9:00." [#0799, #0805]
