# INTAKE EXCEPTION 0001, NO SOURCE

An object the Mill's intake logged at 9:14 p.m. Tue 3 Nov that it could not classify; the system has never thrown that error before. Held in a lockbox in the Lantern control room, two floors above Typhus's coil. Typhus told Arenya on Wed 4 Nov. [#1280]

## Details
- Abernathy's left wrist went cold in a ring the exact size of his lost brass cuff at "no source"; 9:14 p.m. matches when the cuff would reach the stack (his reasoning). So it is almost certainly his cuff. [#1281, #1287]
- Arenya kept this off her face and did not tell Typhus. [#1282]

- **Physical (Typhus, Wed 4 Nov 9:05 p.m. call):** 14×6 cm woven textile, non-conductive, reads four degrees under ambient and does not equalize; intake form: "textile, provenance unknown." [#1360]
- **Lockbox L-3**, Lantern control room, under the rack console. Typhus's badge can't open it. Elias: the key is not a person but a role, **"M-Ops"**; he wouldn't say more on a work phone. [#1360, #1352]
- Arenya's answer to Typhus: "It's something's livery, from somewhere the room never was"; belongs to someone who lost it. She asked him to time any attempt to move the box before Friday. [#1361]
- Abernathy: "I know that kind of box," a pang at "L-3, under the rack console"; uneasy; doesn't know how he knows. [#1363]
