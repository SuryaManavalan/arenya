# Bell's 1912 dime

A worn 1912 dime Mr. Bell paid with at [[tartine-bakery]]; Kino bagged it in a coffee sleeve. Abernathy's reading: Bell did not pay with it but "spent a marker" and wanted it in Kino's pocket.

## Details
- Thu ~7:20: sleeve gone cold; then a dime-sized ring of damp soaked through the cardboard though the morning was mild; Ix refuses to look at it. [#0353, #0357]
- Abernathy: it isn't leaking, it's "answering" (kept old things sweat when told where they are); Bell uses it "like a window"; the palm dot warms when it sweats. Handle by cloth, not skin or phone; **do not throw it in the bay** (it would be a "post," like his note). [#0362]
- Wrapped in Arenya's scarf (3 turns) plus the napkin, carried inside Kino's jacket away from skin. Kino: "Wet is a signature." [#0360, #0363]
- Past Glen Park on BART both dot and dime went quiet (out of range). [#0367]
- Plan: take it to [[nana-itzel]] ("she'll know what a 1912 dime wants"), not the freezer. [#0355]

## b1.c16.s05 "Cal" — destroyed
- Nana's options: lime (face lost for good, Bell blinded), spend it in a till (hands the cold spot to a cashier), or keep it wrapped. A paid coin is "the till's," so Bell was "cheating" through a closed door. Her rule: Kino's *gracias* to Hale is "a receipt, not a contract." Barber dime, SF mint "S". [#0376]
- Arenya copied *1912 / S / Liberty* into her notebook first. Kino dropped it face down into Nana's *cal* with tongs. It hissed, a grey ring spread (the freezer-salt grey), and the bead of damp pulled down. A knit-cuff rib print showed in the grey: Bell leaning on the window to the last. [#0380-#0382]
- Result: the dime is blind and dead; the cal is spoiled for pozole and sealed in a coffee can. [#0382]
