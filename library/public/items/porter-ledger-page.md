# The Porter Page and Abernathy's Brass Cuff

## Details
- At Second piling (Tue 3 Nov, ~7:48 p.m.) the second stake's ruled line read Abernathy back after his strike-through missed, and opened a ledger page beside him in the air. Arenya saw it only through the bond. [#1218]
- Page: old copperplate hand, a heading with the top line smudged, one legible word, **Porter**, and a date column running down past where she could read. The line "leaned toward him like a pen nib finding its place." [#1218]
- When the stake came out and the line went slack, the page **shut** and the writing folded away. [#1227]
- Abernathy says Porter is not a name he gave Arenya or knows, but "something in me said *yes* before I could say *no*." He asked to explain later, not on the mud. Arenya wrote on bond that she'd ask after. [#1221, #1222, #1231]
- **Brass cuff:** the brass-buttoned cuff of his livery. He unbuttoned it and left it on the line to escape. It rode the reeling line toward the Hunters Point stack; nobody saw it go under. Arenya, Ava (written down "so it's official") and Abernathy all hold that they will recover it. [#1221, #1227, #1229, #1232, #1235]
- Link: the s03 slack-water spirit said "somebody tore your page on purpose"; this is the first page of his anyone has seen.
