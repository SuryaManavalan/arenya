# Mill truck D2-04

A white box truck, Mill livery scraped off the door. Part of the Drop Two trucks (see [[ledger-stakes-and-d2-07]]); D2-04 is also the box number on the Sunday receipt ("Box D2-04, Oct 29", [[custody-receipt-proximal-11]]).

## Details
- **Sun 8 Nov, 23:41–23:42, Pier 70 bay 3:** pulled out engine-warm when the Bldg 12 side door opened. Inés Arriaga: it had until 23:42 to leave. [#1527, #1530]
- Turned south on Illinois without a signal. Its route was not the Mill dispatch route: it took a sequence of turns no Mill site requires, ending on an unmapped rail spur past the crane footing and the tank farm (Arenya, Investigation 23). [#1532]
- Stopped at a chain-link dead end of rusted track. Rear door raised; contained a bolted chair facing the door and [[lockbox-l-3]].
- Left with its keys on the bumper, driver [[pruitt]] gone on foot.
- Tailed by Malachi (Stealth 12; made). [#1533, #1534]
