# Frost-tagged sympathy card

Plain white sealed envelope, no name, left by [[mr-bell]] on the sofa arm. Never opened (as of s06). Cold "like a dead battery," unwarmed after four hours. [#0127, #0132]

## Details
- Abernathy sees a fine frost-light thread from the envelope's corner trailing toward the back door: a **tag**, like a bird-ring. Whoever holds the other end knows where the card, and whoever holds it, is. Cutting it would snap clean but "ring" at the far end. [#0130, #0133]
- Kino can't see the thread but sees the card's shadow pointing at the hall. [#0136]
- Nana wrapped it in her damp pozole towel and put it at the back of the Sowri freezer behind parathas and peas: "Cold hides in cold... We don't cut it. Cutting is a word... We just bore them." [#0137]
- Arenya's earlier plan to keep it in her bag was overridden; it must not go upstairs or near Susmithaa's phone. [#0131, #0133]
