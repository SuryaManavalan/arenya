# Susmithaa's Ash Canister

Green steel, thermos-cap size; reads "full" but holds only ash ("her leftovers," not a spirit) [c15.s02]. Caleb carried it in a duffel, stole it from the Drop Two van run, and ticked like a watch.

## Details
- Ticked at Ava's left-palm cold spot and with her ache (s03). In s04 it skipped when Arenya crossed the threshold; slowed through Caleb's confession; on "It's hers" its tick matched Arenya's exactly. [#0677, #0682, #0693]
- Kino's salt crept toward it in a crescent, as toward something "owed." [#0693, #0695]
- Arenya laid her left palm on the duffel: ticks in time, slowed her pulse, smell of boiled filter coffee and cold cream, then it "settled like a kettle off the flame." Ava's cold cleared entirely. [#0696, #0697]
- Abernathy felt a warm, familiar presence sit on the bond (does not seem to have noticed him); later "still there, sat against your palm… listening" to the Nissan. [#0698, #0699, #0726]
- Rule: nobody signs, nobody receives; Arenya "is moving her." Destination: Nana Itzel's house. Rides upright in Arenya's lap in Kino's truck. [#0707, #0727]
