# Lost-Property Notice (Sun 11/1)

Text on Caleb's dead prepaid (battery physically out, in Malachi's coat) on Fri 30 Oct at 11:14 PM, from a number of all zeros — the "Shepherd line."

> LOST PROPERTY — CUSTODY CHAIN INCOMPLETE.
> RECEIPT TO BE COUNTERSIGNED BY NEXT OF KIN.
> SIGNATORY ON FILE: R. SOWRI.
> SUN 11/1. WE WILL COME TO HIM.

## Details
- Buzzed at ~Fri night in Malachi's pocket; Malachi laid it unread beyond the salt, then wrapped it in a wet dishtowel under Nana's clay comal ringed with grey salt; the glow went out. Read Saturday morning with Nana present. Screen cold as creek stone. [#0766–#0772, #0783]
- Shepherd texts "so you know he's already watching what you do next," not to say where he's going. [#0772]
- "Next of kin" = [[ravi-sowri]] (Raghavan); "countersigned" echoes the pink carbon "…CEIVED BY:" scrap on Susmithaa's gate. Malachi's read: they need him alive and holding a pen; ask about the pen, not the paper. [#0787]
- Phone now under the comal at Nana's with the Vesper packs. [#0790]
