# Custody Receipt, REF PROXIMAL/11

The sealed "R. SOWRI — NEXT OF KIN" envelope left at the Sowri door (s06). It was opened on Nana's salt line on Sat 31 Oct, 9:5x a.m., after Appa chose to know.

## Opening (Nana's rite)
- Nothing "sealed with their glue" enters Nana's house. It is opened half on and half off the salt, carried in the non-signing hand away from the chest, and opened with wood (her black comal spatula). The reader is the named person or whoever stands for the name. [#0836]
- It ticked in clusters while sealed and went silent when laid on the salt. The glue "peeled" dry and did not tear. [#0832, #0838, #0841]
- Appa said yes ("I don't want to not know any more"), with Amma and Arenya on either side. Kino opened it. [#0841]

## Contents
Carbon set, white over pink, folded in thirds:
- Title: **RETURN OF PERSONAL EFFECTS — CUSTODY RECEIPT**. REF: **PROXIMAL / 11**.
- ITEM: one (1) container, sealed; CONTENTS: SOWRI, SUSMITHAA; CONDITION: intact. [#0841]
- PRESENT LOCATION: **c/o LUCERO, I.** (Nana Itzel's surname; the canister on her shelf). [#0841, #0843]
- RECEIVED BY (NEXT OF KIN): pre-written pencil **R**, soft grey like Appa's stain, with a tremor in the downstroke (the steadied wrist of March).
- ALTERNATE SIGNATORY: **L. SOWRI ✓**, a small neat teacher's check in the same grey pencil.
- Fine print: "On countersignature this entry is closed and any balance carried forward to the next proximal reference."
- Stamp: faded round ink, a shepherd's crook in a circle.
- The pink copy's lower-left corner is torn off. Malachi's curb scrap is believed to match; he says the torn edges will fit the stub in the runner's book. [#0846]

## Threads (seen by Abernathy only)
- A taut, *set* grey thread runs from the R into Appa's stained right thumbnail. It lives in his skin and the salt only slows it. [#0842, #0847]
- A slack thread ran from the check to Amma's left foot, where the [[pink-archana-slip-lakshmi]] had been. It was tightening. When Arenya took off the shoe and sock, it went limp ("Now"). It is frosted by the salt but not cut. [#0842, #0850]

## Handling and status
- Fire took Amma's sock (pencil-smoke smell) but did not change the writing: "fire takes the thing, not the writing." [#0855]
- Nana set her stone pestle on the folded form, on the salt, half in and half out. Nobody carries it and nobody burns it until the effect of burning is known. [#0851]
- Table theory (Arenya, Kino; unconfirmed): unsigned, the entry rolls ("balance carried forward") to the next name. The slip made Amma the backup line. [#0843, #0845]
- Deadline: the runner is expected Sun 1 Nov, 9:00 a.m. Appa is to hold no pen. His Sheaffer sits in Nana's clay jar. [#0849, #0855, #0859]

## Closed (b1.c17.s11, Sun 1 Nov ~8:47 a.m.)
- [[the-runner]] struck the R line and the alternate aloud ("Opened in error. Issuer's ink. Returned up the line. Closed."). Afterwards the family's copy under Nana's pestle bore a grey stroke through the R and "L. SOWRI ✓" that nobody drew (it gave a *tk*). [#0941]
- Effects: Amma's hand came out of her pocket empty; Appa's grey stain receded to a **crescent under the nail**; Caleb's scar went cool. [#0941]
- Balance returned up the line to the issuer of the ink (Hale's office); runner carries it "on his lap" on the 9:12. [#0941]
- Nana did not sweep the salt where the runner's toes stopped; she covered it. [#0950]

## Possible link: "someone who can sign" (b1.c20.s03, Sat 7 Nov) [#1475, #1478]
- Ollie, under the Crown, said "Sunday. Bring someone who can sign," and half-saw a beige car and a receipt read aloud. Arenya privately links "sign" to the alternate-signatory line (L. SOWRI ✓). Abernathy's cuff warmed at "sign," and he is holding back what he suspects. Unconfirmed.

## Read aloud at the flat (b1.c20.s04, Sun 8 Nov, dusk)
- Arenya reads the line whole at her Mission studio: "c/o Lucero, I." then dots, then "Alternate signatory. L. Sowri." and a tick. Box number **D2-04**, date **Oct 29**. [#1493]
- Caleb repeats the name back to her face: "Alternate signatory. L. Sowri." Arenya: "It sounds like someone's already booked your pen." Nobody signs. [#1491]
- Abernathy names Sowri as the light's house, and says his cuff warmed at "sign," but withholds his suspicion. [#1492]
- Open: this copy is in Arenya's hands at the flat. The chain of custody from Nana's pestle (where it was left in s08) to the flat is not on the page. [#1486]
