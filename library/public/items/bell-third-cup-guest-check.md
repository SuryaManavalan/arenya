# Bell's Guest Check

Green guest check from [[lupes-diner]], written "3 COF." in pencil (visible in reverse through the paper), no charge, with a single upright soft-grey pencil stroke in the SERVER box. Lupe gave it to Arenya at Bell's request.

## Details
- Read in-world as Bell offering himself as Arenya's second witness (two strokes = *dos testigos*). Her palm dot pulled strongly toward the stroke. [#0453]
- Kino: the clerk would count Bell's hand as the second; a polite, free, already-paid trap. A legitimate second would be Nana (her own pencil) or the Livermore room. [#0456]
- Arenya refused aloud ("No"), never touched it skin-to-paper, folded it in a napkin into the zip pocket with the tea-tin paper as evidence ("Evidence is not agreement"). Afterward faintly warm, slightly above body heat: a possible window like the [[bell-1912-dime]]. [#0455, #0459]
