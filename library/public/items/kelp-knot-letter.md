# Kelp-Knot Letter

Wet bull-kelp rope tied in a careful knot, found 6:58 a.m. Tue Nov 3 on Ava's welcome mat; no sand or footprints. [#1162]

## Details
- Ticks three times (not the ledger's eleven). Arenya hears it; at the sink test it ticked only to Ava, not to the counter or Arenya's palm. [#1162, #1172]
- Abernathy: smells of brass and an old library, "somewhere I've worked"; he can't place it. Warned not to handle it bare-handed or freeze it with the cassette. [#1163, #1167]
- Ava bagged it in a Ziploc in her fridge (it fogs from inside); it rode on the Corolla dash and ticked fast near the spirit of hurry, once slowly afterward. [#1169, #1172, #1182, #1192]
- Read by Arenya as "receipt and/or answer" to her question to the old woman.

- **b1.c18.s05:** in Ava's coat pocket it ticked once at Second piling as a soft *tock* to her alone (#1196). At the car at ~7:55 p.m., after the stakes were pulled, it ticked **four** times, not three (#1235). Meaning unstated (a fourth stake? the cuff? a new line owed?).

- **b1.c19.s04 (Wed 4 Nov, 8:58 p.m.):** after the foghorn counted **twelve**, Ava found a **fifth knot** that wasn't there at dinner (checked under the dome light). [#1346]
