# Frost hatband

A thin ring of frost-cold worn around [[mr-bell]]'s head, seen by Abernathy; the DM identifies it as a Crown-fragment [#0144]. Same cold as the grey-coated void in the hospital corridor, but "a man wearing a piece of the void." [#0114, #0116]

## Details
- Smooths whatever comes off the wearer so attention skids; nobody remembers inviting him. [#0116]
- Costs the wearer: after removal Bell's nose bled. [#0128]
- Lifted off "like a tight hat"; its frost smell drops by half when removed. [#0128]
