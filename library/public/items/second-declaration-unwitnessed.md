# Second declaration (unwitnessed)

A second copy of Susmithaa's written "no", on the same cream paper as the March declaration. It was hidden in her red Brooke Bond tea tin in [[nana-itzel]]'s dresser.

## Details
- Folded in thirds; three short paragraphs, the same three ways of saying *no*. Signed "S. Sowri". The witness line is blank, printed: **WITNESS — ITZEL R. MARTÍNEZ.** [#0390]
- Susmithaa asked Nana in March, after the shed, to witness: "a no needs two." Nana stalled. [#0390]
- [[arenya-sowri]] holds it folded and unopened in her notebook between two clean pages. She will not ask Nana to sign: "I'm not going to go looking for a second in the people who love me." [#0391]
- Her second-witness palm dot woke for it, then settled into a "keeping" warmth. [#0382, #0394]
- Arenya reads "Feed Itzel's boy" as the other half of this arrangement.

## b1.c16.s08 "Two Pencils": now witnessed
- Nana signed the witness line "Itzel Rosario Martínez" in No. 2 pencil, with two short strokes beside the name ("Dos testigos. Ella y yo."). [#0474] Frost on her garage jamb melted, Bell's stroke on the guest check snapped under the salt, and Arenya's palm now shows 12, the rule, and two graphite strokes, cool, no pull. [#0474]
- Full text (typed on a machine with a sticky *e*, blue-ballpoint corrections; one sheet of cheap bond) [#0479]:
  1. On 8 March she had an archana performed at the Livermore temple in ARENYA's name with her star and gotra. She asked; Arenya did not, was not present, and did not know.
  2. "The priest who read it was not our priest. I noticed too late." Anything promised in that room in Arenya's name was promised by Susmithaa's mouth, "and I take it with me."
  3. Neither Arenya nor any of her blood is party to any contract, policy or account opened beneath Colma or elsewhere.
  4. Any envelope addressed to the beneficiary is addressed to no one.
  - Postscript to "Ari": sorry for asking without asking; "I wanted the god to know your name before anyone else learned it. Someone else was listening. Don't go back into that room to fix my mistake unless you go with people who will say this again."
  - Address given: 41 Hollister Court, South San Francisco (first appearance in the library).
- Read aloud line by line, call and response (Arenya, Kino, Nana: "así es"); after line 4 the tea tin gave a tinny *pank* and the strokes on Arenya's palm darkened. The postscript was read by Arenya alone, not repeated ("not for the book"). [#0484]
- Kept in the open Brooke Bond tin on Nana's table; Arenya holds only a phone photo. [#0486]
