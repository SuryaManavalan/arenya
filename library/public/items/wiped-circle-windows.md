# The Wiped Circles

- Sun 1 Nov ~8:10: a palm-sized circle wiped at eye height in the condensation on the 110 shelter glass, with nobody having been inside. [#0915]
- Later, after breakfast: an identical clean circle inside **Nana's kitchen window**, at eye height, where nobody had stood. [#0950]
- Dalal-ji, reading the house, frowned at the kitchen window: a **blank page** in his book that "shouldn't be." Abernathy was hidden in the pantry and felt nothing touch him. Malachi heard the frown; Arenya, Abernathy and Kino all flagged it. [#0941–#0945]
- Kino will salt the sill and sit the window alone for an hour; Caleb offered to sit with him. [#0945, #0947]

## The Window Hour (b1.c17.s12, Sun 1 Nov 9:40–10:40)
- Kino sat the kitchen window behind a corner-to-corner salt line on the sill (dip by the faucet held), after leaving a small hill of salt on the linoleum before the pantry as a courtesy. The circle was a degree colder; seated-eye height. [#0954, #0958, #0961]
- 10:21: stove clock stopped 11 seconds, in time with the fog's breath; on the third breath a long back stayed above the offshore water too long. [#0961]
- Arenya, via the oven-door reflection, saw a forehead-height smudge on the OUTSIDE of the glass and no one on the step. A fingertip drew a waterline and a rising hump, then stopped unfinished (like the Nissan's fifth stroke). No name, no word. [#0961, #0963]
- Abernathy: the "blank page" again, closer, like being waited on; he flinched at the drawing as something seen before, place unknown. He judges the salt-taking "manners, not a debt." [#0964, #0965]
- End: sill line intact; pantry salt hill gone, linoleum clean, as if picked up like a gift. Arenya logged times and photographed circle, sill, clock. [#0961–#0963]
- Next: ask Nana (manners or debt?), keep the pantry shut, tell Malachi about the blank page. Caleb released from the table to the front room/porch. [#0958, #0963]
- Abernathy (bond) described the patient voice: in the walled-off place a patient, unpushing voice said a short name, one breath, that he knew "like his own cough"; he did not answer and it let him not. [#0959]

## Open questions

- Who or what looks out of that window; whether it relates to Abernathy's walled-off memory, the Lady, or the fog-thing.
