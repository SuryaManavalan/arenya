# Lockbox L-3

A grey lockbox the size of a loaf, stencilled **L-3**. Role-keyed: Typhus's badge cannot open it, and Elias says the key is a role, "M-Ops," not a person. Originally held under the rack console in the Lantern control room. [#1360, #1352]

## Details
- Moved to the Pier 70 dead spur by [[pruitt]] in [[mill-truck-d2-04]] on the night of Sun 8 Nov; found on the chair's seat, facing the door. [#1535]
- A thermal receipt taped to the lid: **L-3 · HELD FOR M-OPS (VACANT) · SIGN BY MON 06:00 · c/o LUCERO, I.** Under it, in pen: *Alternate accepted.* [#1535, #1539]
- Nobody has signed, spoken the name, or touched the box. Signing deadline: Mon 6:00 a.m. (about six hours from 23:56 Sun). [#1540]
- Abernathy (bond): the cuff "went warm on the word *sign*." He believes the box holds his lost brass cuff. [#1515]

## Open questions
- What is inside? [[intake-exception-0001]] is logged as a 14×6 cm woven textile, not brass. The cuff identification is Abernathy's guess and does not yet match the logged object.
- "Alternate accepted" was written in pen beneath the thermal receipt. By whom?
