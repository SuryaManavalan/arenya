# Meridian Mutual policy

A supposed life policy Susmithaa bought in March, according to [[daniel-hale]]. Nothing in the family corroborates it.

## Details
- Claim ref MM-031944; single beneficiary; "verify in person"; paid in cash at the Colma office on 19 March. [#0158, #0167]
- Amma: Susmithaa never mentioned insurance and didn't trust banks (kept gold in a Horlicks tin). Appa is evasive. [#0161]
- Nana, at the Safeway by the onions, in March: "Itzel, I bought a door, and I put Ari's name on it." Nana thought it meant the temple. "A door can be locked from either side. Whose side is this man on?" [#0161]
- Same month as the Livermore temple receipt in Arenya's name ([[curtain-and-temple-receipt]]). Kino noted the link. [#0160]

## Revealed in b1.c15.s08 (#0189, #0194, #0198)
- **No policy was ever bought.** The cream "Welcome to the Meridian family" letter (Meridian Mutual Life · Beneficiary Services) is dated **9 March**, ten days before Hale's claimed 19 March cash purchase. Grid: INSURED Sowri, Susmithaa **11**; BENEFICIARY Sowri, Arenya (niece) **12**. No premium, no face value; "a representative will contact the beneficiary in person to verify." Susmithaa wrote **NO** across it, tearing the paper.
- Her **notarized declaration** (Colma, 19 March, by the flower shop): she bought nothing from Meridian or any affiliate, names no beneficiary, her niece is party to no contract; witnessed by Ravi Sowri, who was told it was a will and did not read it. The envelope read "Ravi — only if they call."
- Her note: "Writing is not keeping... Don't sign. Don't verify. Don't say your full name to them in a room they chose. A thing gets kept when you agree to it." Her "door" = a refusal, not a purchase. The "I bought a door" remark likely refers to the notarized no.
- The letter is the Lattice's way of saying "we have found you" ("Men who count don't knock. They send paperwork."). Hale's calls are read as pressure to make Arenya name herself. Landline unplugged; nobody goes to Serramonte at noon. Mechanism: see [[el-contador-rumor]] (full account restricted).

## Revealed in b1.c15.s10 (#0249, #0252)
- The "Colma office" is a rented PO **Box 112** at [[colma-notary-and-mail]] ("Meridian Mutual Services"), rented ~1 hour after Susmithaa's 19 March declaration. Notary [[rodel-panganiban]] confirms the declaration's gist and that Ravi witnessed.
- Envelope "TO THE BENEFICIARY" left in the open box as bait; Arenya's full name appears only in a phone photo of it.

## Revealed in b1.c16.s01 (#0261–#0275)
- Hale's noon meeting slot was **Wednesday** (DM ruling #0268); nobody went. At 12:00 the phone buzzed once and showed "Missed call · Unknown · 12:00 PM"; at 12:41 it buzzed again, greying a salt ring around the phone.
- On a **second read** of the bait-envelope photo, Kino saw a frost-written third line: *"c/o the one who reads this to her."* Seen only by him; he refused to say it aloud (saying it = taking the "job" / becoming her mail, per Nana: "Care is a job"). He wrote it for Nana on a PG&E envelope flap; it went into the freezer Ziploc. Arenya chose not to look ("looking is verifying") and pinned the phone face-down under the salt shaker. Her middle name appears on the envelope; Kino hadn't known it. [#0262–#0265]
- **Why she is "12":** Susmithaa had an archana said at Livermore on **Sunday 8 March** (name + Arenya's birth star spoken aloud in a room), the day before the 9 March letter. "Her kindness was the leak… a door she held open." Someone "at the back of the room with a pencil." [#0272–#0274] See [[curtain-and-temple-receipt]].
- Timeline pad: row 11 (Susmithaa) existed on 9 March, before her death; Kino's question "WHO TOLD THEM MARCH 9?" answered by the archana.

## Revealed in b1.c16.s02 (#0296–#0303)
- Hale (in person) explains the "entry": made by Susmithaa's 8 March archana ("kept" = protected to her, the same word in the book). Single stroke ends on her sixteenth day; the entry then closes as written and whoever has standing (Mr. Bell, who filed for "verification of beneficiary") collects.
- Hale's second envelope "closes it in your favor; you receive." Not taking it only keeps it from closing in her favor; it still closes on day sixteen if nobody stands. Un-saying the archana in the same temple room is the other route (untried, per Hale).
