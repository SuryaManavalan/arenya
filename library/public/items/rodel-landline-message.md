# Rodel's landline message (Mon 2 Nov, 11:58 a.m.)

An answering-machine cassette at Nana's, left by [[rodel-panganiban]]; it records that [[dalal-ji]] sold a page of his book of names to a man in a grey suit.

## Details
- Landline in Nana's hall rang at 11:58 (same tone as Saturday's call), four rings, then the machine took it. Display: *1 MESSAGE · MON 11:58*, counter 0047 (photographed by Ava). [#0977, #0986]
- Rodel, from a car, window down, Colma traffic: "Raghavan, it's Rodel… I didn't sell anything of yours." That morning the pandit came to his shop with a man in a grey suit; the man paid him; the pandit gave him a page out of the book; then he bought a bag of oranges from the stand outside. "Tell your guest." [#0977]
- Malachi reads it as: his true name (the page) has been sold. Oranges = what you bring after taking something from a priest; the buyer "was raised properly." [#0982]
- Caleb: Hale wears grey suits (Dorsey, Pruitt don't); "he likes things to look ordinary"; whoever holds the page needs Malachi to *move*. Unconfirmed it is Hale. [#0985]
- Abernathy heard a car turn signal ticking eleven times under the call, then a lane change; blank page nearer when the phone rang. [#0981, bond #0980]
- Abernathy's two theories: Dalal was leaned on, or the sale was forged; the page is worth more than the book. [#0980]
- Cassette: Appa wrapped it in a dish towel and put it in the freezer behind the frozen peas ("Nobody records over anything"); Arenya recorded the playback on a voice memo; Kino salted the hall sill under the window. Malachi wants to replay it slowly for the car, radio, traffic. "ORANGES" added to Appa's fridge envelope. [#0979, #0983, #0986]
