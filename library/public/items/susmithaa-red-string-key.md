# Susmithaa's red-string key

Brass key on faded red string; opens the Sowri house's **side door** by the garage and gas meter. Three keys existed: Amma's, Appa's, Susmithaa's. [#0574]

## Details
- Appa took it from Susmithaa's purse at Seton after Rodel told him to hold it ("the side door is the one they'll come to"); he lied to Amma, "It's handled." [#0583]
- He wore it wound twice round his knuckle; offered it at arm's length over the salt lines. [#0580, #0583]
- Amma laid it on the brass ghee plate at the foot of [[susmithaa-brass-lamp]]; the flame dipped and stood straight. The lamp "took the key without counting it." [#0586, Kino #0588]
