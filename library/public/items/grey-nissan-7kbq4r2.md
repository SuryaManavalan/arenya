# The Grey Nissan (plate 7KBQ4R2)

Vehicle with the window that fogs from inside. Earlier: empty at Colma notary (Hale exits it, s01). Malachi's mirrored read "7K?4R" was Nat-1 garbled; the full plate is now **7KBQ4R2**.

- Engine off, driver's seat empty, cold, tyres wet, folded newspaper on back seat; lanyard on the rear-view mirror with its badge turned backwards (same habit as the Drop Two clipboard man). [#0719]
- Passenger window fogged edge-to-edge; four vertical strokes drawn from inside "like a ruled ledger," one per person in the alley (Malachi, Caleb, Kino, Arenya); a fifth was considered.
