# The Grey Nissan (plate 7KBQ4R2)

Vehicle with the window that fogs from inside. Earlier: empty at Colma notary (Hale exits it, s01). Malachi's mirrored read "7K?4R" was Nat-1 garbled; the full plate is now **7KBQ4R2**.

## Details (b1.c17.s04, Tartine alley, 18th Street end)
- Engine off, driver's seat empty, cold, tyres wet, folded newspaper on back seat; lanyard on the rear-view mirror with its badge turned backwards (same habit as the Drop Two clipboard man). [#0719]
- Passenger window fogged edge-to-edge; four vertical strokes drawn from inside "like a ruled ledger," one per person in the alley (Malachi, Caleb, Kino, Arenya); a fifth was considered. Arenya's palm dropped a quarter-degree twice; Kino's patch tightened. Abernathy: a fifth stroke is "the one it hasn't pinned yet." [#0719, #0726]
- Caleb: not a team vehicle (team uses grey Corollas), but the fog is what he saw in his motel mirror when the Crown tracked him; it pushes him toward the Vesper ("Wrong"). Malachi: "Lattice plate… two cars and one habit." [#0716, #0724]
## b1.c17.s05 (Fri 30 Oct night, Hillcrest) [#0749, #0755, #0759, #0776, #0779]
- Rolled up the hill with no lights or engine under a streetlamp dimmed to "weak tea"; driver's seat empty, lanyard turned once on its cord; four old smeared strokes on the passenger glass. Cottage light came on with a looping voice (see [[susmithaa-ash-canister]]). When no one answered, a fifth stroke began and stopped halfway; a bead of water smeared it ("nobody to witness it, the tally won't close"). Car rolled backward away, lamps relighting behind it.
- Dawn: dry square-cornered rectangle at the curb, no tyre, drip or weight marks; two leather-soled prints (size 10–11, no tread, toes to the blue house, unshifting all night); fresh clipboard-width dry corner on Susmithaa's gate top rail; pink carbonless scrap "…CEIVED BY:" (blank line) in the lower gate hinge (left in place; Malachi tells only Arenya); lamp still lit in daylight, humming four pulses, pause, start of a fifth. Lamp went out at 7:31 a.m. like any lamp. [#0779, #0790]
- Plate: Malachi earlier ran a CHP query ("never issued"); see [[malachi-shawani]].
