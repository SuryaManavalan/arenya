# Sri Ganesh Tiffin

South Indian lunch counter in a strip-mall unit off First Street, downtown Livermore, between a vape shop and a tax preparer. Six tables, laminated menu with prices taped over twice, a muted Tamil serial on the TV. Smells like the Sowri kitchen on a Sunday.

## Details
- Ordinary place: nothing counted or wrong; Arenya's palm stayed cool and quiet. A rare "unlistened-to" room. [#0545, #0549]
- Order slip numbered "2" (paper masala, ghee podi, 2 filter coffees). Counter man refilled Kino's sambar unasked; an uncle with the *India West* wished them "Good appetite, children." [#0545, #0549, #0551]
- Site of the "paper dosa" truce (no names, numbers or writing) ~4:15 p.m. Thursday; see [[kino-balam]]. [#0546]
- Only notable oddity: Arenya's notebook grew briefly heavier when she said "taking notes." [#0551]
