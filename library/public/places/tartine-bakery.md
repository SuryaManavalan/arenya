# Tartine Bakery

Mission institution ("warm sugar and old brick"). Site of [[yuri-melnikov]]'s death; Ava works here. The security firm's footage ("2025_02_18_Tartine_FloorCam2.mov", 5 min, 480p, no audio) shows him freezing ~25 s and collapsing; a sliver shows the corner table with a man in a light grey jacket [c07]. A motionless sunglassed man in a high-collared zip-up stood across the street for 20+ minutes [c11].

## b1.c16.s03 (Thu 7:00 a.m., Guerrero & 18th)
- Arenya's chosen meeting place ("Where I got the footage. Opening. Outside, by the bench."); Ava opens at 7:00; Ava closes at 2 on Thursdays. Malachi watched from the corner table (facing door and bench) from 5:30. [#0332, #0335, #0347]
- Oven glass: flame-light leans toward the street at Arenya's arrival, *away* from Mr. Bell. Bell left a white chrysanthemum by the tip jar ("a reservation, for the sixteenth day") and sat at the corner table; he was still there when the group left at ~7:14. Door chime silent for him. See [[mr-bell]]. [#0335, #0344, #0348]
- Kino bagged a 1912 dime from the till tray in a coffee sleeve. [#0346]

## b1.c17.s04 "The Back Room" (Fri 30 Oct, 8:58 p.m.) [#0676–#0729, provisional]

Front dark, door propped with a flour sack; entry via alley. Lupe does final lock at 9:40, then the back-door alarm. Kino laid a salt line on the threshold.
- Exits: steel alley door; front deadbolt onto Guerrero (no alarm until Lupe arms it). Alley's 18th Street end: the grey Nissan; see [[grey-nissan-7kbq4r2]].
- Smelled briefly of Aunty's filter coffee and cold cream when Arenya touched the ash duffel; Ava's left-palm cold cleared. Ava backed up her "7:14" page to a laptop.
