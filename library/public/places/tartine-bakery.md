# Tartine Bakery

Mission institution ("warm sugar and old brick"). Site of [[yuri-melnikov]]'s death; Ava works here. The security firm's footage ("2025_02_18_Tartine_FloorCam2.mov", 5 min, 480p, no audio) shows him freezing ~25 s and collapsing; a sliver shows the corner table with a man in a light grey jacket [c07]. A motionless sunglassed man in a high-collared zip-up stood across the street for 20+ minutes [c11].
