# Crissy Field warming hut

Small café hut with a picnic deck on the Presidio bay shore at the Golden Gate end of the Crissy Field promenade; Mason St. runs behind it. Typhus Quinn's habitual dawn run. Site of the Wed 4 Nov 6:22-6:45 a.m. coffee. [#1242]

## Details
- Fog bank sat on the Gate a quarter mile out; foghorn on an 11-s interval, skipped once to 12 s as Typhus left. [#1243, #1288]
- Ava's bull-kelp knot lay on the bottom step before she arrived; Voss's shoe left a grey half-print across it; still tied. [#1245, #1269, #1288]
- Lattice watchers: jogger (Voss) and a white Sprinter marked "GGNRA / FOG-SENSOR SURVEY" (new lamination) on Mason St.; the van left toward Lombard. [#1243, #1280]
