# Seton Medical Center

Hospital in Daly City where Susmithaa was pronounced dead (Sunday night/Monday morning).

## Details
- ER family room (beige chairs, dying ficus, muted infomercial TV); vending machines in the corridor; automatic doors slow by half a second [#0072].
- ~3:40 a.m.: a polite American in a suit asked the night nurse for Susmithaa by full name, before ambulance paperwork was filed; he signed nothing and said "niece" [#0088].
- ~4:12 a.m. onward: Malachi ("Shawani, Department of Energy") shows the nurse a card and questions her [#0077, #0078].
- Susmithaa's phone buzzed repeatedly at 3 a.m. unanswered; Arenya took custody. It buzzed once more at 5:51 a.m. [#0084, #0092].
- Dawn ~5:51 a.m. (window "colour of weak tea").
