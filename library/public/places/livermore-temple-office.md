# Livermore Temple Office

Hindu temple on a rise above the vineyards, reached via the Vasco exit and hills south of Livermore. White gopuram tiers, half-full car park, "FOOTWEAR HERE" cubbies, morning puja bell. [#0500]

## Details
- Office door left of the main steps, propped with a brick; laminated sign "OFFICE — ARCHANA / DONATIONS / RECEIPTS. 9–12." Inside: desk, ceiling fan, shelf of yearly ledger binders, older woman in a blue cardigan. [#0500]
- Arrived 9:20 a.m. Palm cool, graphite strokes and the 12 still; Kino's birthmark quiet. Colma's "no" doesn't reach this far; nothing here listens for her yet. [#0500, #0501]
- Arenya asked for the receipt book for carbon No. 2207 (Mar 8, $101, "Ari"), presenting as the niece of the deceased payer. [#0502]
- Route there: Daly City → San Mateo Bridge/92 → 880 → 238 → 580 over Altamont (DM fix, #0495).
- **Office details (s10):** laminated price list (Name+Nakshatra+Gotra $21; Special w/ Sahasranama $51; Family Sankalpam, full $101); tape "RECEIPTS ISSUED ONLY FROM OFFICE BOOK"; sticky "NO CHECKS OVER $500 WITHOUT TRUSTEE"; binders 2022–2025 and "2026 — JAN–JUN"; nadaswaram recording on loop. Keys: Mrs. Ramaswamy, Sastri garu, board president Mr. Iyer. [#0506, #0511, #0537]
- Staff: [[mrs-ramaswamy]] (secretary), [[sastri-garu]] (priest). A "COPY REQUESTS" register holds three entries for the year; 19 Mar line: stub 2207, one copy, $0.10, signed "D. Hale" (fountain pen), with a Meridian Mutual · Beneficiary Services card (no phone). Hale paid in an old coin (put in the hundi). He asked "who reads them" — the full sankalpam is read every Friday at abhishekam for a year from payment. [#0515]
- The wall clock's second hand lagged each tick "as if something has to count it"; slow since end of March; stopped at stub-cancellation; normal after the closing. Kino's salt line under it was rearranged into an evenly spaced row, then slumped to a loose heap. [#0511, #0515, #0519, #0520, #0532]
- Mrs. Ramaswamy struck stub 2207 ("CANCELLED — BY FAMILY, ORIG. CARBON SHOWN. K.R."); this stops the written list only. Closing done by [[livermore-visarjana]] in the sanctum. [#0519, #0532]

Dalal, Fremont"; ruler-straight signature with a small hook at each end (bracket on its side); CONTACT 650-755-… (Rodel's number) and "c/o Colma Notary & Mail, Box 112" — Box 112 was rented 19 Mar, 11 days later. - Arenya photographed the page, signature, contact line and deposit-book line; she drew one bracket-hook and refused to finish. Mrs. Ramaswamy covered the page with a folded dhoti cloth; Kino left his station number under the paperweight and advised salt on the sill. [#0538–#0540]
