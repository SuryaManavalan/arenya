# Lupe's Diner

Mission Street diner in Colma, near a florist and across from the notary strip. Ordinary: grill, Giants pregame on the radio, green guest checks on a spindle. Run by **Lupe**, who pours for regulars and "doesn't need to know" more than the coffee.

## Details
- Bell is a regular of sorts: Lupe calls him "nice old man," says he "counted it out in Spanish" and prepaid a third coffee. [#0444]
- Third mug at the empty place on Kino's side of the booth; its steam leaned toward the window, then bent around Kino and hovered over Arenya's open notebook, as if reading, until she closed it. [#0444, #0449, #0453]
- Bell's message via Lupe: "back when it's cold, don't clear it, his friend likes it warm." After Arenya's "No," steam rose straight; Lupe: "Guess he's not coming back for it." [#0453, #0459]
- Lupe left holding both of Kino's and Arenya's tens (and Malachi's twenty) and did not thank anyone. Malachi told her "the coffee was cold when I came in… I never sat down." [#0452, #0455, #0457, #0459]
- Outside: Bell's Camry gone from the florist, leaving a clean car-sized pollen rectangle; one chrysanthemum in the bucket faced Lupe's door. [#0459]
- Kitchen side exits to an alley and the uphill cemetery road. [#0457]
