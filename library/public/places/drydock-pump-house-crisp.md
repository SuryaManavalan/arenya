# Drydock Pump House (Crisp Ave)

The site Hale named for the Thursday (5 Nov) lift. Unverified: Malachi's vouch is tied to whether it proves true.

## Details
- Old drydock pump house off Crisp, Hunters Point. [#1315]
- **9:30** Team Four enters by the **south roll-up door**; **9:41** the lattice goes live. [#1315]
- Powered not by a coil but by the **pilings**; "that's what the stakes were for. They're tuning the water." [#1315]
- The **apron** is held by Caleb's old crew (Dorsey, Pruitt, [[ollie-baptiste]]); "when the water answers, the apron's the first thing it takes back." [#1315]
- Echoes the Tunnel Ave / Bayline Hunters Point geography; "the Mill" lies in the same district.

- **Daylight recon, Thu 5 Nov ~2:50 p.m. (Malachi):** matches Hale's card. South roll-up rusted blood-colored; rail spur to the apron; pilings ~40 yards out in flat water. New: fresh **brass padlock** on the roll-up, and a white Transit **MO-0117** (hangtag on mirror) parked nose-out by the fence, empty, with a pen and clipboard, blank signature sheet, on the dash. "Hale's door is true. Someone has also been here since Hale." [#1362, #1368]
- Party leaves for Crisp Road 8:40 p.m. [#1368]

- **b1.c19.s06 (Thu 5 Nov, 9:12-9:26 p.m.; ink 12:12-12:26):** Lattice live. Lock is a keyless matte-brass puck (4-digit wheel), warm from a hand, tag "MO / ROLE ACCESS — SEE M-OPS / Thu 21:41" (1 crossed and redone). A held low note came through the seam. Pen on MO-0117's dash lay uncapped on the blank sheet, later pointed at Malachi. [#1381, #1402]
- Eleven pilings, but the water shows twelve: the 12th is [[the-throat]], a hollow pile-spirit in the drydock sump. [#1391]
- Went dormant, unfed, "until another Thursday." Kino: "we find the drydock's door before then." [#1414, #1420]

- **b1.c20.s03 (Sat 7 Nov, 7:14 a.m.):** asked about "the pump house clock," the tide-woman says the clock is not hers, "we only hear it," and that it "runs faster than the tide" / is "always ahead" of it. Who keeps that clock is unanswered. [#1474, #1481]

## Open questions
- Who keeps the pump house clock, and does it run on the Mill's time or the envoy's (11:40 p.m. Sunday)? Ava's question "ahead, or faster?" was answered "ahead." [#1479, #1481]
- Who holds the brass padlock key? (Role-keyed to M-Ops.)
- Is the site true? If false, the Counter's bus returns for Hale.
