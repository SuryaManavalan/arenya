# Sri Venkateswara Satsang, San Bruno

Small Hindu temple in a converted Lutheran church off El Camino Real, San Bruno (a Jack in the Box across the road). Steeple painted saffron; vinyl banner "SRI VENKATESWARA SATSANG — SAT. ABHISHEKAM 7:30 AM — ALL WELCOME." Amma does Saturday seva here.

## Details
- Vestibule with a long wooden shoe rack; hall on carpet (~40 people at Saturday abhishekam; arati at the end); old pews stacked along the side aisle; a framed photo of the Tirumala hills; swinging door to the back kitchen. [#0808, #0813]
- Back kitchen: steel prep table, drum-sized rice cooker, big pot, sink with aunties, back door propped open with a cinder block onto the lot. [#0813]
- Halloween-weekend children in costumes (Spider-Man in a veshti). [#0808]
- Sat 31 Oct: black oxfords (size 10–11, resoled, grey ruled dust in the welt) toes-out on the bottom shelf; Dalal-ji in the kitchen; pink archana slip planted in Amma's left Clark. See [[dalal-ji]], [[pink-archana-slip-lakshmi]].

## Open questions
- Earlier, Amma said Susmithaa chose Livermore because the Daly City temple was close and "everybody's aunty is at ours." Whether the San Bruno satsang is the same as the "Daly City temple" is unstated (apparently a different one).
