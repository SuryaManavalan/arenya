# Colma Notary & Mail

Strip-mall shop on the Mission Road side of Colma, **two doors from the florist *Flores de la Paz***, between it and a shuttered tax-prep office; across the road from the Esposo stone yard (Bell's Camry parked opposite). Window: NOTARY · LIVE SCAN · APOSTILLE · PO BOXES · SE HABLA ESPAÑOL · TAGALOG. Run by [[rodel-panganiban]].

## Details
- Interior: one long room; left wall of brass PO boxes with glass windows; counter with laminator, scale; **real brass door bell** on a spring. [#0243]
- **Notary journal**: fat green ledger "bound like an old hymnal," everyone gives a thumbprint. Under Rodel's palm it made a soft *tick* (Abernathy only). [#0243, #0245]
- **Susmithaa's declaration** notarized here, 19 March (see [[rodel-panganiban]], [[meridian-mutual-policy]]).
- **Box 112**: rented one hour after, as **"Meridian Mutual Services"**, a year paid in cash, new twenties. Collected **Fridays 7:40 a.m.** by a polite suited man with a key to the front, always closing it. [#0249]
- **Wednesday bait**: Box 112 found ajar at 8 a.m. (never so before). Inside: a heavy cream envelope with printed window, same stock as the Meridian letter, typed "TO THE BENEFICIARY / To be collected in person. Identification required." Through glass it reads only that; **in Arenya's phone photo the window shows her real full name**. Hot-brass smell from the box, wet ink "he nearly knows" (Abernathy). Arenya did not touch it and told Rodel to let no one sign for it. [#0249, #0252, #0245]
- Bell's journal-copy request for 03/19: "W. BELL," purpose "Verification of beneficiary," return PO box in Daly City, signature ending in a ruler-straight stroke. [#0252]
- Bell bought a white chrysanthemum (coins) from the florist and entered; the brass bell moved but made no sound. [#0256]

- **Box 12** is Bell's ("Walter Bell," per [[benny-benasaraf]]), same shop as Hale's 112. Rodel silently shook his head at Arenya and Kino through the window, then came out and swept the sidewalk, standing between door and curb. Front window reflects the street in reverse; Bell's Camry was visible only there. [#0417, #0431, #0434]
- Lupe's diner (yellow-lit, PIE sign, six booths, orthopedic-shoed waitress) is half a block past the monument yard on the cross street. [#0436, #0438]

## b1.c17.s01 — Friday 30 Oct, 7:25–7:40 a.m. (#0598–#0613)

- **Rodel's private blue ledger "BOX RENTALS 2014–"** (separate from the State's green journal). Row 112: "S. SOWRI — opened 04/2015 — cash, yearly," eleven renewals, "CLOSED 03/06/26, key returned, box emptied by renter." Above it, in **pencil, ruler-straight with a bracket-hook final *y***: "Hold 112 for Mr. Dalal on vacancy." [#0602]
- Susmithaa's surrender card (6 Mar 2026) is in the back of the ledger. In Arenya's phone photos only: a faint grey **sum-rule** under the pencil line, and a **child-drawn sun** after Susmithaa's signature. [#0606]
- Rodel poured out the coffee Hale told him to offer; wrote in his own slant: "Fri 10/30 7:25 — Ms. Sowri came. Declined. Nothing accepted. — R.P." — a line with **only one hand**, pen left uncapped across the page. The 112 keyhole's shimmer stopped. [#0610]
- Kino laid salt along the inside of the alley-door sill; it stayed dry. [#0610]
- Grey Nissan parked two doors down since before six, empty, dew in a perfectly straight line over the rear wheel; passenger window fogged from inside at 7:34. [#0606, #0610]
- After: Rodel texted Appa — refused Hale's tea ("Not today"), Box 112 closed, new lock ordered, pen untouched. [#0613]
