# Holy Cross Cemetery, Colma

Catholic cemetery on Mission Road, in el Contador's Colma ledger ground. Adonis Quinn is buried here (black granite stone); the Lucero family plot is near the top of section L (*Ernesto Lucero, 1938–2011, Esposo y Padre*, Nana's late husband).

## Details
- Spirit face: the bulk shows it as a lamplit city of the dead, graves as lit windows, fog as a black sea. [#1022]
- Susmithaa's ash canister is buried here, *prestado*, at Ernesto's feet, marked with marigolds, until the water is clean. [#1011, #1022]
