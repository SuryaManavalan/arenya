# Monolith campus

Silicon Valley headquarters: white curves, reflective glass, "engineered" trees, courtyard sculpture of the company logo (black circle crossed by one vertical line). Embedded screens cycle satellites and asteroid-mining rigs; staff in graphite-grey jackets without name tags guide visitors [c09, c12]. Two security layers and a smoked-glass corridor lead to backstage [c12]. Hosted the "Beyond Earth" symposium.
