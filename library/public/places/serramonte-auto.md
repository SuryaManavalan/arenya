# Serramonte Auto

Narrow used-car lot on Serramonte Blvd. (Daly City / Colma line), between a tire shop and a shuttered Sizzler; ~30 cars, pennant string, yellow grease-pen prices, stucco office with a door bell. Owned by [[benny-benasaraf]]. Source of the chrome dealer frame on Bell's beige Camry. Across the boulevard from a Honda dealership. [#0399, #0425]
