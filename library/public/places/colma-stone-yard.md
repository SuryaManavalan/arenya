# Esposo Monument Co. yard (Colma)

Working headstone yard off Mission Road, Colma ("SINCE 1961"), approx 37.6835, -122.4600. Chain-link lot of uncut granite blanks in rows, an open-front polishing shop, a Tagalog talk radio. Colma: ~1,500 living, ~1.5 million dead; cemetery gates alternate with florists. [#0215]

## Details
- Owner **Tito Boy Esposo**, about 60, dust-white apron, mundane; cuts the stones, knows Nana ("Mama"). Of the back shed: "City says it's on our lot. We don't go in." Asked for a call-ahead; "It's been busy this week. Lot of visitors." [#0214, #0215, #0232]
- Across Mission Road, near a florist with bucket roses and two doors from **Colma Notary & Mail**, Mr. Bell's empty beige Camry (plate 7K…) was parked, validation slip on the dash. Likely the "Colma office" of the March 19 policy; see [[meridian-mutual-policy]]. [#0232]
- Spirit face (ruled rows, ledger shed) is restricted; see el-contador.
