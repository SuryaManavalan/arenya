# San Francisco

**Physical face:** the modern tech city: Waymos, startup rooftop parties, fog, Mission studios, Daly City family homes, SoMa, Glen Park, Dolores Park, Lower Nob Hill, Inner Sunset, the Excelsior. Monolith's campus is down the Peninsula in Silicon Valley [c09]. Story year per the chapters: 2025.

**Spirit face:** the astral plane is *not* a mirror of the city; it holds projections and shadows (tree spirits, tinkering intelligences near AI labs) in an emergent, abstract world [human Q6]. The land's old dark lineage is rooted here [see [[dark-land-lineage]]].
