# Sowri family home, Daly City

Row house on a hill; Susmithaa's cottage sits dark behind it. Puja alcove off the kitchen holds [[susmithaa-brass-lamp]] (wick trimmed short; flame stands upright, leans toward the hall when something walks). Fog arrives ~4:30 p.m. [#0555]

## Spirit-layer notes (b1.c16.s12)
- **Side yard gas meter** by the garage door: Appa (30 years at PG&E) reads it every Sunday into a small green meter book. Ordinary reading **4471**. [#0574, #0577]
- Thursday ~4:30–5: the meter glass frosted from the inside; a pencil-thin ruler-straight frost line ran from its base across the concrete to the garage door handle (same as Nana's side door). Dials cleared top-down to **2207**, then one tick of the last needle: Arenya's shut notebook gained one stroke of weight, Kino's sternum patch ticked and his heart slipped to ~4 behind. Nobody accepted the "receipt," so the needles returned to 4471 and the frost line snapped; notebook stays heavier. [#0577, #0580]
- Appa's meter book, 3:12 p.m. entry, pressed through the paper: ***Ari does not sign.*** [#0574]
- Rodel's rule: "Things that count come in where things are counted"; the front door has a lamp, the side door a meter. Work shoes toes-out by a door "so whatever came would walk away" (Appa's mother's custom). [#0583, #0586]
- Two lines of Nana's salt (Arenya's, Kino's) laid across the kitchen–hall threshold; Kino swept his up afterward. [#0574, #0588]
