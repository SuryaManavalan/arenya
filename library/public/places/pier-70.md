# Pier 70 (Dogpatch shipyard)

The old industrial shipyard on the Dogpatch waterfront in San Francisco. In the mundane layer it is a converted set of shipyard buildings and rail spurs.

## Details
- **Building 12, side door, 23:40 Sunday 8 Nov.** The time and address on the [[door-receipt-pier-70]]. [#1508]
- **Building 2 kiosk.** Typhus's "clean terminal" for reading the M-Ops role log, at 22:30 on Sunday, not before. He warned it logs whoever sits in the chair next. [#1493, #1501]
- The Lantern desk, which Typhus refused to use, is the terminal that would log his name first. [#1493]
- **Sun 8 Nov, 23:14–23:56 (b1.c20.s05).** Watched from the car across the shuttered rigging yard. Side door of Bldg 12: sixty metres of corrugated wall, one grey steel door, a caged bulb over it that lit itself at 23:31. [#1520]
- **Loading bay 3** holds [[mill-truck-d2-04]]; the bay roll-up rises at 23:41.
- **The steel door** opened unhelped at 23:40 onto lit empty space and closed itself at 23:42 "like a held breath let go." Uphill water ran to the threshold. [#1524, #1530]
- **Dead rail spur** (unmapped): reached through the crane footing and past the tank farm, to a chain-link dead end beside black water. Here [[pruitt]] left [[lockbox-l-3]] on a bolted chair facing the open door of the truck. The spur has no lights and is not on any Mill route. [#1532, #1535]
- Coordinates are approximate. The DM has not placed the buildings within the yard.
