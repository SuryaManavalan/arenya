# Arenya Sowri

28-year-old Tamil-American writer of "Lifestyle & Wellness" listicles (dogs, hamsters, self-watering pots) for a San Francisco content company; secretly investigating the "freezing deaths." Protagonist of the series; protected character (cannot permanently die; if she falls she is *Lost*) [human: Round 2 Q6].

## Appearance
Ponytail; one blazer she wears to weddings and interviews; coffee-stained notebook. "Not ugly. Not beautiful. Smart enough. Funny when she tried." [c01]

## Voice & manner
Dry, deadpan, self-deprecating ("Wow. Bronze medal."). Clipped when scared, precise with evidence.

## Known history
- Lives in a studio in the Mission; family in Daly City [c01, c03].
- Boss Teddy calls her "Renny" [c01]. Mug "Investigative Badass", a gift from Saiesh [c01].
- Began the investigation after spotting a headline about a third sudden death in a week [c02]; interviewed Ava and Galina Melnikov [c02].
- Committed felony fraud (cloned Teddy's keycard, keylogger, faked project "Urban Mortality Patterns – SF 2025") to obtain Tartine footage [c04, c06].
- Recurring dream: a fork between a warm waterslide and a living, sleeping mountain; she chooses the mountain [c07, c14].
- Met Elias Khatri, then Typhus Quinn for five minutes; he proposed coffee [c09, c12].
- Confronted in her apartment by Malachi Shawani, who told her to stop [c13]. Took down her wall (photographed it first), then slept through the weekend [c14].
- Fought with Saiesh [c13].
- An old college tweet reads "Kill the rich" [c12].

## Relationships
Close to aunt [[susmithaa]]; strained with [[saiesh]]; friendly with [[ava-lindqvist]]; wary rival/ally [[brooke-chatterjee]].

## Status
Alive; start of Book 1, c15.
