# Malachi Shawani

Man who entered Arenya's apartment and told her to stop. Claims to be with the "Special Intelligence Division of the Department of Energy" ("That's not a thing." "That's what makes it special.") [c13]. Showed no badge. Protected character [human: Round 2 Q6]; expected to become a major character [human: Round 1 Q8].

## Appearance
Tall, lean, dark coat, sharp but unassuming features; vanishes in a crowd until he looks at you; eyes "too clear, too focused." [c13]

## Voice & manner
Calm, even, economical. "I'm not your enemy. But I'm also not your friend." "If I were following you, you wouldn't have noticed." "Please don't make me come back." [c13]

## Known history
Warned Arenya that things "far older, far colder, and far less patient than the human mind is built to comprehend" are in motion [c13]. Saiesh thinks he is a goon [c13].

- Agreed not to come to the Sowri house. Arenya gave him her burner number; he will call once tomorrow, saying only "it's me" and a time, and won't call again if unanswered [#0089, #0090].
- Watched Arenya drive in (surveillance admitted) [#0086].
