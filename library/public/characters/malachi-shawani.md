# Malachi Shawani

Man who entered Arenya's apartment and told her to stop. Claims to be with the "Special Intelligence Division of the Department of Energy" ("That's not a thing." "That's what makes it special.") [c13]. Showed no badge. Protected character [human: Round 2 Q6]; expected to become a major character [human: Round 1 Q8].

## Appearance
Tall, lean, dark coat, sharp but unassuming features; vanishes in a crowd until he looks at you; eyes "too clear, too focused." [c13]

## Voice & manner
Calm, even, economical. "I'm not your enemy. But I'm also not your friend." "If I were following you, you wouldn't have noticed." "Please don't make me come back." [c13]

## Known history
Warned Arenya that things "far older, far colder, and far less patient than the human mind is built to comprehend" are in motion [c13]. Saiesh thinks he is a goon [c13].

## b1.c15.s05 "Seton"
- Appears at Seton before dawn as "Shawani, Department of Energy," lanyard shown briefly; apologizes for the hour, waits in the corridor by the vending machines facing the family-room door, hands always visible; bought two coffees [#0078, #0080, #0084]. Early forties, newly clean-shaven, black oxfords with winter salt lines (not local), coat hangs heavier on the left [#0077]. Abernathy sees no light on him at all, "like a hole in a fence," and half-remembers having seen such a thing [#0073, #0075].
- Admits "Energy" is a courtesy; real affiliation withheld [#0086]. His "people" are three analysts and a phone, an unsanctioned cell [#0088]. Keeps a list of "unusual" people (Susmithaa added in September, never contacted) and a separate count of nine freeze deaths; he claims none of the nine were on his list beforehand [#0086, #0090].
- Names Adonis Quinn (Tartine), cousin of Monolith's founder; says he has a man in grey on his own wall and "three more like him" [#0086].
- Has not seen the coin-mark before; warns Arenya off doctors and photographs [#0088]. Notes Susmithaa did not freeze: found sitting by a tree in her cardigan [#0090].
- Agreed not to come to the Sowri house. Arenya gave him her burner number; he will call once tomorrow, saying only "it's me" and a time, and won't call again if unanswered [#0089, #0090].
- Watched Arenya drive in (surveillance admitted) [#0086].
