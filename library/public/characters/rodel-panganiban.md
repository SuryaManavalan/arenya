# Rodel Panganiban

Colma notary, ~60, Filipino-American; heavyset, pressed guayabera, reading glasses pushed into grey hair, notary seal on a lanyard "like a saint's medal." Eats sinigang from a styrofoam cup and sets his spoon at precise right angles. Speaks English with Tagalog/Spanish on offer. Runs [[colma-notary-and-mail]].

## Voice & manner
- Courtly, dry, stubborn about paper: "Everybody signs for me. That's the job." "Stubborn men are good at paper" (Nana). Polite to a fault (shook Bell's cold hand "because I'm polite").

## Known history
- Recognized [[susmithaa]] in Arenya: "You have her chin." [#0243]
- Notarized Susmithaa's declaration on **19 March**, witness Ravi ("very nervous man, sweats through his shirt"). She read every line aloud; wrote it three ways ("It's not for California"). She brought him champorado in a jar ("your soup was sad"). Wore three scarves in March, rings on every finger. She told him: "Rodel, if anybody comes asking about this day, you make them write it down." [#0249, #0252]
- Gist of declaration: she bought nothing, named nobody, her niece is party to "no contract, no agreement, no understanding." [#0252]
- An hour later a polite man in a nice suit (read as [[daniel-hale]]) rented Box 112. [#0249]
- Refused Arenya and Kino access to his journal; made [[mr-bell]] file a written request instead. Does not want Arenya's name: "Don't tell me." [#0243, #0249]
- **s12 (via Appa):** Twenty years ago Susmithaa sat nine nights with Rodel's dying mother at Seton when he couldn't leave his shift; he owes her. After the funeral he phoned Appa: "they used my number, I am sorry, I will handle the check"; told him to hold her key (side door), write the family's no at the meter, and said "Things that count come in where things are counted." Also: "*He* asked me for one-twelve before it was empty. I should have known then." (Box 112 was rented 19 Mar by Hale; "he" unnamed.) Appa calls him "the notary, from the flower shop." Plan: question him in daylight Friday morning before 7:40, with his register on the table, Appa driving. [#0583, #0586, #0590]
- **b1.c17.s01 (Fri 30 Oct, 7:25):** Showed Arenya/Kino his private blue ledger. Late Feb (a Friday, ~this hour) a faceless man, "Mr. Dalal from Fremont temple," in a nice shirt with cold hands, knew Susmithaa's star and her 11-year box; had Rodel pencil "Hold 112 for Mr. Dalal on vacancy" (Rodel thought the neat hand was his idea; it was written through him) and borrowed his number "for the paperwork" — how it got on the 2207 line. A week later (6 Mar) Susmithaa emptied/closed 112, angry: "Rodel, they're moving in next door to me." Bell's Box 12 had left unstamped long envelopes in 112 every March. Hale's call came 9:12 p.m. Thursday, 41 seconds, flat "like a recording of polite," said Arenya's full name, told him to offer coffee and ask only for ID. Rodel poured the coffee out, wrote a one-hand "Declined" line, refused Hale's tea ("Not today"). Says being used feels like "being helpful… useful"; resolves to make tea first. Thirty-one years in business; every line in his book normally has two hands. [#0602–#0613]
- **b1.c18.s01 (Mon 2 Nov, 11:58):** Called Nana's landline from a car and left a message: he sold nothing of the Sowris', but Dalal came to his shop with a grey-suited man who paid for a page from the book; Dalal then bought oranges outside. "I'm sorry. Tell your guest." See [[rodel-landline-message]]. [#0977]
- Open: protecting the family or used by the counters; he foreknew before Susmithaa's death.
- Status: last seen with Bell entering his shop, holding to the rule that people must write it down.
