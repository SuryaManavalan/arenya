# Kino Balam

26, California State Parks ranger on Mount Tamalpais; raised in the Excelsior by grandmother [[nana-itzel]], keeper of the coast's old **dark** spirit line (roots Yucatán to the Bay). Trained from childhood (fasting, night-walks, breath, dreaming on the mountain) as the land's **champion**, the one the [[sleeping-lady]] would wake for. Hosts a lesser dark spirit, a night-jaguar sentinel. Jaguar-spot birthmark on left forearm ("the Lady's thumbprint"). Quiet, dry, still "the way animals are still." Switches to Spanish with his Nana.

## b1.c15.s03 "Dawn Watch" (East Peak fire road, ~3 a.m.)
- Night-sat on the rock above the East Peak fire road since midnight, Nana's method. At 3:01 Ix (the jaguar) looked south; the mountain "turned" its face south toward the city, and the birthmark went cold. [#0042]
- Sensed a small steady warmth south, low under the fog, smelling of burnt butter and something medicinal "like a church that isn't yours"; Ix respects it. Survival check 6 vs DC 14 failed to narrow it. [#0045, #0046]
- Nana (by phone): the Lady turned her *face*, not her back; the land looks at what is new, old, "not from here," worrying or curious. "You were shown it." Told him to bring "the thing you're not saying" to her table at first light. [#0045, #0050]
- Is withholding something (dreams, fear of "why not me") from Nana for now. [#0043, #0047]
- Vowed to the mountain: "I'll go where you're looking. You don't have to turn around for me." Walking down, the wind fell into step at his shoulder and the birthmark's cold eased briefly. [#0051, #0053]

## b1.c15.s04 "Nana's Table" (~6:40 a.m.)
- Confessed: since September (5–6 weeks), before the first freezing death, he has dreamed the Lady lying as always, then slowly rolling while he slides off her shoulder unnoticed. Hid it so Nana wouldn't worry, and from shame. Real name used by Nana: Joaquín. [#0060, #0061]
- Nana's reframe: she turns toward a door, not away from him. Kino voiced jealousy ("twenty years... for somebody who doesn't believe in anything"; "even if it should've been me"), apologised, then chose: a champion stands *beside* the door, watches who enters and what follows. [#0063]
- Plan: carry the pozole at 2 p.m. to the Sowri house in a black shirt, silent; Ix waits in the fog past the porch light. Wants someone to show Arenya how to hold the light. Does not yet know her name or that she is the niece he has met. [#0063, #0065]
- Habits: removes boots on newspaper at Nana's door; drinks tea with both hands; breathes in-4/hold/out-slow; presses birthmark when stressed (note: s04 puts it "under his shirt" at the left side; IDENTITY says left forearm). [#0059, #0060]

## b1.c15.s06 "Pozole" (Sowri house, 2–6+ p.m.)
- First meeting with Arenya; introduced himself as Joaquín, then "Kino." Carried the pot, said "Lo siento mucho." Ix waits in the fog, later under the porch (Kino speaks to him through a mental line held since age nine). [#0099, #0104, #0124, #0134]
- The memorial lamp leaned toward him; Nana's silent verdict to "¿Soy yo?" was *no, not only you*. Birthmark: cold → aching cold; described as pale rosette/jaguar-spot like a thumbprint. [#0101, #0105, #0146]
- Shielded Arenya from Mr. Bell ("She's eating. Let her."), gave his full name "Joaquín Balam," sent Ix to trail Bell (DM roll 24). Wrote down plate 7K…R advice. [#0118, #0121, #0125, #0129]
- Showed Arenya and Amma the birthmark; said it is cold *toward* something, "I think we're both pointing at the same door"; accepts "beside, not through." Sleeps on the Sowri sofa that night. [#0146, #0148]
