# Caleb Strand

33, ex-Army (two tours, logistics, contract security). Recruited 14 months ago by a Monolith subsidiary for "the Lattice Program". Average height/build/face, close-cropped hair, small surgical scar at the crown [IDENTITY].

## What the characters know
Arenya has his blurry face on her (former) wall: the grey-jacketed man at the Tartine corner table [c07]. He is unnamed to her.

- **b1.c15.s01:** Arenya sees him (unnamed, face smeared by grey static) among four men drawing the net around Susmithaa. She failed to pin him (Perception 10 vs DC 14) and kept only "left pocket" (jacket hangs long on the left). His net filament "counted" her, leaving a cold coin-sized spot inside her right wrist. At the end he alone stayed still "like a man taking out a pen." Arenya vowed "I'm going to remember you." [#0006–#0014] (Identification of him as the one in the dream rests on the grey jacket.)

## b1.c15.s02 "Woke" (Caleb's POV; the party doesn't know this yet) [#0021–#0040]
- Wakes 2:52 a.m. in Team Four's grey sedan, Daly City, half a block from the Sowri house. Crown burnt but live; canister reads "full" but holds only ash (Susmithaa's).
- Fakes flat compliance to handler Shepherd (Deception 18); passes his extended filament off as a "residual tether" and **folds it back instead of cutting it**. Arenya's wrist tag is now masked, faint, and findable only by Caleb. Cost: first bite of Vesper withdrawal (faces in fog, cramp behind left eye).
- Bails from the sedan at an on-ramp light with the canister (Acrobatics 18). Team Four (Dorsey, Pruitt, Hale), Crown-flat, don't pursue and drive off to report.
- Shepherd calls him "Caleb" for the first time in 14 months, offers to "fix" him and erase memory, warns the tether "will unfold." Caleb names Susmithaa, refuses ("I'd rather be scared"), and heads away from the south-east pull so as not to lead the program to the woman.
- Hides ~3:14 a.m. in a steel storm culvert in a drainage cut; the Crown goes silent there (the hum returns outside; Shepherd is still listening). Withdrawal day 0; 14 months of memory return. Recites victims aloud (Tartine man/Yuri, Susmithaa); never learned his teammates' real names.
- Keeps the canister: won't hand it back "till somebody not them" tells him what it is. Two Vesper doses untouched in left pocket. Carries: knife, keys, dead burner, $380, pen.

## Status
Joins the party after b1.c15, when his Crown implant burns out. Currently (end s02) alone in the culvert, hunted by the program. His role in the killings: [[collection-program]] and [[meta-mages]] (restricted).
