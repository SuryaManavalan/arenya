# Caleb Strand

33, ex-Army (two tours, logistics, contract security). Recruited 14 months ago by a Monolith subsidiary for "the Lattice Program". Average height/build/face, close-cropped hair, small surgical scar at the crown [IDENTITY].

## What the characters know
Arenya has his blurry face on her (former) wall: the grey-jacketed man at the Tartine corner table [c07]. He is unnamed to her.

- **b1.c15.s01:** Arenya sees him (unnamed, face smeared by grey static) among four men drawing the net around Susmithaa. She failed to pin him (Perception 10 vs DC 14) and kept only "left pocket" (jacket hangs long on the left). His net filament "counted" her, leaving a cold coin-sized spot inside her right wrist. At the end he alone stayed still "like a man taking out a pen." Arenya vowed "I'm going to remember you." [#0006–#0014] (Identification of him as the one in the dream rests on the grey jacket.)

## Status
Joins the party after b1.c15, when his Crown implant burns out. His role in the killings: [[collection-program]] and [[meta-mages]] (restricted).
