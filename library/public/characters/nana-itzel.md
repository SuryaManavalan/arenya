# Nana Itzel

Kino's grandmother; keeper of the old dark spirit line of this coast. Calls his birthmark "the Lady's thumbprint." Not yet seen at the table.

## b1.c15.s03
- Heard by phone at ~3 a.m. in Daly City (kettle, Spanish radio); awake already, felt the mountain's turn "like the bed tipped." Reads it as the Lady turning her *face*: something new, old, "not from here" has come into the house. [#0045]
- Told Kino "You were shown it," to bring "the thing you're not saying," and she'd set out the good bread at first light. [#0050, #0051]

## b1.c15.s04 "Nana's Table" (~6:40–7 a.m.)
- Home: stucco house on the hill above Mission St, Daly City; door left unlocked when she expects someone; kitchen of rice, ginger, always-warm kettle, oilcloth table. Sets an upside-down cup for someone absent or gone. [#0059]
- Knew about Kino's hidden dreams indirectly: he'd stopped eating her Sunday tamales sitting down. [#0061]
- **Friend of Susmithaa Sowri:** Thursday tea at her table for eleven years; each knew what the other was. Susmithaa took too much sugar and brought "a sweet that tastes like a candle." Nana woke at ~2:45 a.m., felt her die, turned the cup over. [#0061, #0062]
- Reading: the Lady is turned toward a "door"; the light went to Susmithaa's brother's daughter, "the niece... who doesn't believe in anything" (Arenya). Susmithaa worried about her more than about dying. The light "belongs to that family the way the dark belongs to ours." Kino's gift is night-eyes: a lamp needs someone who sees what stands outside its circle. [#0061, #0064]
- Plan: bring pozole to the Sowri house at 2 p.m. (family returns from Seton by noon); Kino carries the pot in a black shirt and says nothing of champion/Lady/light, only *lo siento mucho*. If Ix goes "hiding quiet," they leave. [#0064, #0066]
- Signs of "wrong": (1) a forgettable stranger nobody invited, "made, not accidental"; (2) the memorial lamp's flame leaning with no draft; (3) anyone watching the niece "like they're working out how heavy she is." [#0066]
- Tires visibly (sits at once without arguing). [#0066]
- **b1.c15.s05 (via Amma):** Susmithaa told the family Itzel, the "grandmother in Daly City," taught her tamales and would come with pozole if she died; Amma has no address but trusts she will. Arenya wants to call her. Dovetails with Nana's 2 p.m. plan. [#0082, #0083, #0084]
## b1.c15.s06 "Pozole"
- Age **78**; wears a small worn silver jaguar brooch on her shawl; 11 years of Thursdays with Susmithaa. [#0143]
- Checked Arenya's right hand on entry; "You have her chin." Taught Kino: say your name to the mother, look at the lamp, look at who's looking. [#0097, #0100, #0101]
- Hid the frost-tagged card in the Sowri freezer ([[frost-tagged-card]]). Named [[los-que-cuentan]]. Says the light came "down her mother's side... never to the eldest" and in March began to look for a new home, at Arenya (provisional). Susmithaa predicted Arenya "would laugh, then write an article." [#0137, #0143]
- Given Susmithaa's room to sleep in. [#0148]
## b1.c16.s01 "Read It Twice" (Wed, her kitchen, Daly City)
- Salted the back-door sill; served pozole; supplied the legal pad. Folk rule: "Saying is how they get you… Whoever signs gets the next letter. *C/o* means care is a job." Wrote-it-down not saying. [#0261, #0264]
- Moved the frosted Ziploc to a salted saucer; admitted "I put it in there because cold keeps things… *Pendeja*." Her mother's rules: *agua que corre* (letters to running water, only your own); **REHUSADO** (refuse-and-return by mail), "a door that is not a door can't be counted." [#0272, #0275, #0285]
- Missed the garage side door when salting; a frost ledger-line appeared there. Blocked it with the old Christmas-lights dresser plus an unlit Virgen de Guadalupe candle: "Count furniture, *cabrón*." "He drew on my house." [#0282, #0285]
- Drives Corolla; takes Kino and Arenya to Pacifica at 3:30. Has a parish (Our Lady of Mercy) calendar. Fought utility companies forty years. [#0261, #0275]
## b1.c16.s05 "Cal" (Thu ~8 a.m.)
- Cal lore: "Salt is for keeping things out. *Cal* is for taking the skin off. Corn, walls, bones in a grave, ink." Supervised the dime's destruction ([[bell-1912-dime]]); sent Kino to Mi Tierra for Morelos cal (yellow bag, not the rooster one) and tortillas. [#0373, #0376, #0382]
- Kept Susmithaa's red Brooke Bond Thursday tea tin in her dresser's top drawer (camphor, almond-jasmine lotion; Susmithaa called Nana's tea *agua de calcetín*). It held a second declaration signed "S. Sowri" with the witness line blank: "WITNESS — ITZEL R. MARTÍNEZ." [#0390]
- Confessed: Susmithaa asked her in March, after the shed, saying a no needs two; she stalled ("let me think") because her name in the book would be Kino's address. Arenya took the paper folded and refused to ask her to sign; Kino promised to stand beside her if she ever does. Nana's last words on it: "She said you'd be polite. She didn't say you'd be *kind*." Turned the Guadalupe to face the garage door. [#0390-#0394]
## b1.c16.s08 "Two Pencils" (Thu afternoon)
- Salted Bell's guest check dead; explained witnessing: "A witness is *I was there, and I will say so again*"; the book keeps the witness, entered with where she lives (who eats and sleeps at her table). Then monthly offer-envelopes ("sign here and the boy is left alone"); Susmithaa got eleven years of them. [#0470]
- Felt Kino's cold lift on Tuesday when he gave his note to the water; if she signed he'd re-enter as "who sleeps here." Asked Kino to choose; he did. Learned from Kino that the Lady has turned her back on him in dreams for six weeks. [#0470, #0472, #0474]
- Signed Susmithaa's second declaration "Itzel Rosario Martínez" with two pencil strokes: "Ya está... I've only stopped being alone." [#0474] See [[second-declaration-unwitnessed]].
- Produced check carbon No. 2207 ([[livermore-archana-check-2207]]). Susmithaa always paid by check: "cash was for people who wanted to forget." [#0489]
- Rules the two-stroke no holds in Colma but not at Livermore. Keeps the declaration in the open tin; Ix stays with her; will leave a note in the freezer for Malachi. [#0484, #0485]
## b1.c17.s04 (Fri 30 Oct night, by phone) [#0712]

## b1.c17.s05 "Behind Her Salt" (Fri 30 Oct night → Sat 31 Oct 7:31 a.m., her house, Hillcrest) [#0733–#0790]
- Wore a man's cardigan; porch light off, candle in a jar; laid the salt line (grey-mixed salt, see [[nana-salt-line]]). Invited Arenya across with a pinch of salt ("now you're invited"), admitted Caleb on "*pase*," set Malachi on the outside bottom step with coffee. Told Kino: "A woman who turns her back on the street is guarding the house." Susmithaa tripped on the third kitchen step in 2009 carrying a box of mangoes. [#0733, #0737, #0743]
- Ofrenda shelf over the kitchen sink: Ernesto (man in sombrero, tin frame), a girl in a First Communion dress, salt dish, water glass; cleared end for Susmithaa's canister. Monday (Día de los Muertos / All Souls Nov 2 church "bring their names") the name goes to church; after that the ash is the family's to decide, "yours and your father's." [#0743, #0766]
- Rules: salt holds what walks, not what talks ("only your mouth holds against that"); clay keeps its mouth shut, metal talks. Wrapped Caleb's phone under a clay comal ringed with grey salt. Second visitation will be "someone with hands," who must come up Mission Street. [#0755, #0766, #0772]
- Sent Malachi to drive Arenya home; twist of grey salt for Appa's step; message: "Itzel Lucero says [Raghavan] can stop being brave alone now." Raghavan came to her porch in March and stood where Malachi stood, wouldn't come in. Surname given by Malachi: "Señora Lucero." (Earlier signed "Itzel Rosario Martínez" — see open question.) [#0739, #0790]
- Note (resolved in s04: her house is in Daly City, on the hill above Mission St); earlier flag: her house is in Daly City by the scene's text, though Kino was raised in the Excelsior; and Kino says "from Novato" for himself. [#0045]
