# Nana Itzel

Kino's grandmother; keeper of the old dark spirit line of this coast. Calls his birthmark "the Lady's thumbprint." Not yet seen at the table.

## b1.c15.s03
- Heard by phone at ~3 a.m. in Daly City (kettle, Spanish radio); awake already, felt the mountain's turn "like the bed tipped." Reads it as the Lady turning her *face*: something new, old, "not from here" has come into the house. [#0045]
- Told Kino "You were shown it," to bring "the thing you're not saying," and she'd set out the good bread at first light. [#0050, #0051]

## b1.c15.s04 "Nana's Table" (~6:40–7 a.m.)
- Home: stucco house on the hill above Mission St, Daly City; door left unlocked when she expects someone; kitchen of rice, ginger, always-warm kettle, oilcloth table. Sets an upside-down cup for someone absent or gone. [#0059]
- Knew about Kino's hidden dreams indirectly: he'd stopped eating her Sunday tamales sitting down. [#0061]
- **Friend of Susmithaa Sowri:** Thursday tea at her table for eleven years; each knew what the other was. Susmithaa took too much sugar and brought "a sweet that tastes like a candle." Nana woke at ~2:45 a.m., felt her die, turned the cup over. [#0061, #0062]
- Reading: the Lady is turned toward a "door"; the light went to Susmithaa's brother's daughter, "the niece... who doesn't believe in anything" (Arenya). Susmithaa worried about her more than about dying. The light "belongs to that family the way the dark belongs to ours." Kino's gift is night-eyes: a lamp needs someone who sees what stands outside its circle. [#0061, #0064]
- Plan: bring pozole to the Sowri house at 2 p.m. (family returns from Seton by noon); Kino carries the pot in a black shirt and says nothing of champion/Lady/light, only *lo siento mucho*. If Ix goes "hiding quiet," they leave. [#0064, #0066]
- Signs of "wrong": (1) a forgettable stranger nobody invited, "made, not accidental"; (2) the memorial lamp's flame leaning with no draft; (3) anyone watching the niece "like they're working out how heavy she is." [#0066]
- Tires visibly (sits at once without arguing). [#0066]
- **b1.c15.s05 (via Amma):** Susmithaa told the family Itzel, the "grandmother in Daly City," taught her tamales and would come with pozole if she died; Amma has no address but trusts she will. Arenya wants to call her. Dovetails with Nana's 2 p.m. plan. [#0082, #0083, #0084]
- Note (resolved in s04: her house is in Daly City, on the hill above Mission St); earlier flag: her house is in Daly City by the scene's text, though Kino was raised in the Excelsior; and Kino says "from Novato" for himself. [#0045]
