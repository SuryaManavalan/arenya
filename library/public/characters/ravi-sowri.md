# Ravi Sowri

Arenya's father ("Appa"); [[susmithaa]]'s brother. Works a job with a PG&E fleece (he wears one over Amma's nightdress at Seton) [#0072]. First named in b1.c15.s05.

## Details
- Big hands, grey dry face after Susmithaa's death; stood at the family-room window with his back to the room and would not sit until Arenya asked [#0072, #0082].
- Tells himself "a very bad liar." Told the polite American at Seton "there is no niece" and it cracked him [#0082].
- **March call:** Susmithaa phoned him at work (she never did): "Ravi, if an American ever comes asking about my niece, you tell him there is no niece." Asked which niece (she has only one): "Exactly." He thought her dramatic [#0082, #0093].
- Found Susmithaa's body (see [[susmithaa]]).

## b1.c16.s12 (Daly City kitchen)
- Full name given as **Raghavan Sowri** ("Ravi" presumably short form); ex-PG&E 30 years, engineer's habits, slight grey man in a cardigan, "faintly apologetic." Signs small, leaning left, lifting the pen "like it costs money." [#0563, #0580, #0574]
- **March phone call:** flat-voiced, perfectly pronounced "Sowri-garu," American, posing as the Livermore temple, asked if the family approved dakshina for the visiting pandit; Appa said "Whatever my sister wants is fine." The check went "per family." Never told Amma. [#0583, #0586]
- Rodel (notary, who owed Susmithaa) phoned him after the funeral: hold her key, write the family's no where the meter reads, press hard. He took the key from her purse at Seton, lied to Amma ("It's handled"). Wrote *Ari does not sign* at 3:12 p.m., set work shoes toes-out, then bought the wrong washers. Offered to drive Arenya to Rodel and "read the meter on the flower shop." [#0583, #0590]
- See [[susmithaa-red-string-key]], [[sowri-family-home]], [[amma-sowri]].

## Status
Alive, at Seton then home with Amma. Arenya told him to shout her name if anyone non-family enters, and to send questioners to her first [#0083, #0085].

## Open questions
