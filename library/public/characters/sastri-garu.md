# Sastri garu

The Livermore temple's real priest: small, old, bare-chested under a folded angavastram, steel tumbler of water. Reads the morning archanas. [#0523]

## Details
- Was in Fremont (bad knee) on 8 March; the "visiting man" who read Arenya's archana signed his register in a "very neat hand" and he dislikes it. [#0523]
- Held that "the name is for the people in the hall... He already knows who she is": star, gotra and touch suffice. Performed the closing with "ayam" for the name. [#0523, #0527]
- Told Kino to be fed. Watched them out of the lot. [#0532, #0540]
- His lamp is something the visitor never walked past. [#0540]
