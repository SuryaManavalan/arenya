# Brooke Chatterjee

Arenya's coworker in the next pod: the company's "real journalism" writer. Columbia J-School, fluent in French, interviewed the president of Iceland and the Swedish PM; wears neutrals; never spills [c02, c08]. Once shared a clean-tech panel with Typhus and describes him as awkward, intent, "off-the-grid" [c08, c10]. Unexpectedly set up Arenya's Monolith meeting and gave her the advice "Don't flinch" [c09]. Seen at a political gala with a senator's nephew [c06].
