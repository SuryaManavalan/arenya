# Ava Lindqvist

25, barista at Tartine and Berkeley cognitive-science PhD student studying perception and psychosis [IDENTITY]. Nervous blink, pen in her hair, fogged glasses, many tote bags. Watched Yuri die while foaming oat milk; described the four laptop men [c02]. Became Arenya's odd friend; notices things (the motionless man outside the bakery) [c11]. Sarcastic; narrates symptoms like a case report when scared. Terrible dating luck ("Emotional. Alignment. Model.") [c11].

## b1.c15.s03 "Dawn Watch" (Inner Sunset, ~5:48–5:52 a.m.)
- Woke with cupped, warm palms (smell of camphor and ghee) after dreaming of a woman made of lamps in a sky garden, four men pulling a net, lamps going out until one remained in someone's hand. [#0042, #0044]
- Coffee maker blinked 12:00; microwave and oven froze at 2:41 until resetting to 5:52. [#0045, #0050]
- Oven-glass reflection showed her holding a gold wick-flame while her real hands were empty; consistent across angles, no lag; it gutters when she holds her breath. She filmed it (footage shows only a deniable smear of light). [#0045, #0048, #0050]
- Behind her reflection: another window with a brass lamp, browning marigolds, an old woman's hand with a ring on the smallest finger reaching to pinch the wick, then stopping (Susmithaa's shrine; Ava does not know whose). [#0050]
- Said "Don't go out. Whoever you are. I've got you." Afterward a candle-sized warmth stays in her palms without needing her breath. [#0052, #0053]
- Phantom calendar reminder, blank title, 3:20 a.m. (the minute Amma called Arenya). [#0053]
- Dream-log and notes-app protocol habit; flagged her fear as "not psychotic-scared, different scared." Awarded an upshift charge by the DM. [#0052, #0054]
- Lives with a roommate who never wakes. [#0044]
