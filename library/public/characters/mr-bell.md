# Mr. Bell

A forgettable watcher, mid-fifties, grey cardigan, flat Midwestern voice; left-handed, no ring (per Arenya). He appraised Arenya's warm right hand at the Sowri wake. "People call me Mr. Bell. It's on nothing." Whether he is Lattice staff is the DM's reading [#0144]; he never says so.

## Details
- Came in the back door at 11 a.m., before the family returned and before Ix's watch; holds a paper bowl of pozole he never eats. [#0109, #0122]
- Wears a **frost "hatband"** (see [[crown-fragment-hatband]]) that makes eyes and memory skid; Appa skipped his bowl without noticing. [#0109, #0116]
- Knows Hindu etiquette (namaste at the garlanded photo, "learned from an Indian colleague"). [#0115]
- Said of Susmithaa: "She was very good at keeping her hands still." He "read about her for a long time." Noted *Curtain* upside down: "I don't think that was an accident." [#0115, #0122]
- Called Ix "your dog," unbothered. Left a sealed sympathy card ([[frost-tagged-card]]) "for the family; open it when the aunties go home." [#0122]
