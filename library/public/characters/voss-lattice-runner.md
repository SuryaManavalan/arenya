# Voss

Lattice field operative (Team Four per Caleb's recognition of Hale's voice) who worked the Crissy Field meeting disguised as a jogger: black headband over a brushed-steel Crown, red chipped nails, earpiece in left ear, flat recorder at the small of her back; no accent. [#1255, #1267, #1269]

## Details
- Her Crown locked onto Malachi's sold true name; she mouthed it. Stepping on Ava's kelp knot made her earpiece squeal and the van's beam swung to her. [#1269]
- Malachi: "Tell whoever paid in water rights that they overpaid." She faltered half a step. His read: hired runner, flinched at someone angry in her ear; likely a second buyer of his name. [#1275, #1278]
- Heard via the van: Hale said "walk it off, Voss, we're burned." [#1275]
- Walked east into the fog; the Sprinter left toward Lombard. [#1280]
