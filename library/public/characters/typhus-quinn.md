# Typhus Quinn

Founder and face of [[monolith]], an ~$80B space-tech company. Began launching micro-satellites on spare NASA payload at 25; by 28 a government contractor [c08, c10]. Grew up middle class in Marin: mother taught at a magnet school, father an electrical contractor [c10]. Close to his cousin [[adonis-quinn]].

## Appearance
6'2" or taller, slick black hair curling at the ends, minimalist dark suit; sharp, calm, guarded face [c12].

## Voice & manner
Low, even; exact about time ("So far, you've used twelve seconds."). Awkward in small talk per Brooke [c10]. Flinched when Arenya touched his arm, then relaxed [c12].

## Known history
Keynote at "Beyond Earth: Private Exploration and the New Mineral Age" [c10]. Gave Arenya five minutes backstage; offered "coffee, maybe" to talk more [c12].
