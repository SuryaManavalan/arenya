# Susmithaa Aunty

Arenya's aunt. The family vaguely calls her "international" and says she was "doing some kind of work in Peru last year, maybe?" [c03]. Flowing scarves, silver rings, hair braided with tiny charms, sandalwood scent.

## Known history
- At the Daly City engagement dinner, warned Arenya: "Something old is stirring. Something that hasn't touched this city in a long, long time." and "go with all the light you can carry. And be careful who sees you." [c03]
- The outline names her the current host of the family's main spirit ([[sahasrajyoti]]), and the next victim of the killings [outline]. Arenya will witness her death in a dream, Critical Role style [human: Round 3 Q2]. Details in b1.c15 once played.

- **b1.c15.s01 (Sunday night, 2:40 a.m.):** In a shared dream in the bulk above her Daly City garden, took the form of a woman made of lamps (a thousand flames, braids with silver charms). A faceless four-man collection team drew a magnetic net of humming filaments that snuffed her flames one by one. Abernathy fought at her side as squire. She chose to save Arenya ("I pick. The kid.") over the east side, giving up a flank of her flames. [#0006, #0013]
- Poured all her remaining light into the single wick in Arenya's fist; the net caught only ash and the ring of falling silver charms. Kissed Arenya's forehead. [#0014]
- **Last words:** "You reached, kanna. That's all it ever was, reaching... They aren't the war. They're people who think they can win it before it starts. Something old is waking. The doors are going to open, and every lamp will matter. Keep it lit. Go with all the light you can carry. And look after that one. He'll say he's looking after you. He's lying." [#0014]
- Her real body sat down by the curry-leaf tree in the garden, in only a cardigan, and died. Appa found her. Taken to Seton, pronounced dead. Her window lamp (kuthu vilakku, lit 31 years) went out with oil still in it. [#0014, #0019]
- A polite American in a suit reached the hospital before the family, asked for "Susmithaa Sowri" by full name, and asked whether she had a niece. [#0019]

## Status
Dead (b1.c15.s01). Her light passed to [[arenya-sowri]]; [[abernathy]] was bound to Arenya.
