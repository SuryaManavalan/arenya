# Daniel Hale

Voice on Susmithaa's phone voicemail (6:12 p.m., Monday) and the Sowri landline answering machine (that night). Claims to be from "beneficiary services, Meridian Mutual Life." Never seen. Flat, trained, accentless American; pleasant and unhurried. [#0158]

## Details
- Pronounces "Sowri" exactly as the family does (tapped r), which no outsider has done all week. [#0158]
- Knew the time of death as 3:19 a.m. Sunday, which the hospital never gave (they said "around 3:30"). [#0158]
- Claim ref **MM-031944**; says Susmithaa took out a policy in March naming a single beneficiary, to be verified "in person." [#0158]
- b1.c15.s10: Per [[rodel-panganiban]], a polite man in a nice suit (Hale, inferred) rented Box 112 as "Meridian Mutual Services" an hour after Susmithaa's 19 March declaration, cash in new twenties, and collects Fridays 7:40. Kino judged the shed's counting voice "older than Hale." [#0239, #0249]
- Second message (landline, addressed to Arenya): policy paid in cash, in person, at the Colma office on **19 March**; "covers more than money"; meet **noon Tuesday, Serramonte Library, second floor, by the periodicals**, bring ID; otherwise "happy to come to you," with a veiled threat about the family's mourning period. [#0167]
- Signed off "Keep that hand warm," which only Mr. Bell had seen (Arenya's inference). [#0167, #0168]
- Under his voice a second speaker counted "eleven, twelve," stopping on "hand." [#0167]
- Called the landline as a car pulled away dark from the end of the block. [#0164]
- The lamp flame ducked small and blue on the word "her" in his first message; Kino's birthmark pulsed. [#0158]

## Met in person: b1.c16.s02, Pacifica Pier (#0292–#0303, 4:30–5 p.m. Wed)
- **Appearance:** fortyish, clean-shaven, forgettable pleasant face; good navy overcoat over a suit, no tie; paper bait-shop coffee he never drinks. Sat a respectful foot from Nana. Keeps his hands on his knees, in sight. [#0292]
- **Tells (via Abernathy):** smells of hot brass (fresh-stamped), nothing of salt or sea; wristwatch ticks a few seconds slow on purpose. His footsteps are ruler-even and end at an empty parking space with no oil stain or tire marks. [#0293, #0297, #0307]
- Introduced himself "Daniel Hale. Meridian Mutual"; knew Nana's name ("Mrs. Lucero") unprompted, and Kino's "posted, not thrown." Says "I don't lie. It's bad for business." Said "You're welcome" to Kino, which registered on Kino's birthmark as a small "receipt." [#0296, #0303, #0304]
- His claimed authority: "I'm a reader with a briefcase"; the clerk in Colma writes what is true, he only reads it. [#0300]
- Answers given: Susmithaa herself entered the dispute (8 March archana, Livermore, asked for Arenya to be "kept"); he rented Box 112 **59 minutes** after her declaration; "no one's" decision closes it. Two strokes keep a dispute open; one runs out on her sixteenth day. [#0300]
- Carries a "real" envelope that closes the entry "in your favor; you receive" (a trap: receiving = being kept); the one in the box is a copy. Won't hand it over; "it's yours to take." Box open until **Friday 7:40**, after which he is "only a phone number." [#0296, #0300, #0303]
- Named Bell: filed "verification of beneficiary" that morning; with the notary journal Bell can verify without her saying her name. Hale "watched him" do it in March. [#0300]
- Asked Arenya what her aunt was carrying; she refused. Arenya put "I don't agree" on his record; he said "Noted… I'll carry that." [#0300, #0302, #0303]
- Offered a second route: unsay the archana in the same temple room; warned of other listeners there. [#0303]

## b1.c17.s01, Colma Notary (#0606, #0610, #0613)
- Phoned Rodel 9:12 p.m. Thursday (41 s): "Daniel Hale of Meridian Mutual"; said Arenya's full name; box "open to her until seven-forty… needn't sign… only identification. Offer her coffee." Flat, "polite like a recording."
- Fri 7:40 sharp stepped out of the grey Nissan (empty since before six, none seen entering), went in, came out 90 s later with a cream envelope held by one corner, unlooked-at. Nodded courteously toward Appa's hidden car, drove toward Colma. Later asked Rodel for tea; refused.

## b1.c17.s02 theory (#0619, #0622)
- Arenya and Kino conclude Hale may be a "form" the visitor fills when he needs a hand: the Nissan fogged from inside with no one breathing, and he emerged "poured into the coat" at 7:40. He knew their window because Rodel's book (written through Rodel) told him. Unconfirmed theory.
