# Daniel Hale

Voice on Susmithaa's phone voicemail (6:12 p.m., Monday) and the Sowri landline answering machine (that night). Claims to be from "beneficiary services, Meridian Mutual Life." Never seen. Flat, trained, accentless American; pleasant and unhurried. [#0158]

## Details
- Pronounces "Sowri" exactly as the family does (tapped r), which no outsider has done all week. [#0158]
- Knew the time of death as 3:19 a.m. Sunday, which the hospital never gave (they said "around 3:30"). [#0158]
- Claim ref **MM-031944**; says Susmithaa took out a policy in March naming a single beneficiary, to be verified "in person." [#0158]
- Second message (landline, addressed to Arenya): policy paid in cash, in person, at the Colma office on **19 March**; "covers more than money"; meet **noon Tuesday, Serramonte Library, second floor, by the periodicals**, bring ID; otherwise "happy to come to you," with a veiled threat about the family's mourning period. [#0167]
- Signed off "Keep that hand warm," which only Mr. Bell had seen (Arenya's inference). [#0167, #0168]
- Under his voice a second speaker counted "eleven, twelve," stopping on "hand." [#0167]
- Called the landline as a car pulled away dark from the end of the block. [#0164]
- The lamp flame ducked small and blue on the word "her" in his first message; Kino's birthmark pulsed. [#0158]
