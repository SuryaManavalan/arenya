# Benny Benasaraf

Heavyset, genial owner of [[serramonte-auto]]; Warriors windbreaker, hoses cars, calls customers "beautiful." Inherited the Polaroid wall from his father; buys film off eBay. Mundane, friendly, sharp enough to ask for names.

## Details
- Sold the beige Camry (auction car, in February; sold March) to **"Walter Bell"** for cash: old soft bills folded once, counted aloud in Spanish ("diez, quince, veinte"); signed with a fountain pen, read every page twice; clean title; address **Box 12, Colma Notary on Mission**. Bell refused a photo ("I don't photograph, Mr. Benasaraf"); the Polaroid came out with the face washed out. [#0417]
- Bell phoned him "last week, Tuesday maybe," asking him to take the names of anyone who asked about the car ("I'd like to send a thank-you"). [#0417]
- Met Kino and Arenya posing as **"Joe Ramos"** and his girlfriend/cousin; neither gave a real name; Kino told him to hold the lady dentist's Camry till Saturday. After, he phoned someone while looking at the corkboard. [#0419, #0420, #0425]

## Status
Alive, at the lot; probably reporting to Bell.
