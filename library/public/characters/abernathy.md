# Abernathy

A lesser spirit who presents as **squire** to the Sowri line's great light spirit, [[sahasrajyoti]]. Arrives at the side of whoever hosts it. Protected character [human: Round 2 Q6]; expected to become a big character [human: Round 1 Q8].

## Appearance
Wrong in a hard-to-name way, "like a person drawn from memory by someone who's forgotten the details." Lanky, slope-shouldered, shabby long coat that doesn't move with the wind; edges flicker; sometimes in two places for a frame.

## Voice & manner
A **cockney accent that sounds forced or put on**, jarring and annoying; occasionally slips into something older for half a word. Calls Arenya "guv", "love", "your Ladyship". [human: Round 3 Q5]

## Perception
**Only Arenya can perceive him** in the physical world [human]. In the bulk, other hosts sense him faintly and oddly [IDENTITY].

## Known history
Claims long service to the Light ("since before your nan's nan"). His own past is **blurry even to him** [human addendum].

- **b1.c15.s01:** First appears in the shared dream as Susmithaa's squire: swings a lantern on a chain, wrong-fingered hand, flickers between two places. Told Arenya "They count what they can see, love. Don't let 'em count *you*." His lantern strike on the net (roll 11 vs DC 15) failed; the net pulled the heat in and his light guttered to an ember. He shielded the flame Arenya held. His voice slipped out of cockney: "keep it lit, child." Says "Union rules." [#0009, #0013]
- When Susmithaa died he was torn loose "like a hook unpicked and set into someone else" and bound to Arenya. [#0014]
- Susmithaa warned Arenya he would say he was looking after her and "is lying." He told Arenya on `bond`: "I don't know what she meant," and that he filled her kettle from muscle memory ("Don't ask whose"). He speaks only on `bond` in the physical world. [#0018]
- Advised her to hide her warm right hand and not scratch or study the cold wrist spot. [#0018]

Bound to [[arenya-sowri]] as of b1.c15.s01. Secrets: [[abernathy-true-nature]] (restricted).
