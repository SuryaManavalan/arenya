# Mrs. K. Ramaswamy

Volunteer office secretary, Livermore temple. About 70, half-moon glasses, gold nose stud, pen on a beaded chain, blue cardigan. Initials K.R. Mundane. [#0506]

## Details
- #0511 wrongly named her "K. Padmavathi" with initials K.P.; the DM corrected it in #0513. Canon: Ramaswamy, K.R.
- Remembered the "grey suit, insurance" man of 19 Mar (Hale), who paid ten cents in an old coin. Susmithaa had said "Cash, Aunty, and no copies." [#0511, #0515]
- Struck stub 2207, handed Arenya Hale's card, hid the March page under a dhoti cloth, and promises not to see "nice shoes." [#0519, #0540]
- Hale's forgetting: she did not notice he never took off his shoes. [#0537]
