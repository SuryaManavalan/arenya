# Pruitt

A big man with a trimmed beard. Former member of Team Four (Caleb's crew until Thursday; his Crown leash still held). Crown-flat: he acts on instruction and does not look away when told to hold a stare.

## Appearance
- Big, trimmed beard. Does not squint in high beams.

## Voice & manner
- Flat, reading from the inside of his own head. Answers the eyes, not the question. Looks like a man who has done the arithmetic and does not like the answer.

## Known history (b1.c20.s05, Sun 8 Nov, Dogpatch, ~23:50)
- Drove Mill truck [[mill-truck-d2-04]] down a dead rail spur off the tank farm and opened the rear door: a bolted chair facing the door, and [[lockbox-l-3]] on its seat.
- Says he was paid by "a phone, a voice I didn't know, no name," with cash in a Mill envelope under the seat in bay three. Instructions: park it, raise the door, say Caleb's name once, walk away.
- Took orders from "something that sounded like Four" and says "Four's gone. I don't take orders from it."
- Set the truck keys on the bumper and walked into the dark toward the black water. Caleb: "He's not the one who sent for us."
