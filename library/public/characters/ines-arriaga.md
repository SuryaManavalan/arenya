# Inés Arriaga

The suspended intermediary (the DM's term). Mid-forties, rain jacket, lanyard tucked inside the collar so only the cord shows. Ran "the register for that room for six years." Her own thermal receipt reads **c/o Arriaga, I.**, in the mailbox since Friday.

## Appearance
- Mid-forties, rain jacket. Lanyard tucked inside her collar (the MO-0120 intermediary was seen with a backwards lanyard; not yet confirmed to be her).

## Voice & manner
- Dry, careful, speaks across distance rather than approaching. Hands open, palms to the light, when she gets out of the car.

## Known history (b1.c20.s05, Sun 8 Nov, 23:14–23:42, Pier 70)
- Waited in a beige sedan against the far fence (plate ends -41, [#1521]); windscreen fogged from inside.
- Came out at 23:37, both hands out. Said she is not Lucero and was "told to be the one who reads it aloud." Arrived at 8 p.m. "so I'd have time to decide not to." [#1524]
- Refused to read the second line of her receipt: a name meant for "the water under the pier," to let someone inside be "let past looking" for one count of sixty at the 23:40 threshold. [#1527]
- Warned that Mill truck D2-04 in loading bay 3 must leave by 23:42. Set the receipt on the sedan's hood and stepped back three paces at Arenya's request. [#1528, #1530]
