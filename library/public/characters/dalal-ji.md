# Dalal-ji

First met in person at [[san-bruno-satsang]], Sat 31 Oct. Presents as the second-Sunday archana priest of the Livermore temple, the "H. Dalal" on the endorsement of check 2207 and the "Mr. Dalal / Pt. H. Dalal" of Rodel's pencil line and the 8 March register.

## Appearance
- Sixties, silver at the temples, reading glasses on a cord, white veshti, sacred thread, **black dress socks**, plain blue ballpoint behind the right ear. [#0813]
- Very soft, kind voice; looks at Arenya "like a man who has read her horoscope."

## Voice & manner
- Speaks English and careful Tamil. Gentle. Offers seats and food ("Sit, sit"). Asks nothing directly.
- Laid the pen flat, cap toward himself, when Malachi's weight shifted. Looked "like a man revising an estimate." [#0817]

## Known history (b1.c17.s07)
- Mentioned Susmithaa's "beautiful" March archana "for this one," $101. [#0813]
- Said in answer to Malachi's "box held for you on vacancy": "I don't keep boxes, sir. I keep names." [#0817]
- To Amma, in Tamil: "I said her name to God, amma. That is my work. God has many ears in this country. Some of them pay." [#0817]
- Warned: tomorrow (Sun 1 Nov) at nine someone "who does not know the words" will come to the side door with a pen, "not me"; offered himself as one who does know them, there until the prasadam is served. [#0817]
- Left his blue pen lying on the steel table. Did not follow the family. [#0817, #0821]

## Open questions
- Is he Hale's alias, a hired real priest, or a separate person? His warning offers to stand between them and the pen-bearer, yet he was the endorsee of the forged check. The oxfords in the vestibule (size 10–11, grey ruled dust in the welt, Ix: "pencil") were presumably his, but he sat in black socks, so "toes-out, taken off properly" respects thresholds, unlike Hale's visitor at Livermore (shoes on).
- "Pay" may mean the 2207 cash flow, or a hint about who his client is.

## Later (b1.c17.s09)
- By the Sat afternoon he was not in the hall. His reading glasses (cord coiled around them) were left folded on the lamp table by the shrine door; the oxfords were gone from the rack, lifted straight up, leaving a clean two-shoe rectangle. Malachi took the glasses in a handkerchief (Kino: don't wear them; keep from salt until Nana says). [#0862, #0864, #0867]
- Open: whether the "collected" oxfords were his or the runner's is still unresolved.

## b1.c17.s10
- His glasses, kept in [[dalal-glasses-blue-jar]] at Nana's, greyed the salt and now pull a grey thread to the 110 stop. Nana: "a man who keeps names, who's lost his eyes, comes looking for his eyes first." [#0887, #0892]

## Status
Gone from the San Bruno satsang by Sat 31 Oct afternoon; his glasses are with Malachi. Whereabouts unknown.
