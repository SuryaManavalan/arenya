# Lakshmi Sowri ("Amma")

Arenya's mother; married to [[ravi-sowri]] (Raghavan) 34 years; [[susmithaa]]'s sister-in-law, "twenty-nine years my sister." Reading glasses she "pretends not to need," house sari and cardigan. Feeds everyone ("Sit. There's rice"). Reads documents "like a lab result: once, all the way through." (Library note: earlier entries had her unnamed; she calls Arenya "Kanna.")

## Details
- Kept the March bank statement in a manila folder, **SUSI — ACCOUNTS**; the back of check 2207 shows a forged *R. Sowri* (not Appa's small left-leaning engineer hand) and "Pay to the order of H. Dalal." She knew in one second, asked Appa the night after the funeral, was told "Leave it, Lakshmi. It's handled," and hid the folder from him, not from Arenya. [#0563]
- Explains Susmithaa chose the Livermore temple because Daly City's was four streets away and "everybody's aunty is at ours." [#0563]
- Prays in a language other than English when frightened; lays a rag folded square when she has decided something. [#0563, #0574]
- Took the red-string key from Appa's finger and laid it at the foot of the lamp: "Now the lamp has it. Not you, not her." [#0586]
- Knows now (from s12) that Appa said yes on the phone in March; keys to the side door: hers, his, Susmithaa's. [#0574, #0583]
- Does Saturday seva at [[san-bruno-satsang]]; in her plain Saturday cotton, scolds the pepper in the kitchen. Told [[dalal-ji]] in Tamil: "You cashed it. I saw the back of that check. My husband never wrote like that in his life." Turned off the flame herself. [b1.c17.s07 #0813, #0817]
- Found the [[pink-archana-slip-lakshmi]] in her left Clark: "I did not ask for this." Handed it flat to Arenya. Demands everything told in the car; asked Appa of the grey hand "since when?" [#0817, #0821]
- Arenya told her short version: Susi's death isn't an accident, Appa's signature is being used, they come Sunday at nine, so the family goes to Nana's. [#0818]

- s08: Arenya told her everything on 280. Amma: in March Susi phoned on a Sunday (she was making dosa) for stars for a "family health archana" at Livermore; she gave Arenya's, Kino's and Appa's from memory but **not her own**, and Susi didn't ask. "So somebody else knew it." To Appa: "You had since March." Asked "What do they want her *for*?" Said "Susmithaa Sowri" aloud, then Appa did, and the cold drew back into the envelope. [#0829, #0832]
- Named "ALTERNATE SIGNATORY: L. SOWRI ✓" on the [[custody-receipt-proximal-11]]: "That's what the slip bought. If you won't, they ask me." After her left shoe and sock came off, the thread to her foot went limp and the foot felt "better" (the sock burned with pencil smoke). Declared "Then we don't get tired." [#0841, #0850, #0851, #0855]
- Took Appa's green Sheaffer, wrapped it in a napkin and tucked it at her collarbone, then put it in Nana's salted clay jar herself ("Hm"). Accepted that Malachi will introduce himself tonight. [#0855, #0859]

## b1.c17.s10 (Sat 31 Oct, Nana's kitchen) [#0879, #0892, #0900]
- Asked Malachi "Did you watch my Susmithaa?"; on his confession of being ordered to forget: "My husband also signed what someone put in front of him." Neither forgiveness nor sentence.
- Announced she would face the runner herself ("I'm the one with the pencil already next to my name"); Arenya argued her down ("you're the second… the person they ticked the box for"). Agreed: stands **behind** Arenya, barefoot, Appa's capped Sheaffer in her pocket "not to write, so that I know where it is." Took Arenya's hand (grip from crossing El Camino); called her "kanna." Ate for the first time since the truck. Appa sleeps on Nana's couch.
