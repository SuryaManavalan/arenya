# The Runner

Caleb's name for the kind of figure who serves a Shepherd's-crook "runner's book." He belongs to the side of the office that *closes*, not the crew side. Whether this is a single man or a type is unresolved.

## Details (per Caleb)
- Holds a carbon-set clipboard bound at the top with a steel spine, flat against his chest in both arms "like a hymnal." Keeps the stubs, which are how the office knows a receipt was delivered. [#0851, #0855]
- Does not break in. He waits at the curb until someone comes out and signs "just to make him leave." He is polite and takes his shoes off if asked. [#0851]
- Black oxfords, laced tight, polished but grey along the welt like pencil dust; lanyard worn backward. [#0855]
- Never drives: he took SamTrans route 110 from the Linda Mar stop on Highway 1. Caleb: "I think it's in the rules." [#0855]
- Precedent: Mr. Okafor, Linda Mar, Pacifica (stucco house, boat on a trailer), two Februaries ago. His wife "was ours." He signed on the car hood at 7:40 a.m. [#0855]
- Table inferences (Kino, Malachi): bus routes are a map. A man without a car keeps a schedule. The oxfords left at [[san-bruno-satsang]] may be bait or a habit. Rule from Malachi: if he arrives, nobody answers, offers water, a chair or a kindness. [#0854, #0857, #0858]
