# The Runner

Caleb's name for the kind of figure who serves a Shepherd's-crook "runner's book." He belongs to the side of the office that *closes*, not the crew side. Whether this is a single man or a type is unresolved.

## Details (per Caleb)
- Holds a carbon-set clipboard bound at the top with a steel spine, flat against his chest in both arms "like a hymnal." Keeps the stubs, which are how the office knows a receipt was delivered. [#0851, #0855]
- Does not break in. He waits at the curb until someone comes out and signs "just to make him leave." He is polite and takes his shoes off if asked. [#0851]
- Black oxfords, laced tight, polished but grey along the welt like pencil dust; lanyard worn backward. [#0855]
- Never drives: he took SamTrans route 110 from the Linda Mar stop on Highway 1. Caleb: "I think it's in the rules." [#0855]
- Precedent: Mr. Okafor, Linda Mar, Pacifica (stucco house, boat on a trailer), two Februaries ago. His wife "was ours." He signed on the car hood at 7:40 a.m. [#0855]
- Table inferences (Kino, Malachi): bus routes are a map. A man without a car keeps a schedule. The oxfords left at [[san-bruno-satsang]] may be bait or a habit. Rule from Malachi: if he arrives, nobody answers, offers water, a chair or a kindness. [#0854, #0857, #0858]

## The visit (b1.c17.s11, Sun 1 Nov 8:38–8:49 a.m.)
- Stepped off the 110 at 8:38 (bus reflection in the shelter glass ran long, too many windows, every face turned uphill). Looked at Kino's boots, touched the clipboard with two fingers ("already on the list"), held nothing out. [#0915]
- Appearance up close: forties, shaving nick, lanyard card blank side out; breathes in and out with the fog bank and did not blink until the negotiation (first blink: after Malachi's win). Flat, pleasant clerk's voice. [#0923, #0934]
- Stated terms: "Return of personal effects. Proximal eleven. Signatory on file, R. Sowri." Needs a struck line from signatory or alternate; "Objects don't sign." Called the comal "a settlement plate." [#0923]
- Admitted "opened in error is a real category": the R is Appa's hand but **the ink is the office's**. An error "isn't erased, it's *returned*": the balance goes up the line to whoever issued the ink ("somebody's bad week"). A void needs a **witness who keeps names** (Dalal-ji), who reads everything facing him; the price of a clean void is *being seen*. [#0931, #0934]
- Said the 9:12 tick is booked for him: "Me and what I carry back up... The balance sits on my lap, not on any seat of yours." [#0941]
- Took a **grey pencil from behind his ear** and struck the line aloud: "Proximal eleven. Opened in error. Issuer's ink. Returned up the line. Closed." Second stroke: "Alternate struck with it." Left with "Good morning," down the middle of the sidewalk, toward the 9:12. [#0941]
- Did not enter the salt, did not take anything handed, and kept toes one inch short of the comal handle. Persuasion by Malachi (16 vs DC 15) won the void. [#0931–#0934]
