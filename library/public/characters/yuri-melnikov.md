# Yuri Melnikov

62, Russian immigrant, retired architect, Tartine regular (morning bun, double espresso, navy scarf, window seat). Played chess with his brother Saturdays [c02]. Complained satellites made the night sky too busy; no Monolith link [c11]. Froze and stared at four laptop users at a corner table (~25 s on video, "almost a minute" per Ava) then collapsed [c02, c07]. Officially an aneurysm. Wife: [[galina-melnikov]].
