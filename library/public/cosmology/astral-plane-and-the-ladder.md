# The astral plane and the ladder

Planes ladder up from the physical: **Astral → Cosmic → Temporal → Ethereal → Celestial → Causal** (the book titles); each book opens the next, "with new levels come new devils" [human]. Only the Astral is in play in Book 1 (the later names are series structure, known to staff).

The astral is not a mirror of the physical; it's an emergent world of anthropomorphized archetypes and properties with shadows/projections of the physical [human]. Rifts (not yet known to characters) let spirits leak into the physical world and meta mages use spirit powers there [outline].
