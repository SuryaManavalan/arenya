# Visarjana at Livermore

A sankalpam (a vow made to the deity) is stopped on paper by the office but closed only by a priest's visarjana. [#0519]

## Details
- Hale's "un-saying" route required Arenya's full name said aloud in the hall; Sastri closed it instead by star, gotra and her fingers on a brass plate, saying *ayam* ("this one") for the name, in the sanctum, door a hand's width ajar, until he poured the water and said *samaptam*. [#0523, #0527, #0532]
- Condition: the subject may not say her name, even silently, until the water is poured. Arenya used a "poodle" thought; Wis save DC 14 with advantage: 19. [#0527, #0528]
- Trap: a planted flip phone, call timer running, imitated Amma ("Ari? Kanna...") to draw out her name; Arenya noted wrong word order and an unusual hour. Kino folded the phone against his chest. [#0527, #0530, #0531]
- Result: the phone died (CALL ENDED 0:41, number 650-755-xxxx = Colma Notary & Mail); Kino keeps a phone-shaped numb cold rectangle over his sternum (cold like the freezer, heart slow beneath, no pain). The clock and salt returned to normal. [#0532, #0536]
- The Friday abhishekam reading (a year of Arenya's name) is closed with it.
