# Spirit houses and hosting

- Six houses: **light, dark, heavy, sharp, flexible, sticky** (sticky also akin to magnetism) [human].
- Spirits are metaphysical entities emerging from the archetypal properties and mechanics of physical reality, anthropomorphized in the astral plane [human].
- **Hosting** is both bloodline and reincarnation; esoteric families used high **tantra technology** to tie spirit lines to bloodlines. Details deliberately open [human].
- A family's main spirit can be incomprehensibly complex (dragon, angel, Hindu deity). Lesser spirits serve it as squires/retainers [human].
- Place-spirits can adopt an outsider as champion [human]. Arenya is a child of light and dark [human].
- Seeing a spirit reads as psychosis to an ordinary person; spirits never look like ordinary animals [human].
