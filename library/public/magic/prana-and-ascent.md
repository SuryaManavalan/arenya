# Prana, chakras and Ascent

[human] Prana (life force) can be aimed into astral and higher bodies; like kundalini, the chakra a being is currently embodied in shows its level. Bodies: physical, astral, ether, … causal; a fully incarnated being needs all.
[dm-proposed] "Ascent 1–7" (root to crown); Ascent 3+ needed to fight in the astral; Book 1 heroes ~3–4; rises by story milestones.
