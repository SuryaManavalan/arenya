# The bulk and spirit battles

**The bulk** [human term] = the extra dimensions stacked on physical reality; higher planes are deeper layers, each with its own clock inside the instants of the one below. Mathematical flavor: three dimensions plus a second set of three extra [human]; not tied strictly to the time notes.

**Spirit battles** are fought there. Combatants commit whole energy and astral bodies, so physical bodies freeze, staring at the opponent [human]. Physical time slows hugely rather than stops; hosts/carried allies can join mid-battle [human Round 2 Q3]. A stabbed frozen body is "probably cooked" [human]. Losing doesn't always kill: prana is pointed into astral bodies; death comes when the astral body is killed [human].

[dm-proposed] numbers: ~1 astral round per physical second; Entranced condition; Astral HP; Astral Wounds (see mechanics.md).
