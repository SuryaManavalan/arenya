# Nana's Salt Line

Folk warding at Nana Itzel's house (laid Fri 30 Oct), mixed with something grey that glints once in candlelight.

## Rules stated at the table
- Unbroken line along the porch foot; a body-wide gap by the glider closed by Kino after the last person crosses. Only Nana's word opens it ("*pase*"); a guest waits outside until invited (pinch of salt at the guest's feet = invitation). Open for no voice until sunrise, "not even mine." [#0733, #0737, #0743]
- Salt stops what **walks**, not what **talks**; only not answering holds against a voice. A tally needs a witness. [#0754, #0755]
- Clay "keeps its mouth shut"; metal talks. Wet cloth, clay comal, grey salt ring silenced Shepherd's phone. [#0771, #0772]
- Written things can be found; Malachi and Arenya keep the notebook shut inside it. [#0740, #0741]
- **Opening rite (Sat 31 Oct, s08):** anything sealed with "their glue" does not enter the house. It is opened on the salt, half in and half out, carried in the non-signing hand away from the chest, and opened with wood (Nana's comal spatula), never a blade or pen. The reader is the named person or whoever stands for the name, and the family decides who. A new unbroken line was poured by Kino inside the gate. Salt frosts "threads" where they cross but does not cut them. See [[custody-receipt-proximal-11]]. [#0836, #0841, #0842]
- Nana refused to be moved: "I'm not a box." The pestle sits on the form as a paperweight. [#0851]
- **Greying (Sat 31 Oct, s10):** Malachi crossed cleanly but a hand's width of line went grey ("pencil rubbed on a thumb") because of Dalal's wrapped glasses in his pocket, not his shadow. Nana: cover with fresh salt, don't sweep ("sweep it and you've moved it"). Arenya poured it; the grey stays visible beneath like a scar. Marked "less" because carried clean. Guests wait unspoken until asked; Nana's invitation set Malachi's seat at the end, back to her wall. [#0882, #0887]
- Ix the cat faced west all night, lay down across the threshold when the watch was over. The Lady (per Kino) stands outside, facing west. [#0733, #0759]
