# The Range of Spirits

Human decree (#0906, binding): spirits come in every shape, size, form and non-form.

## Details
- Kinds: **human-like** spirits; **nature** spirits (trees, fog, tides, mountains); **archetypal** spirits (the Clerk, the Mother, the Threshold); **characteristic** spirits (heat, rust, hurry, a city's pride); and **unimaginably abstract, incomprehensible** entities. [#0906]
- The **six houses** (light, dark, heavy, sharp, flexible, sticky) are **attractors in the space of spirits**: domains that pull every kind of spirit toward them. A house is the shape of the pull, not of the body. [#0906]
- Authorial direction: introduce spirits through the familiar (human spirits, done), then make them steadily stranger. **Book 1 is about the astral plane and should climax with massive kaiju-like spirits.** [#0906]
- Larger and stranger spirits are harder to perceive; to an ordinary mind they look like psychosis (mechanics file).
- Early B1 instances: the Sleeping Lady (a mountain), Sahasrajyoti (a family's main spirit), el Contador (a clerk archetype), and in s11 the breathing fog bank with something vast beneath it (see [[hillcrest-fog-bank]]).
