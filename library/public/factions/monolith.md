# Monolith

Typhus Quinn's ~$80B space-tech company: micro-satellites, satellite automation, self-building rovers, AI-run off-world manufacturing, and asteroid mining pitched as sustainable [c10]. Logo: black circle with a vertical line. Described by Arenya as "infrastructure. Power. Orbiting influence." Employees include Adonis Quinn and Elias Khatri. Email domain monolith.systems.

Restricted: a Monolith subsidiary runs the Lattice Program; see [[collection-program]].
