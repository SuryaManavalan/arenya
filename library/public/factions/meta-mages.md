# Meta mages

Mass-produced spirit users: Typhus's army. Weak, dependent link to the spirit realm via a made-up chemical plus a neural-link chip that induces hypnosis. Individually weaker than true hosts; in numbers they can take hosts down [human]. Tech names (Crown, Vesper) are [dm-proposed] and not yet known to anyone but participants.

## What's been seen in-world
Four laptop users at Tartine; "forgettable by design" men—blank, idle-less—like the man outside the bakery [c02, c07, c11].
