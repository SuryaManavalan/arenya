# Los que cuentan

Nana Itzel's folk name, from her grandmother, for whoever catalogs people who carry power: "priests' ledgers" in her grandmother's time, "clipboards from the government" in her mother's, "cardigans with rental cars" now. "Different coats. Same hands. They write down people like your aunt... then the people go quiet." [#0143]

## Details
- Echoes Susmithaa's "Don't let them count you" and the collection team's "counting" net (see Arenya's dream). [#0140, #0145]
- b1.c15.s07: Nana, citing Susmithaa (told years ago over Christmas wine): "they count the ones who carry." While still hunting they don't say the number aloud; saying it means they have "stopped looking and started collecting." After a counting voice said "eleven, twelve" under [[daniel-hale]]'s message, Nana: "Eleven was somebody. Twelve is you." Not Arenya's age, her number. [#0171]
- Whether this is a real historical lineage of the modern program or only Nana's framing is an open question the DM put to the human. [#0144]
