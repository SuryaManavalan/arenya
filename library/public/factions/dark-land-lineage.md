# Dark land lineage

San Francisco's land lineage "rolls up indigenous American beliefs (the peoples who lived here, plus Olmec and Maya)" [human]. [dm-proposed] Here *dark* means fog, night, underworld, dreams, the hidden and fertile — not evil — with Mesoamerican currents (jaguar sun, layered Xibalba, sacred mountain). Keeper: [[nana-itzel]]; champion-in-training: [[kino-balam]]. Writing note: a fictional tradition, not a depiction of any living people's sacred practice; indigenous characters are full people.
