# Terms and customs

- **kanna** — Tamil endearment ("dear one"); Arenya's aunts use it [c03].
- **Aunty** — honorific for older women in the Sowri family circle.
- **Renny** — Teddy's nickname for Arenya; tolerated, not liked [c01].
- **Don't flinch** — Brooke's rule for borrowed credibility [c09].
