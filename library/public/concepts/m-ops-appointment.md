# M-Ops: the appointment

Per the intermediary (nat 20 persuasion by Malachi): **M is a role, "above, not beside" Mill Operations.** [#1414]

## Details
- The blank signature sheet on MO-0117's dash is an **appointment**, not a debt or confession. Whoever signs is M-Ops "for the term of the instrument": the chair, the key, every box keyed to the role, including lockbox L-3 (likely Abernathy's cuff, [[intake-exception-0001]]). [#1414, #1415]
- The chair has been empty since summer; they wanted it filled by someone who "had already sold the thing that makes a person say no" (fits Malachi's sold name, [[malachi-name-lease]]). [#1414]
- Guarantor "M" on the Bayline lease is thus a role, not a person.
- Abernathy: at the word "chair" the cuff's hook went **warm, not cold**; it may have been cut to fit someone other than him. Unconfirmed. [#1418]
- Party rule: nobody signs.
- The brass puck lock is role-keyed ("SEE M-OPS").

- **b1.c20.s02 (Sat 7 Nov):** the [[tide-envoy]] offered to give "the hour the Chair's keeper looks away," in exchange for a holder at the throat on Sunday 11:40 p.m. The hour is set; the keeper is not named to the party. [#1454, #1462]
- A line in Arenya's notebook from the envoy's sender: "He will be at the Chair's door first. Choose who is there." [#1463]

## b1.c20.s04 (Sun 8 Nov, dusk)
- The Sunday check on the M-Ops role log is set for 22:30 from a clean Building 2 kiosk at [[pier-70]]. Typhus's text: the 18:12 Friday request carried no badge number, only the tag "T4." Team Four was dissolved Friday, so "somebody is still answering to it." [#1501]
- The role log will show whoever sits in the chair next. Arenya and Malachi told Typhus not to use the Lantern desk. [#1493, #1496]

## b1.c20.s05 (Sun 8 Nov, 10:50 p.m.–11:56 p.m.)
- The M-Ops signer line on Typhus's role log filled and cleared four times between 22:30 and 22:58, a different name each time; the last was four letters. Nothing kept the old names. [#1520]
- The chair is real: a plain steel-framed chair bolted to the floor of the mill truck's rear, **facing the door**, with [[lockbox-l-3]] on its seat. [#1535]
- The receipt on the box: **HELD FOR M-OPS (VACANT) · SIGN BY MON 06:00 · c/o LUCERO, I.** Pen note: *Alternate accepted.* [#1535]
- The "keeper" appears as a voice: [[pruitt]] was paid by a phone, and took orders from "something that sounded like Four." [#1539]
- The intermediary [[ines-arriaga]] refused to read the second line of her "c/o Arriaga, I." receipt, which names the water under the pier. [#1527]
