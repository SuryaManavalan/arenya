# El Contador (what is said)

Among the old stonecutter families of Colma, *el Contador* is a clerk that keeps the valley's dead. Folk saying: **you don't lie on paper near Colma.** Nobody prays to it; it is neither devil nor saint.

For the fuller account (origin, mechanics of a written "no", dos testigos) see the restricted entry; DM asked for this split (#0203).
