# The Throat

A tall, narrow, wet, hollow pile-shaped thing standing in the old drydock sump under the Crisp Ave pump house: the "twelfth piling." Lattice-tuned. [#1391]

## Details
- Drinks the tide a finger's width at a time; the water doesn't return. Its pull pressed the [[slack-water-spirit]] against the shore. [#1391]
- Tuned to every pitch but one. The missing pitch is a **true name said into it**. Its held note is the breath before a swallow. [#1391]
- Tuned to Ollie's call; when he was withdrawn it slid "across names like coats on a peg" and slowed on Malachi (pen pointed at him). Kino's stillness (Nana's house in the lining) hid him. [#1402, #1411]
- Dormant and unfed after the intermediary stood down; "waiting for another Thursday." [#1414]
- Something enormous offshore, which knows one of their names, listened for the swallow, then eased. [#1391, #1414]
- Rule: nobody says a name near the door.

## Open questions
