# Foam-hands

Hand-sized crawling shapes of seawater and kelp, present in both worlds when the rift opened. AC 11, ~7 HP. Hurt by salt, fire, blows and light; drawn to heat (Ava's palm) and to a pulse (Arenya's cold wrist).

## Details
- Four came at Holy Cross: two on Arenya's entranced body (attacks on it auto-crit), one on Amma's wrist, one under the turf wrapped around the canister. All destroyed by Malachi, Kino, Nana's salt, Ava, Caleb. [#1023–#1090]
- Cold crit took Arenya's body to 2/9 HP; frostbite superficial, no scar. [#1051, #1097]
