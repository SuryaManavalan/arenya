# Spirit of Hurry

Archetypal spirit (rifts step 4) met on US-101 at the Cesar Chavez interchange, Tue Nov 3, 5:52 p.m. A headless body the length of the interchange, made of every commuter's "I should have left ten minutes ago"; its front is wherever the gap is. Not malicious, hungry. [#1182]

## Details
- Effects: dash clocks and tide-table app skip ahead, hearts race "late, late"; phantom brake lights across all lanes; the kelp ticks fast (5–7). [#1182]
- Beaten as a 3-before-2 skill challenge, four for four: Ava's two-count and "that's a symptom" (Wis 14), Kino's herd-calming (AH 16), Caleb's off-beat (21), Abernathy's ticket-taker livery ("Ticket, please") in the bulk, where it appears as a queue of coats tapping watches. [#1183–#1192]
- Arenya's Lamp-Eyes (1 prana, 23) showed the whole spirit being drawn east at the Bayshore split toward the Hunters Point stack, thin threads running down into the shipyard: the Mill drinks the city's hurry as well as its tide. [#1192]
- Clocks settled at 5:53 afterward.
