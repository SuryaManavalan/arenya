# The Breathing Fog Bank

A long low fog bank along the ridge from Westlake to the sea on Sun 1 Nov that **rises and settles on an eleven-second cycle**, like the back of something enormous breathing under a blanket. Each rise dims the sodium lamps and the shelter streetlight. [#0915, #0920, #0923]

## Details
- Caleb saw it breathe twice from the porch at ~8:10; the streetlight over the 110 shelter had dimmed at 2:17 a.m. as if someone walked under it. [#0905, #0915]
- The runner breathes in time with it. [#0923]
- At ~8:48, as Proximal eleven was struck, it **stopped half-risen for one count**, the whole bank lifting off the hills "far higher than fog goes." Only Ava (at bay C, facing it) saw, beneath it, a shape too big for her mind to hold the edges of **turn over like a sleeper turning from a light**, then lie down farther out over the water. Lamps returned to full. [#0941, #0946]
- Ava timestamped it: 8:48. Her oven-light at Tartine had pointed SW all night. [#0913, #0946]
- Nature/large-scale spirit in the sense of [[range-of-spirits]]; its stance toward the family is unknown.
