# The Slack-Water Spirit

Bus-sized, heaped woman of oyster shell, eelgrass hair and grey clay (the old south shore), at Candlestick Point. Calls the heron her "brother." [#1125]

## Details
- Bulk face: lies braced on her side with heels dug in to the ankle; dozens of "ledger lines" (water ruled perfectly straight) hook into her shell-skin and run NE to the Hunters Point cranes (black ledger-pens, nibs down). Each pulse (every 11 s) she loses an inch. "They're writing me down. When they've written all of me, I go where the pens go." [#1125]
- Eye like a tidepool with a crab in it. Voice from all wet things. Calls Kino "land's child"; Abernathy "a filed man, without his ledger." [#1125]
- Hasn't seen a bow in a hundred years; answered Arenya because she asked properly (Persuasion 20). [#1134]
- Told them: a stone house that grinds (the Mill) under the old dry dock; the pens are **iron stakes in the real mud**, set at low water by men; a "white door on wheels" hums by them and each tide another line is ruled. "Pull a stake, a line comes loose. Pull enough and I can stand up." [#1134]
- To Abernathy: the hand on the lines is older than the yard; "somebody tore your page on purpose." [#1134]
- Won her first-ever inch when the line hesitated at Abernathy; lost it again when the survey rod went in left of the second piling. Asked for "one more tide, with manners." Digs in for another tide. [#1143, #1153, #1158]
- The 11-second cadence matches the Hillcrest fog bank's breathing; connection unconfirmed.

- **b1.c18.s05 (Tue 3 Nov, 7:51 p.m.):** with all three stakes pulled she is "looser, not free": under Kino's two fingers the water pushed against the current once, "like a hand closing over a hand"; rust reek thinned to plain salt. Kino: "Falta lo demás" ("the rest is still owed"). Survey rod still stands and reads one line. [#1234, #1235]

- **b1.c19.s04 (Wed 4 Nov, 8:53–8:58 p.m., Second piling):** Mill crew came to re-drive three new stakes at slack (8:56). Voice of mud-suck: "I am what is left of her while the rest is owed." Said that nailed she would hold their song Thursday; loose she goes "to the one who reads her" (Arenya). "Choose for me. I am tired of men choosing." [#1333] Kino *asked*, offering his door rather than an order; she: "You asked. Nobody asks." Her price: Kino drives **his own pulled stake (#2)** into **Nana's door-sill**, so she is "held by a house and not a ledger", keeps no song for them, keeps his salt and bowl, waits for the sea. He accepted; salt throw spoiled the first nail (Survival 14 vs DC 13), the maul rang dead, rime climbed the stake. She leaned on the ruled stroke on his forearm "like a cat," said "Held," and promised to be **at the creek mouth** when he drives it ("Go home before the ebb"). She withdrew toward Sunnydale and the culvert up toward the Sunset. Kino is now her **keeper**; her inch is still owed; he must walk her to the sea. [#1338, #1341, #1344, #1346]

- **b1.c20.s01 (Fri 6 Nov, dusk, creek mouth):** Kino and Caleb (Caleb at Kino's side, hands open) reach the creek mouth. She does not surface; she speaks from an inch of water over the stones. She **defers Kino's inch** ("Not tonight... I owe it nothing and you nothing"). She warns that **something is coming up her river wanting a door**; it has asked her for a name, and she said she would ask "the one who owes me." Summons: dawn Saturday, with salt, the Gate-talker (Arenya) and Caleb, "the man with the shaking hands," whom it has asked about. Across the inlet the Bayline lamps come on at once. [#1444]
- Ledger-line status: the stake in Nana's sill holds her "family"; the Lamp-doorway and the Missing Man are not hers. [#1444]

- **b1.c20.s02 (Sat 7 Nov dawn, creek mouth):** she confirms that the envoy is "not a spirit of my river. Not a spirit of the sea," and that it has been asking the kelp about "the Gate-talker" and "whose name opens it." The envoy's sender is "the one the creek runs to." The seven kelp strands stood upright; an eighth rose after the envoy left. [#1447, #1463]
- The sea "keeps one clock," and the tide-woman's count ("eleven-forty") is the same hour the envoy keeps. [#1454]
