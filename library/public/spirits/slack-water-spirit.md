# The Slack-Water Spirit

Bus-sized, heaped woman of oyster shell, eelgrass hair and grey clay (the old south shore), at Candlestick Point. Calls the heron her "brother." [#1125]

## Details
- Bulk face: lies braced on her side with heels dug in to the ankle; dozens of "ledger lines" (water ruled perfectly straight) hook into her shell-skin and run NE to the Hunters Point cranes (black ledger-pens, nibs down). Each pulse (every 11 s) she loses an inch. "They're writing me down. When they've written all of me, I go where the pens go." [#1125]
- Eye like a tidepool with a crab in it. Voice from all wet things. Calls Kino "land's child"; Abernathy "a filed man, without his ledger." [#1125]
- Hasn't seen a bow in a hundred years; answered Arenya because she asked properly (Persuasion 20). [#1134]
- Told them: a stone house that grinds (the Mill) under the old dry dock; the pens are **iron stakes in the real mud**, set at low water by men; a "white door on wheels" hums by them and each tide another line is ruled. "Pull a stake, a line comes loose. Pull enough and I can stand up." [#1134]
- To Abernathy: the hand on the lines is older than the yard; "somebody tore your page on purpose." [#1134]
- Won her first-ever inch when the line hesitated at Abernathy; lost it again when the survey rod went in left of the second piling. Asked for "one more tide, with manners." Digs in for another tide. [#1143, #1153, #1158]
- The 11-second cadence matches the Hillcrest fog bank's breathing; connection unconfirmed.

- **b1.c18.s05 (Tue 3 Nov, 7:51 p.m.):** with all three stakes pulled she is "looser, not free": under Kino's two fingers the water pushed against the current once, "like a hand closing over a hand"; rust reek thinned to plain salt. Kino: "Falta lo demás" ("the rest is still owed"). Survey rod still stands and reads one line. [#1234, #1235]
