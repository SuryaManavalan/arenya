# The Sleeping Lady

The great dark spirit of the land: alive, ancient, asleep, breathing; echoed in Mount Tamalpais [dm-proposed]. In Arenya's dreams a glowing-red mountain that "breathed… A question that hadn't yet been asked" [c07]; in c14 it stirs as she tumbles. [dm-proposed] It chose Arenya, over the champion raised for it ([[kino-balam]]) [human: adopted outsider].

## b1.c15.s03
- At 3:01 a.m., the mountain "rolled over" like a sleeper, a slow movement felt through rock but visible in nothing, and turned her face south toward the city and something lit under the fog. Kino's birthmark ("thumbprint") went cold; Ix whined. [#0042]
- Nana's reading: this is a turned *face*, not a back. The land looks at what is new, old and "not from here." [#0045]
- Later, her wind fell into step beside Kino and his birthmark's cold eased briefly, a sign she has not abandoned him. [#0053]
