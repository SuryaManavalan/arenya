# The Sleeping Lady

The great dark spirit of the land: alive, ancient, asleep, breathing; echoed in Mount Tamalpais [dm-proposed]. In Arenya's dreams a glowing-red mountain that "breathed… A question that hadn't yet been asked" [c07]; in c14 it stirs as she tumbles. [dm-proposed] It chose Arenya, over the champion raised for it ([[kino-balam]]) [human: adopted outsider].
