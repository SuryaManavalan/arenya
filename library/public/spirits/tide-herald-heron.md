# Tide-herald Heron

Seven-foot, parking-garage-grey heron spirit, white coin eye; the viral Candlestick clip was "too small to hold" it. Each step leaves a tideline. It came for Susmithaa's light in the canister.

## Details
- Stats at table: AC 13, 45 HP; attack *Low Tide* (2d6 cold; DC 13 Will save, pull 15 ft, restrained; recharge 5–6). [#1023, #1041, #1074]
- Behavior: grey eyelid shuts like a filing drawer; "tick" like a turn signal; bows like a clerk to a man he has "filed" — it did so twice to Abernathy. [#1029, #1060]
- Marked by Night's Mark, burned throat, cracked eye, raked shin; broken by Kino's bite (9/45). Spared by Arenya, who sent a message: Susmithaa is an open entry, borrowed at Ernesto Lucero's feet, its master may come and ask with manners. It bowed "received" (one tick) and sank into the sea. [#1101–#1104]
- The vast coastline-sized thing offshore breathed with its last Low Tide. [#1074]
- Its master is unknown; a bright film of Arenya's Burn the Veil flowed NE toward the industrial Bay shore, suggesting something inland pulls the tide.
