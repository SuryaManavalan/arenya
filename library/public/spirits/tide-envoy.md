# The Tide Envoy

A tall, wet-dark spirit in a coat "mostly tide," sent up Ocean Beach creek at dawn Saturday (Sat 7 Nov, 6:12 a.m.) by an unnamed sender. It leaves no prints. It is not the slack-water spirit's own creature: the tide-woman says it is "an envoy, sent ahead of something that wants a door." [#1447]

## Details
- Speaks in a voice "of many small voices politely taking turns." Its mouth is not quite where a mouth should be. Bows too deep, and holds the bow. [#1447]
- Keeps "the sea's clock," not the Mill's and not the party's. Its clock is set to **11:40 p.m. Sunday**. [#1454]
- **The offer:** one holder, one breath, one name. The holder holds a door for a single breath on Sunday night so that the Missing Man's true name can be spoken into [[the-throat]]. In return it gives the box's route and the hour the Chair's keeper looks away. [#1454]
- **The cost (stated plainly, "the only thing I own"):** the holder's pulse drops to a sleeper's; the lips go the colour of the inside of a shell; afterward the holder cannot say their own name until the next tide turns, about twelve hours. [#1462]
- **Sender:** named only as "the one the creek runs to." It has "a long way to come, and a short time to do it." [#1462]
- Abernathy's cuff goes cold when the envoy speaks of "him," and colder at its silence. Abernathy: "It isn't a warning. It's recognition, and that's worse." [#1450, #1454, #1462]
- Its hem ran upstream, toward the city, while it was pressing the terms; the tide in it stopped running that way once Kino refused. [#1454, #1462]
- Refused by Kino for the Lady's house, and refused outright by Caleb ("nobody holds a breath for me"). Its bow to Caleb is final: it wrote his no into the water and dissolved. [#1461, #1463]
- Left behind: an **eighth kelp strand** beside the other seven, knotted "loosely, around nothing." A line in Arenya's notebook, in a hand she did not write: *"He will be at the Chair's door first. Choose who is there."* [#1463]

## b1.c20.s03 "The Name in the Ear" (Sat 7 Nov, 7:12–7:19 a.m., creek mouth) [#1467–#1481]
- The envoy's terms return to Caleb's heel through the seventh strand: "In your ear, before the breath. Only yours. The sender agrees. Choose by Sunday dusk, or the door goes to a different mouth." Deadline now **Sunday dusk** for the answer; the box still moves Sunday 11:40 p.m. [#1467]
- Caleb recited the terms word for word. He refused to answer until he has slept (he has not slept since Tuesday). Caleb's pulse fell from 118 to 104 under Ava's count. [#1472, #1480, #1481]
- The eighth strand's sand message (not the envoy's voice): the name goes to the drydock mouth, not the Chair; the keeper looking away is the price asked of the Chair. [#1474]
- Nothing was signed. The party agreed: no answer at 118 bpm. [#1476, #1480]
