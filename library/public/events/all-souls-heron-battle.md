# All Souls — the Heron battle

Mon Nov 2, 4:50–5:14 p.m., Holy Cross Cemetery, Colma, section L (the Lucero plot). First real astral battle; Arenya awakens as a Spirit Host.

## Details
- Morning: All Souls Mass at [[our-lady-of-mercy-daly-city]]; Nana wrote "Susmithaa Sowri" on the parish dead list in her own hand; the priest said it correctly. The canister sat on its white cloth on the pew all Mass. [#0991]
- Adonis Quinn's black granite stone (Holy Cross) held a folded printout under a grey pebble: sensor trace "LANTERN · BENCH 3 · CH2 · 10/31 23:40–23:58 PDT", three peaks bracketed 11.0 s apart; on the back a note "Ado — It's eleven seconds. It was always eleven… You wrote WHERE FROM and I thought you meant the money. I'm sorry I didn't come in May. — T." Read by Arenya (sleeve over fingers), photographed by Ava, put back squared on the dry grass. Groundskeeper: a man came at 6:30 a.m. in the dark, a big white electric truck, sat an hour on the wet grass in a good coat, left a parking-lot pebble. (Arenya/Kino infer T. = Typhus; unconfirmed.) [#0998–#1003]
- Amma's question: does giving the ashes to the sea give them to "the other thing"? Tenth-day custom: moving water (her father's ashes at Visakhapatnam). Nana offered Ernesto Lucero's feet, *prestado*, "he was always good with guests." Arenya: borrowed until the water is clean; she will drive Amma to the sea herself. The fog's breath came 2 s late (13 count) and the cloth corner pointed at the Bay. [#1010–#1011]
- Turf lifted, canister buried at Ernesto's feet under marigolds. The breath stopped; fog poured over the ridge; seawater beaded in the carved names; a rift opened across section L. A hatchback family fled. [#1022]
- Arenya's spoken promise "counted": candles flared white, canister glowed through the soil, and the land breathed on her count. She awakened: level 2, Ascent 3, light by blood + dark by birthplace. See [[arenya-sowri]]. [#1022–#1023]
- Bulk: Colma as a city of the dead in lamplight, graves as lit windows; the fog a black sea; something "the size of a coastline" watching from beyond the ridge. [#1022]
- Foes: [[tide-herald-heron]] and four [[foam-hands]]. Single initiative for both clocks (rift shallow). [#1023]
- Notable beats: Abernathy drew the heron's eye; it bowed to him twice with a *tick* and he briefly wore a high-collared, brass-buttoned lamp-house livery, which held the lamp against the tide; Kino stepped into the bulk as the land's jaguar; Squire's Word gold dice passed Abernathy→Arenya and Abernathy→Kino; Arenya's body took a critical frost hit (2/9 HP) from the foam-hand at her pulse; Nana salted Arenya's wrist ("Los vivos primero") over the canister; Ava (palm hot as a stovetop) and Salt freed the canister; Malachi cut the last kelp; Ava stabilized Arenya (scarf, case report); Kino's bite broke the heron. [#1026–#1101]
- Arenya refused the killing Lance ("you don't kill the only witness") and sent the heron home with her message; her Burn the Veil failed (saved), but the light film flowed NE against the wind toward the industrial Bay shore. The heron bowed "received" and sank. [#1102–#1104]
- Aftermath: Susmithaa's lamp warm in the canister, Arenya heard her laugh (Amma: "She always laughed at the wrong time"); Appa's stain shrank to a crescent; fog back to 11 s on its own side of Mission Road. Malachi's read: Hunters Point or Brisbane side. Caleb: Drop Two is a Peninsula lot with a roll-up door (not claimed to be the same). Ava invites Arenya to sleep at hers. [#1104–#1114]
