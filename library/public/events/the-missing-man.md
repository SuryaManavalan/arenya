# The Missing Man

A public mass event on Friday (in-world Fri 6 Nov, 5:41 p.m.). Sixty-odd riders walked off the T Third platforms at Bayview toward the bay after three trains passed in silence. Phone clips went up by 5:50; the crowd routes around a tall gap that autofocus will not hold. Name coined on Reddit before the evening news. [#1437]

## Details
- **Time:** 5:41 p.m., Friday. Ava's kitchen clock (the only one she trusts that night) logged it at 5:41:00. [#1442]
- **Crowd:** about sixty riders walked to the shore, "calm as people who've remembered something terrible left at home." [#1437]
- **Rescue:** SFFD pulled nine people from the India Basin shallows. None dead; twenty-two treated for "acute dissociation." [#1437]
- **The gap:** a tall, unholding presence in the crowd that people walk around without seeing. Abernathy: "Nothing in him to bargain with." [#1440]
- **Companion:** a doorway lit in the Lamp that walks along its seam, which Abernathy says is not after Arenya but after "whatever's left in the bag." He recognizes its walk and will not guess whose. [#1440] (Unresolved.)
- **Same minute, elsewhere:** Ava's oven light died for 11 seconds (no surge, no breaker); Arenya's Lamp went cold mid-sentence; gulls on the Crissy/Kino roofline faced east silently; every driver at the Bayline gate on Tunnel Ave turned toward the water. [#1437, #1442]
- **Bayline tie:** the lead D-series driver (plate 8ZK-41) pulled out of line at shift change and mouthed "Chair" through the sedan glass, eyes Crown-glass. [#1444]

- **b1.c20.s02 (Sat dawn):** a [[tide-envoy]] names the Missing Man only as "the man who crossed the platform." The throat wants his true name spoken into it, by a holder, on Sunday 11:40 p.m. The envoy warns "he will be at the Chair's door first." [#1454, #1463]

- **b1.c20.s04 (Sun dusk, the Mission flat):** the doorway that walks the Lamp's seam crosses the kerb two blocks down and comes up to the flat. Its approach matches Kino's steps on the stairs. It knocks Team Four's loading-door cadence (tap, pause, tap-tap) at the front door. Caleb recognizes the cadence as Team Four's, from Hale and Pruitt. Questioned by name, it slides a [[door-receipt-pier-70]] under the door and steps back to the kerb. Abernathy: "I know how that step goes," but withholds whose. [#1497, #1501, #1508]
