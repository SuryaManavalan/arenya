# Typhus's arc

- Not evil: an accelerationist who cares about *humanity* more than humans; detached from body and animal nature [human].
- Fusing exhausted traditional tech with spiritual tech; his projects are all one tangle [human].
- Initially unaware he causes the **rifts**; learns mid-Book 1, is scared, then embraces it as destiny [human].
- Uses spirits as power sources for interplanetary vessels [outline]. The rendering is what tears the rifts [dm-notes].
- Does **not** know his program killed cousin Adonis; learning it is a Book 1 turning point [dm-notes].
- Uses global influence to paint spirits and Arenya as evil; ends Book 1 imprisoned, warning of impending doom and saying he was protecting Earth (must hit) [outline].
- Later: escapes (Book 4); contacted by the void angel (Book 2) [outline].
