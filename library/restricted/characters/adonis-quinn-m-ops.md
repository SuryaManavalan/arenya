# Adonis Quinn — M-Ops tenure (restricted)

Human-confirmed truth (#1458). Not known to the characters as of b1.c20.s02.

## Details
- Adonis held the **M-Ops chair** from **March to 14 August** of this year. The chair is the appointment described in [[m-ops-appointment]].
- During that tenure he found the **WHERE FROM?** accounting in the Mill's books, tore up his own appointment, and was killed for it. His death is the freezing death of [[adonis-quinn]] (b1.c08).
- Typhus reads that same log during Sunday's heist (the b1 climax run) as his **step 4**. Typhus did not know the program killed Adonis (human #0027); the log is how he learns the why.
- The "WHERE FROM?" phrase matches the note found under Adonis's pebble at Holy Cross (b1.c18.s02, [[all-souls-heron-battle]]).
- The chair has been empty since 14 August, which matches the "empty since summer" line in [[m-ops-appointment]].
