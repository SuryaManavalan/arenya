# The angels (Books 2–6; spoilers)

- **Void angel**: contacts Typhus (B2); finds its "true vessel" (B5); regains the republic and pursues void-harnessing tech; the void consumes all (B6). Its previous vessel was destroyed by the void.
- **"Good angel"**: whispers in Arenya's mind; freed in B5; Arenya hosts it, Abernathy's sacrifice saves her spirit; it is destroyed; she is later reborn with its power (B6).
- **The creator**: believes void is better than suffering; Arenya shows him love. Link to Malachi TBD.
