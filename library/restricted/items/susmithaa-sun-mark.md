# The sun mark

A small circle with a crown of quick rays. Susmithaa signed every birthday card to Arenya with it, and the kettle-pad farewell note. [#0189]

## Details
- Rose like a watermark under the Counter's cold 12 in Arenya's palm (seen by Abernathy), then sank back. [#0181, #0208]
- To Abernathy, the sun on the note is *lit*, the lamp's colour: the same mark, same hand, "sent" not merely signed. [#0190, #0193]
- It triggered Abernathy's fragment (hot brass, lamp-smoke, his own older voice): **"Mark it so it can be found again."** When Arenya later asked, he found the memory behind a clean wall, "like somebody tidied up after me." [#0190, #0208, #0209]
- DM working theory: a Sahasrajyoti mark Abernathy once made. **Human decree (#0207): Abernathy's past stays sealed until the Time Masters appear in later books**; in Book 1 he honestly does not know. [#0207, #0210]
