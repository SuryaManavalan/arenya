# Collection program

**[human]** Assassins collect powerful spirits for esoteric cybernetic tech that grinds them into usable physical and astral power; easiest route is via powerful hosts. Victims died because their **astral bodies were killed**.

**[dm-proposed]** details:
- **Collection teams**: meta mages in fours, made forgettable by the **Crown** (neural-lace implant, hypnotic flattening). **Vesper** (injected/inhaled; "every dose is part of a spirit") opens a borrowed channel to the bulk. Long-term users are forgettable.
- Teams kill the host's astral body in the bulk and capture the freed spirit in a containment lattice. Recruited via a Monolith subsidiary ("the Lattice Program").
- Spirits go to **the Mill**, a Monolith facility (location unknown, dm-free), to be *rendered* into power for interplanetary drive research and Vesper.
- Victims were hosts: Yuri Melnikov (heavy house, architect—gravity/structure); others various. Clumsy: sometimes kills hosts whose spirits are too small to keep.
- **Adonis Quinn**: latent sharp-house host who found Mill power-accounting records; recognized his killer as a Monolith colleague (rage, not fear).
- Run beneath Typhus by an unnamed program director, insulating him.
- **Susmithaa** was the biggest prize (deity-class light); collection failed as the bloodline tantra passed Sahasrajyoti to Arenya. The program now wants Arenya.
- **Caleb Strand** was on Susmithaa's team; her discharge burns out his Crown.
