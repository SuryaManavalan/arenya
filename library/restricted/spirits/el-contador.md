# El Contador

A clerk-spirit of the Colma valley, born of the 1912 removal of San Francisco's dead (150,000+) to Colma and the years of ledger-keeping that followed. DM-invented, treated as provisional; human has not yet ruled on whether it is a land spirit in the Sleeping Lady's family, or on any deeper Lattice bargain (#0199 items 1–3 open).

## Details
- Origin: cemetery relocation 1912 onward; clerks recorded who/from where/to which plot, by lamplight, in a shed by the stone yard that smells of wet granite and ink. The clerks died; the counting did not stop. Named *el Contador* by Nana's grandmother's stonecutting people. [#0198]
- Nature: neither devil nor saint, "a clerk." Records who is dead in the valley and who is going to be. Scrupulously fair; "never once in a hundred years made a mistake in its sums." Nobody prays to it. Never crosses out. [#0198, #0203]
- Methods seen: writes in a cold grey bookkeeper's hand that rises "from down," below the floor and the bulk; wrote **12** with a ruled line (a sum line, "Entered") on Arenya's right palm at 2:47 a.m. [#0175, #0179, #0181]. Logged Ix at the Colma gates "like a toll booth logs a plate" without chasing it. [#0184]
- Grey "beneficiary" men read its books and collect (Lattice, front "Meridian Mutual Life"); the entity itself is not shown to be their ally. DM lean: the Lattice exploits it, the Counter doesn't care, but it will honor a properly made contest. [#0198, #0199]
- A witnessed written **no** (notary declaration plus a copy under the shed door with salt and a candle) is *entered*; it "buys time, not freedom" (Susmithaa's bought 7 months, March–October). It cannot be unwritten, even by the grey men; they can only pressure the speaker to give their full name in a room they chose. [#0198, #0202]
- Second salt mark: see [[dos-testigos]].
- Mundane layer: Colma, California, the town of cemeteries (more dead than living); stone yards, flower shops, notary office (Susmithaa's declaration, 19 March, "by the flower shop").

## Stone-yard visit (b1.c15.s09)
- The shed stands: corrugated tin at the back of [[colma-stone-yard]], door facing away from the street, pale salt crust under the gap; tin above wiped clean, recently used by someone careful. Pen sound "since 1912." [#0215, #0226, #0229]
- The yard is ruled like a ledger page: a faint grey line at identical height across every blank. Twelve rows run to the shed (the "heading," where row 13 would be); a margin file of narrower blanks stands along the left fence. [#0226]
- Row 11 margin: Susmithaa's old salted chisel stroke; line dips there ("entered and argued with"). Row 12 margin: polished, only finished face in the yard, one pencil stroke, a witness of one awaiting a second. [#0226]
- Voice at the wall is the same dry voice as Hale's voicemail (under Hale's): "…once, entered. Once, contested. Sostenido por una. Doce. Testigo: una. Falta una." Silently asked Kino *¿Y tú?* [#0229]
- Kino refused to answer by word or gesture, and looked to Arenya ("not mine to give"). The clerk followed the look and made a tick: **referred**. A pencil dot (clerk's "come back to this") now sits beside the rule under the 12 in Arenya's palm. [#0230, #0232]
- At the gate it ruled a blank line for Abernathy/Ix: *not yet*, puzzled, not hostile. [#0217]
- Heard to be unhostile and procedural; attention now rests on Arenya.
