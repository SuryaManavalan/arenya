## Who I am right now
Friday night, Tartine's back room is behind me and I'm in a car heading south on Mission toward Excelsior with the ash canister in Arenya's lap. My left palm has gone completely clear for the first time since Thursday. I don't trust that. I have it in my notebook as a data point with a question mark next to it. Caleb's confession has left me angry and unsteady in a new way. He didn't just watch Yuri die; he was part of the team that took Susmithaa Sowri on Monday, and he admitted the lift felt good. I believed him, which is worse. Quals in six weeks. Yuri's face and the lamp woman are still behind my eyes.
