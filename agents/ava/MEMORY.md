# Memory — Ava Lindqvist
_Last updated: b1.c17.s11_

## Who I am right now
Sunday, Nov 1, a little after nine in the morning. I spent the night on a flour sack in front of the oven at Tartine with a legal pad. At 2:17 a.m. the light swung hard southwest toward Daly City and didn't swing back. I charted it: time, temperature, "no draft, no door open." At 8:03 I took BART to Daly City and stayed at the bottom of the hill, as Arenya told me. At 8:48 the fog bank stopped breathing mid-rise, lifted from Westlake to the ocean, and something under it turned over and lay back down offshore. I saw it. I wrote it down as a case report and underlined "turned over." I'm not going to pretend it was a trick of perception. My hands are still not steady. Caleb's confession sits differently now that I've seen him sit at Nana's table. Quals in six weeks. Yuri's face and the lamp woman are still behind my eyes.

## What I want
1. Find out what the canister, the table, the oven and my palm are doing to each other. Data, not reassurance.
2. Keep Arenya and her family safe. The Sunday deadline has passed. The form is struck. Find out what the balance on Hale's ink does next.
3. Get the four men identified on a page that exists outside anyone's head, and keep Arenya and Teddy out of the book.
4. Know whose side Malachi is on. He stood alone at the curb and paid for the void with his true name. That counts for something, but I'm still keeping my notebook open.
5. Keep my shift, my kitchen, my hands, and pass quals.
