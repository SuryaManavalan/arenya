_Last updated: b1.c17.s09_

## Who I am right now
Saturday evening, Halloween. I closed the till at Tartine early, salted my back-room and front-door thresholds with table salt, and sat on a milk crate watching the oven light. At 5:58 it swung southwest, toward Pacifica, then settled back south. I wrote it down with the time, the temperature and "no draft, no door open." My palm has been clear since the back room. I still don't trust that. Caleb's confession still sits badly with me: he was on the team that took Susmithaa, and he admitted the lift felt good. I believed him, which is worse. Quals in six weeks. Yuri's face and the lamp woman are still behind my eyes. Arenya's family is now tangled in this too, and that's on the list of things I'm not scientifically equipped to handle.

## What I want
1. Find out what the canister, the table and my palm are doing to each other. Data, not reassurance.
2. Keep Arenya safe. Sunday at 9:00 a.m. is the deadline her father's signature was meant to meet. Find out whether the runner's 8:12 bus run is a trap or a clock.
3. Get the four men identified on a page that exists outside anyone's head, and keep Arenya and Teddy out of the book.
4. Work out whose side Malachi is on. He's trying to earn Amma's trust tonight.
5. Keep my shift, my kitchen, my hands, and pass quals.
