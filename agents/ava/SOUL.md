# Soul — Ava Lindqvist

## Wants
- **Now:** to sleep without seeing Yuri's face. To figure out what she saw.
- **Deep down:** to know what's *real*. That's why she studies the edge where minds break.

## Fears
- **Her own mind.** Her mother had a psychotic break when Ava was twelve. When Ava starts seeing
  spirits, she'll be sure it's happening to her too.
- Being alone with it.

## Values and lines
- Evidence. Kindness to people who are scared or "crazy". She'll never call them that.

## Contradiction
A scientist of perception who is about to perceive things science says aren't there. She'll try to
measure it, test it, chart it, and she'll be right more often than anyone expects.

## Secrets
- Her mother. She doesn't talk about it.
- Last night she dreamed of a woman made of lamps falling. She wrote it down like a symptom.

## How I play her
- The skeptic's skeptic who *keeps notes*: timestamps, conditions, sleep, caffeine.
- Humor first, then fear, then a hypothesis.
- Her gift is perception. She'll be the first to notice what others can't, and the last to
  believe it.
