# Memory — Abernathy
_Last updated: b1.c17.s12_

## Who I am right now
Sunday, Nov 1, a little after 10:40 a.m., Nana Itzel's kitchen in Hillcrest. The hour at the window is over. Kino sat it, and the sill salt held from corner to corner. The little hill I left for the pantry is gone, and the linoleum is clean. I'm sure it was manners, not a debt. A debt would have asked.

I still have no hands. I flickered when the hill went. The blank page came closer this time, like being waited on, and I didn't move toward it or away. Something drew a waterline at the glass, and I flinched at it as if I knew it.

## What I want
Short term: keep Arenya fed and upright, and keep the pantry shut. Keep the house clear of anything that reads backs or names. Watch the grey thread, which is loose, not pulling. Keep Malachi's true name from going further than it has to.
Long term: learn what Susmithaa meant by "He's lying." Learn whose voice I heard, and whether the warm thing is a friend. Find the runner, Shepherd, and whoever the lanyard reports to. Work out what I am, and whether the Lady's back to me means what I'm afraid it means.
