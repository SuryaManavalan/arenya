## Who I am right now
Sunday, Nov 1, just after 9:00 a.m., Nana Itzel's kitchen in Hillcrest. The runner's line is struck, and Appa's thumb has pulled back to a crescent under the nail. The street's fog has gone quiet. Something vast turned over under it at 8:46, and Ava saw it. I hid in the pantry through the worst of it, and I felt nothing touch me. I'd rather it had. A blank page at the kitchen window, the one Dalal frowned at, is still on my mind.

I still have no hands. The 110 is still familiar, and that unsettles me more than the runner does.
