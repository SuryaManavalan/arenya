# Memory — Abernathy
_Last updated: b1.c15.s05_

## Who I am right now
Susmithaa is dead. Her lamp came with her family to Seton, cracked through the chimney, and Amma wouldn't give it up. I'm bound to Arenya, torn loose from the lamp-woman's side, and my hold on the physical world is thin. I can speak only to her, on `bond`. The lamp is listening, not empty. Her right fist is starting to loosen for the first time, and the warm light in it is still warm. I'm frightened, and I've been hiding it behind the patter.

## What I want
Short term: keep Arenya's hand shut around the flame until we know what it is, keep the wrist mark covered, and get her away from the man at the nurses' station before he asks the right question twice.
Long term: learn what Susmithaa meant. Find the man in the grey jacket. Keep my head down, though that's a lie now and I know it.

## People
- **Susmithaa**: dead. Her last words were a charge: "look after that one. He'll say he's looking after you. *He's lying.*" I can't answer that yet. Amma says she was a sister who kept things from her family. Her last dinner plans were for Itzel, her Thursday friend in Daly City, to bring pozole if she died.
- **Arenya**: Susmithaa's niece and my host. She hid the wrist mark from her mum with a steering-wheel excuse, and she took the phone and the lamp. She's frightened and doing it anyway. I'd die for her, and I still don't know if I'm allowed to say so.
- **Amma**: Susmithaa's sister. Cried into Arenya's shoulder, wouldn't give up the lamp, and remembered the Thursday pozole. I've met her now, at last. She can't see me.
- **Appa (Ravi)**: Arenya's father. Says *okay, okay, okay* to nobody. In March, Susmithaa warned him to deny any niece to an American who came asking.
- **Malachi**: calls himself "Shawani, Department of Energy." He knows about the mark and warned Arenya off doctors and photographs. He isn't a man I'd trust, and he isn't the one I fear. Still to be seen.
- **The American in a suit**: polite, asked for Susmithaa by full name at about 3:40 a.m., before the paperwork. Asked the nurse whether she had a niece. Malachi says he reached the hospital first. Not yet found.
- **The man in the light grey jacket**: the collector. Faceless to the eye, and at the nurses' station at Seton, a man with *no light at all*, like a hole in a fence. I've never seen a living man like that, and I don't know whether I have.
- **The four collectors**: faceless, counting what they can see.
- **Adonis Quinn** (per Malachi): founder's cousin at Monolith. Not met.

## What I know
- Hosts are dying in the city. Their spirits vanish rather than pass on. Nine freeze victims so far. Malachi says Susmithaa did *not* die like them. (s05, from Malachi)
- The collectors use a magnetic net of humming filaments that pulls and snuffs flames. The filament that touched Arenya's wrist "counted" her. The cold coin-sized mark is still there. (s01)
- Susmithaa's light passed to Arenya rather than vanishing. It's warm in her right hand, and it stayed warm past dawn. (s01, s05)
- "Something old is waking. The doors are going to open." I only heard it. (s01, rumor-grade)
- Arenya's Perception at the grey jacket failed. She holds only "left pocket" and "something heavy." (s01)
- Monolith is in this. Malachi says the American and the collectors have a line to it. (s05, from Malachi, unverified)
