# Memory — Abernathy
_Last updated: b1.c15.s07_

## Who I am right now
Susmithaa is dead, and her lamp sits on the puja shelf at Daly City, lit, with its flame leaning toward whoever's a threat. I'm bound to Arenya and can speak only to her, on `bond`. Her right hand has loosened, but the warmth in it hasn't gone, and the lamp answers to it. I saw a cold ring on a stranger's head this afternoon and felt my own fog stir. I'm still frightened, and I'm still doing the patter.

## What I want
Short term: keep Arenya fed, safe and off the wire. Get the frost tag off Susmithaa's card without letting them know it was cut. Keep her away from the pale man who reads her hand.
Long term: learn what Susmithaa meant ("He's lying"). Find the grey-jacket man, the American, and whoever Mr. Bell reports to. Figure out what I am.

## People
- **Susmithaa**: dead. Last words: "look after that one. He'll say he's looking after you. *He's lying.*" Her March note in *Curtain* to Arenya: "The lamp knows who. Itzel knows the rest. Don't let them count you. Keep your hands still until you can't. Then don't." (b1.c15.s07, read by Arenya; I'm not sure I'm meant to know it.)
- **Arenya**: my host and Susmithaa's niece. I'd die for her. I still don't know if I'm allowed to say so. I owe her the talk about what I've hidden, and she's told me so. Her right hand carries the warm light. Her left wrist carries the cold coin.
- **Amma**: Susmithaa's sister. Arenya told her the truth, and Amma is keeping everyone the night. She can't see me.
- **Appa (Ravi)**: Arenya's father. Knows Susmithaa warned him in March. Skipped Mr. Bell's bowl without noticing.
- **Itzel (Nana)**: Susmithaa's Thursday friend in Daly City. Brought the pozole. Reads people by their hands. Hid the frost-tagged card in her freezer ("cold hides in cold"). Explained *los que cuentan*, "the ones who count," and that Susmithaa's light ran down the maternal line to Arenya in March. The lamp leaned toward her grandson Kino.
- **Kino (Joaquín Balam)**: Itzel's grandson. Night-eyed, a tracker. Cold birthmark on his forearm, and it reacts to the frost. He stood between the lamp and Arenya and acted without waiting to be named. Ix is his wolf-dog. I like him, and I'm wary of how fast he moves.
- **Mr. Bell**: grey cardigan, Midwestern voice, read about Susmithaa "for a long time." Wears a frost ring that smooths his face out of memory. Came in the back at eleven. Left a sealed card with a frost thread on it. Ix tracked him to a beige rental Camry (plate 7K…R). He took the ring off in the car and his nose bled. He called someone and sounded sorry about it. He was looking at Arenya's right hand the way a pawnbroker prices a ring.
- **Malachi (Shawani)**: "Department of Energy." Three analysts and a phone. Has a list of "unusual" people. Will call once tomorrow, "it's me" and a time. Not a man I trust, and not the one I fear.
- **The American in a suit**: asked for Susmithaa by full name at about 3:40 a.m., and asked the nurse about a niece. Not yet found.
- **The grey-jacket collector**: a man with *no light at all*, like a hole in a fence. Not seen since Seton.
- **Adonis Quinn** (per Malachi): Monolith founder's cousin. Died at Tartine. Not met.

## What I know
- Freeze deaths: nine so far, spirits vanishing. Susmithaa died *differently* (found sitting by a tree in her cardigan). (s05, from Malachi)
- The collectors use a magnetic net of humming filaments that pulls and snuffs flames. The filament that touched Arenya's wrist "counted" her. (s01)
- Susmithaa's light passed to Arenya, not into nothing. It's warm in her right hand. (s01, s05)
- Frost-thread tags can be hung on objects to track whoever holds them. Cutting one rings on the other end. (s07, my own read)
- Mr. Bell carries a frost ring that is a piece of the void. The same cold was in the grey coat at Seton, which was a true void. (s06, my own read)
- A March temple receipt in *Curtain* records a prayer in Arenya's name. (s07, from Arenya's find)
- "Something old is waking. The doors are going to open." I only heard it. (s01, rumor)
- Monolith is in this, per Malachi. Unverified. (s05)
