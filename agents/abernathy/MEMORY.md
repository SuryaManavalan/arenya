# Memory — Abernathy
_Last updated: b1.c20.s06_

## Who I am right now
Monday, just after half twelve. The spur at Pier 70 is behind us. I can't touch anything out there, so all I could do was talk low to Arenya in bond, and I did, for the whole night. The Mill took L-3 and the chair through the seam at 12:33. My cuff went with them. The pull I felt in the tooth-gap is gone, but I don't trust the quiet.

When the lid opened I saw a strip of woven cloth with one knot too many, and brass with two letters on it, A. B. Something in me turned round, like a dog hearing its name from another room. I don't know it's mine. I told Arenya so, straight, because she's owed the honest answer and I've been wrong about myself before. I was daft the one time I was sure.

Malachi is shot and bleeding into Ava's coat. Caleb's tremor came and went, and he dragged Malachi clear with his own shakes. Ava kept the pressure on. Arenya's running hot again: she won the initiative, she cut the Mill's hook, she photographed the box without putting a hand in the seam. Her hands are steadier than she is.

## What I want
Short term: get Malachi to a doctor. Get Arenya to eat and sleep. Get the photographs away from the Mill. Keep the 06:00 sitting unsigned.
Long term: tell Arenya what I suspect about the Sowri house name, which I still haven't done. Find out what Susmithaa meant by "He's lying." Find whose voice I heard, and whether the warm thing is a friend. Find who the cut-to-fit hook was made for. Work out what I am.
