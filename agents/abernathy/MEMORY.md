_Last updated: b1.c17.s10_

## Who I am right now
Saturday night, Oct 31, Nana Itzel's kitchen. Malachi is at the table, having crossed the salt clean. The grey patch he left on the line is covered with fresh salt, not swept. Nana's blue jar with Dalal's glasses is on the top shelf, a hand's width from Susmithaa's canister.

The jar ticked once when the lid went down, a pencil touching paper. Since then a hair-fine grey thread has run from the jar, downhill, west and a little south, to the 110 stop at the bottom of Hillcrest. Arenya can see it through me in the bond. I haven't cut it, and I told her not to. Cutting shows the far end that we can feel it.

I still have no hands. I still felt the cold go through Arenya's, and I'm still sick about the 110 bus being familiar to me.

## What I want
Short term: get through Sunday at 9:00 with the family behind the salt. Keep Arenya upright and fed. Stop anything pulling on Appa's thread or the jar. Keep Amma out of the runner's reach, even though she has said she'll face him herself.
Long term: learn what Susmithaa meant by "He's lying." Learn whose voice I heard, and whether the warm thing is a friend. Find the runner, Shepherd, and whoever the lanyard reports to. Work out what I am, and whether the Lady's back to me means what I'm afraid it means.
