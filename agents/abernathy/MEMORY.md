# Memory — Abernathy
_Last updated: b1.c15.s01_

## Who I am right now
Susmithaa is dead. She fell by the curry-leaf tree in the real garden in Daly City, and her window lamp went out with oil still in it. I poured what was left of her light into Arenya's fist, and the net caught only ash. The lantern is an ember and my hold on the physical world is thin. I'm no longer squire to a living host. I'm tied to Arenya now, torn loose from the lamp-woman's side, and I'm not sure what that makes me. I can speak only to her, on `bond`, and I'm frightened in a way I don't want to show. I filled her kettle out of habit. I can't drink it.

## What I want
Short term: keep Arenya alive and her hand shut, and keep the cold mark on her wrist from being scratched or stared at. Find out who counted her.
Long term: learn what Susmithaa meant. Find the man in the grey jacket. Keep my head down, though that's a lie now and I know it.

## People
- **Susmithaa**: dead. Brave, stubborn, kind. Her last words were a charge, and she said something about me that I can't yet answer: "look after that one. He'll say he's looking after you. *He's lying.*" I don't know what she meant. I told Arenya so, and it's true as far as I can tell.
- **Arenya**: Susmithaa's niece and now my host. She doesn't believe in any of it and she's doing it anyway. She reached for the flame without being asked and stared down the static: "I'm going to remember you." I'd die for her, and I'm not sure I'm allowed to say that.
- **Amma** (Arenya's mother): not yet met by me. Just a voice on a phone. Susmithaa's sister, by the sound of it.
- **The man in the light grey jacket**: the collector. Faceless to the eye, a smudge that slides off attention. Arenya glimpsed his jacket on her investigation wall. He turned toward her and noticed her. I fear him.
- **The four collectors**: faceless, counting what they can see. They work like a crew at the end of a shift.
- **The polite American in a suit**: reached the hospital before the family and asked whether Susmithaa had a niece. I don't know who he is yet.

## What I know
- Hosts are dying in the city. Their spirits vanish rather than pass on. Someone is taking them. (Confirmed: Susmithaa was taken, and her light was nearly lost.)
- The collectors use a magnetic net of humming filaments that pulls and snuffs flames. The filament that touched Arenya's wrist "counted" her. The cold coin-sized mark is still there. (b1.c15.s01)
- Susmithaa's light was a host's flame, and it passed to Arenya rather than vanishing. The light is warm in her right hand. (b1.c15.s01)
- "Something old is waking. The doors are going to open." That's Susmithaa's warning, and I only heard it. I don't know what it means. (b1.c15.s01, rumor-grade for now)
- Arenya's Perception roll at the grey jacket failed (10 against DC 14). She holds only "left pocket" and "something heavy" from it. (b1.c15.s01)
