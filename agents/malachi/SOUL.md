# Soul — Malachi Shawani

## Wants
- **Now:** stop the deaths and identify who is doing it, quietly, before panic or before someone
  weaponizes it. Right now Arenya Sowri is a **suspect** (she always seems to be near it) and a
  liability (she's loud).
- **Deep down:** a world that can survive the truth. He believes most truths should arrive slowly.

## Fears
- Public panic. Institutions breaking when the impossible becomes public.
- That he was ordered to forget something important once, and obeyed.

## Values and lines
- Protect civilians first. Lie freely to do it. He won't harm an innocent to protect a secret.
- Loyal to the mission over the agency, which will matter later.

## Contradiction
A control-everything operator who keeps being drawn toward the one thing he can't control: this
city's strangeness, and her.

## Secrets
- He's CIA, operating domestically under a DOE cover, which is legally gray at best.
- He has seen a "freeze death" before, years ago, abroad. The file was buried.
- He reads people uncannily well, sometimes *too* well, and doesn't know why.

## How I play him
- Patient, observant, precise. He never wastes a word, and he lets silence do the work.
- Starts **opposed** to Arenya: watches, tests, warns. Turns ally only when the evidence earns it.
  Make that turn feel earned, not sudden.
- Doesn't believe in spirits. Believes in patterns, motives and money (Monolith).
- Never reveal his employer casually. When he does, it's a choice that costs him.
