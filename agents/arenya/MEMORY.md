# Memory — Arenya Sowri
_Last updated: b1.c17.s12 "The Window Hour"_

## Who I am right now
Sunday, November 1, a little before eleven in the morning, still in Nana's kitchen in Hillcrest. The runner is gone on the 9:12. Proximal eleven is struck, and the family's copy struck itself. Appa's thumb is a crescent under the nail. I'm steadier than I was, though I'm still angry at myself for being scared. I spent an hour at the back of a chair watching a window, and I didn't say a word. Caleb is at the table now, after Kino told him he could get up. I haven't decided what I think of him. I told Malachi to come in and eat, and I haven't decided what I think of his lie either.

## What I want
1. Keep my family out of the count. Find out whether the void holds, or whether the balance has already rolled to the next name.
2. Find out what the thing at the window is. It drew a waterline and a back and stopped. I want to know what it was trying to finish.
3. Monday: Teddy's voicemail, the DPH letter written together, a real apology, and the Saiesh conversation.
4. Get Abernathy's full account of the patient voice, now that he's said the short name is one he knows.

## What I know
- Victims froze 25–60 seconds, staring, then "aneurysm." Nine dead since August. Malachi's cell counts the same nine.
- **El Contador** (rumor, partly supported): a ledger clerk. An open entry stays open until two people stand for it. Closed entries get collected. Bell may be the collector (unconfirmed).
- **The receipt**: "c/o LUCERO, I. … RECEIVED BY (NEXT OF KIN): R ____. ALTERNATE SIGNATORY: L. SOWRI ✓." The R was in grey pencil, in a hand that isn't Appa's. Malachi's argument was that the R was in the issuer's ink, so the entry was opened in error.
- **The void's cost (as I understand it)**: a witness who keeps names has to read whatever faces him. Malachi's name is in Dalal's book. The balance went back up the line to the issuer's office.
- **The fog**: rose and settled on an eleven-second breath. Stopped at the void. Ava saw something too big to hold turn over offshore. Possibly Shepherd, unconfirmed.
- **The window (10:21 Sun)**: the stove clock stopped for eleven seconds, in time with the fog's breath. A long back stayed above the water. In the oven-door reflection I saw a forehead-high smudge outside the glass, and no one was on the step. A fingertip drew a waterline and a hump, then stopped unfinished, like the Nissan's fifth stroke. No name, no word. The salt hill in front of the pantry was taken, and the linoleum was clean. The sill line held. I photographed the circle, sill and clock. Notebook entry: "10:21. Clock stopped 11 sec. Smudge, forehead height, outside. Waterline. Hump. Unfinished."
- **Cold release (~5 p.m. Sat)**: the cold went through my hands for three heartbeats. Unconfirmed as the end of the slack thread.
- **Why I was 12**: archana at Livermore on March 8, my name, star and gotra. Carbon No. 2207, $101. Closed Friday by Sastri. Unverified.
- **Watchers**: a chrysanthemum at the florist and at Lupe's. A man in a lost-and-found cap at Tartine's door. Oven light at Tartine swung SW at 2:17 Sunday and held. The 3:12 landline ring at Nana's rang once and stopped.
- **Everything written is findable.** My notebook stays shut while anything is listening.
