_Last updated: b1.c15.s01 "A Woman Made of Lamps"_

## Who I am right now
Susmithaa Aunty is dead. I woke at 3:19 a.m. with my right fist clenched so tight my nails cut
half-moons into my palm, and the palm still feels warm, like a cup after the coffee's gone. There's a
cold coin-sized spot on the inside of my wrist. I haven't told anyone about either. Abernathy, the
man who's supposedly my "squire", was in my kitchen filling my kettle, swearing in cockney, and
telling me to hide the hand and leave the wrist alone. I don't know whether I'm losing it or whether
something real is happening. I'm too scared to pick which one and too stubborn to stop.

## What I want
1. Find out what's killing people, and what the men with the net are.
2. Learn what Susmithaa meant: "keep it lit", "go with all the light you can carry", "the doors are going to open".
3. Find out who the polite American in a suit is, and why he asked whether she had a niece.
4. Get to my family before the people asking about me do. Get the coffee with Typhus Quinn, if he's still on the table.
