# Memory — Arenya Sowri
_Last updated: b1.c17.s11 "All Hallows"_

## Who I am right now
Sunday, November 1, a little after nine in the morning, Nana's kitchen in Hillcrest. The runner is gone on the 9:12 with the balance on his lap. Proximal eleven is struck through on the runner's own paper, and the family's copy struck itself. Appa's thumb has gone from a grey stain to a crescent under the nail. I'm shaky, I'm furious at myself for being scared, and I'm grateful to everyone who stood where they were told. I still haven't decided how I feel about Malachi's lie, though I told him to come in and eat. Caleb confessed in front of Appa that he helped kill Susmithaa. Appa didn't forgive him, but told him to sit at the table, not on the floor. I haven't decided what I think of that either.

## What I want
1. Keep my family out of the count. Find out whether the void holds, or whether the balance has already rolled to the next name.
2. Work out what Dalal read at the kitchen window: a blank page that shouldn't be blank. I haven't said it aloud.
3. Monday: Teddy's voicemail, the DPH letter written together, a real apology, and the Saiesh conversation.
4. Learn what Abernathy heard in the bond, now that the runner has gone.

## What I know
- Victims froze 25–60 seconds, staring, then "aneurysm." Nine dead since August. Malachi's cell counts the same nine.
- **El Contador** (rumor, partly supported): a ledger clerk. An open entry stays open until two people stand for it. Closed entries get collected. Bell may be the collector (unconfirmed).
- **The receipt**: "RETURN OF PERSONAL EFFECTS — CUSTODY RECEIPT. REF: PROXIMAL / 11 … c/o LUCERO, I. … RECEIVED BY (NEXT OF KIN): R ____. ALTERNATE SIGNATORY: L. SOWRI ✓." The R was in grey pencil in a hand that isn't Appa's. The tick beside L. Sowri was there before anyone signed. At 9:0x Sunday the runner struck the entry closed on the family's copy, which struck itself. Malachi's argument was that the R was written in the issuer's ink, so the entry was opened in error and voided.
- **The void's cost (as I understand it)**: a witness who keeps names has to read whatever faces him. Malachi's name is now in Dalal's book, and Dalal knows the Lucero house by sight. The balance goes back up the line to the issuer's office, so somebody else has a bad week.
- **The jar**: Dalal's glasses were in a blue lidded jar. They went onto the wooden comal, Dalal read with them, and they're back on his face now. The jar's thread to the 110 went loose once the bus left.
- **The fog**: rose and settled on an eleven-second breath along the ridge, dimming the sodium lamps. It stopped once, at the moment of the void. Ava saw a shape too big to hold turn over and lie down farther out over the water. Likely Shepherd or something under him, unconfirmed.
- **The 9:12 tick**: the 8:12 tick at bay C moved to 9:12 in heavier pencil without a hand on the glass. Ava logged it.
- **Cold release (~5 p.m. Sat)**: the cold went through my hands for three heartbeats. Unconfirmed as the end of the slack thread.
- **Why I was 12**: archana at Livermore on March 8, my name, star and gotra. Carbon No. 2207, $101. Closed Friday by Sastri. Unverified.
- **Dalal's frown**: a blank page in his book at the kitchen window that "shouldn't be blank". Unknown whether it's me, Amma, Appa, or someone unlisted.
- **Watchers**: a chrysanthemum at the florist and at Lupe's. A man in a lost-and-found cap at Tartine's door. Oven light at Tartine swung SW at 2:17 Sunday and held. Palm-sized wiped circle in the kitchen window condensation, matching the shelter's.
- **The 3:12 landline ring at Nana's**: rang once and stopped. Nobody answered.
- **Everything written is findable.** My notebook stays shut while anything is listening.
