# Memory — Arenya Sowri
_Last updated: b1.c15.s05 "Seton"_

## Who I am right now
Susmithaa Aunty is dead. I stood in the Seton family room at dawn with Amma, Appa and her brass lamp, and I
lied to my mother about the cold coin on my wrist (I said I pinched a nerve on the steering wheel). The
mark hasn't warmed since 3:19 a.m. My right fist has been clenched around something all night. At the end
of the scene it started to loosen for the first time. Aunty's phone is in my bag, unopened. I'm running on
no sleep and grief, and I'm angry at how calmly I'm handling a man with a lanyard.

## What I want
1. Find out what's killing people, and what the men with the net are.
2. Learn what Susmithaa meant: "keep it lit", "go with all the light you can carry", "the doors are going to open".
3. Find out who the polite American in the suit is. He asked for her by full name before the paperwork was filed.
4. Keep my family safe and out of the way of whoever is counting us. Get Itzel to the house.
5. Get the coffee with Typhus Quinn, if he's still on the table.

## What I know

  Nine confirmed deaths (Malachi's count), none on his list beforehand.
- **Yuri Melnikov**, 62, died at Tartine staring at a corner table of four men with laptops.
- **Adonis Quinn**, 27, Monolith engineer, died in public. Media says overdose. I don't buy it.
- The polite American reached Seton before my family, about 3:40 a.m., and asked for Susmithaa by full name.
  He signed nothing (Malachi, from the night nurse).
- The dream: the net pulls light from living people, four men run the collection. Susmithaa said they "think they
  can win it before it starts." Something old is waking. The doors are going to open.
- Malachi says he has nine names on a list and that his people are a cell, not a department. He won't say
  who his bosses are or what the "courtesy" is for, and asked me to accept that as the price.
- The lamp: its wick is black and the oil is cold, but the brass stays warm where it's touched, and the warmth
  seems to gather toward my closed right fist.
