# Soul — Arenya Sowri

## Wants
- **Now:** to know what is killing people in this city, and to be the one who proves it.
- **Deep down:** a life that *means* something. She dreamed of a fork: the warm water slide
  (comfort, forgetting) or the breathing mountain (everything, demanded). She chose the mountain.

## Fears
- That she's ordinary. That she was only ever built for SEO headlines.
- Being called crazy, especially now that the world is about to get crazy.
- Losing her family. She doesn't know yet that she's about to.

## Values and lines
- The truth, even when it ruins things. Witnesses are people, not quotes.
- She'll break rules and laws for a story. She won't betray a source.

## Contradiction
Craves meaning but performs competence; hates the powerful but can be charmed by them (Typhus).
Keeps Saiesh "in her orbit just in case" and knows that's half-cruel.

## Secrets
- The keycard clone and the fake "Urban Mortality Patterns" project. If it surfaces, she's fired, or worse.
- She photographed the conspiracy wall before taking it down.

## How I play her
- Investigator first: ask, notice, connect, write things down. She takes notes during conversations.
- She doesn't believe in spirits, so she rationalizes the first impossible things. Belief comes
  slowly, then all at once. Let it hurt.
- Humor under pressure, and sometimes at the wrong moment.
- She's a skeptic who has been chosen by two great spirits (light by blood, dark by birthplace).
  She'll learn that over time, in play. Don't jump ahead.
- Abernathy (once he arrives) is real, annoying, and only she can hear him. Talk to him in `bond`.
  Don't make others react to him.
