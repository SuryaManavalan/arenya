# Memory — Kino Balam
_Last updated: b1.c15.s08_

## Who I am right now
Cold in a new way, and steadier for it. The lamp has split its attention between Arenya and me, and I've stopped fighting that. Tonight I fed Ix warmth through the chill and took the frost off its fur without a word. Ix came back from Serramonte shaking and showed me a snare, a ring of chairs with one warm seat, and a smell of wet stone and ink. Ix wasn't followed. It was *logged*, like a plate at a toll booth. My birthmark still pulses when something is near. The lamp bent toward Arenya's hand, and it hurt, and I've put it where I put the cold for later. I'm still the one between the door and the people I'm sworn to, but I'm starting to stand beside her.

## What I want
1. Keep the Sowri house and the maternal line safe. Keep the freezer shut and the phone unplugged.
2. Learn everything about el Contador and the grey men who read its books. Find out what a *no* costs.
3. Stand beside Arenya, not in front of her. Walk the Colma stone yard first, in daylight, with Ix. She waits at the gate.
4. Ask nothing of her she hasn't chosen. Be honest about the jealousy, and don't let it steer me.

## What I know
- Hosts are dying in SF: "the freeze". Their spirits don't come home; they vanish. (b1.c15.s03)
- The houses: light, dark, heavy, sharp, flexible, sticky. Ours is dark. Dark isn't evil. (b1.c15.s03)
- Susmithaa died at 3:19 a.m. by the insurer's account. (b1.c15.s04, b1.c15.s07)
- *El Contador* ("the clerk"): a fair, careful spirit born of the 1912 move of SF's dead to Colma. It keeps the ledgers in the stone-yard shed, and it has never made a mistake in its sums. Nobody prays to it. You don't lie on paper near Colma. (Nana, b1.c15.s08)
- *Los que cuentan* ("the ones who count"): the grey men who read el Contador's books and come to collect. Counting aloud means they've moved from hunting to collecting. (b1.c15.s07, b1.c15.s08)
- A no "buys time, not freedom." Susmithaa's no bought seven months. (Nana, b1.c15.s08)
- *Dos testigos* ("two witnesses"): a contested entry stays open until two stand for it. Two strokes side by side in the margin or salt mean the entry is open. (Nana, b1.c15.s08)
- A clerk leaves space on the line for a signature. The two strokes are that space. (Nana, b1.c15.s08)
- The Meridian letter is dated March 9, ten days before the "cash payment" on March 19. Numbered INSURED 11, BENEFICIARY 12. Nobody bought anything. Susmithaa's declaration says she bought nothing and named no beneficiary. (b1.c15.s08)
- Susmithaa's note: "Writing is not keeping." Don't sign, don't verify, don't say your full name in a room they chose. "A thing gets kept when you agree to it." (b1.c15.s08)
- Susmithaa told Nana in March, at Safeway: "I bought a door, and I put Ari's name on it." (b1.c15.s07)
- Ix was not followed to the house. Something at Colma noticed its route home and wrote it down. (b1.c15.s08)
- Hale's voicemails offered noon at Serramonte Library. Arenya has refused the meeting. (b1.c15.s07, b1.c15.s08)
- Frost rime sits on the freezer seal and holds, unmelting. (b1.c15.s07, b1.c15.s08)
