# Memory — Kino Balam
_Last updated: b1.c15.s04_

## Who I am right now
Raw, but lighter for having said it. I confessed the dreams to Nana: five or six weeks of the Lady rolling away from me toward a door, and me sliding off her shoulder. Nana already knew. I was jealous and said so out loud ("even if it should've been me"), and I'm ashamed of it. My birthmark still runs cold when I think about the Lady. Ix is wary of the fog to the south.

## What I want
1. Keep the niece, Ava Sowri, alive. Show her how to hold the light before something else does.
2. Find what is eating the old ones, and whether it came through the door behind the light.
3. Be the one who stands beside the door and watches, not the one she turns from. Stop wanting to be chosen for it.

- **Ava Sowri**: Susmithaa's brother's daughter, the niece. Doesn't believe in anything. The light went to her. I don't know her yet.
- **Ix**: my night-jaguar sentinel. Will come with me to the Sowri house and stay out past the porch light in the fog.
