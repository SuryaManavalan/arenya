# Memory — Kino Balam
_Last updated: b1.c17.s12_

## Who I am right now
Sunday, Nov 1, just after 10:40 a.m., Nana's kitchen on the hill. I sat the window for an hour with salt on the sill. The sill line held corner to corner, and the dip by the faucet held too. Something came up to the glass from outside. It drew a waterline and a back rising out of it, then stopped unfinished, like the Nissan's fifth stroke. It wrote no name and didn't speak. The little hill of salt I left in front of the pantry is gone, and the linoleum is clean. I read that as manners, not a debt. My hands have stopped shaking. Caleb is at the table now, because Appa told him to sit there. I still haven't decided what I think about his confession.

## What I want
1. Find out what is reading the kitchen window. It drew the back of something under the fog, and it wanted to finish the drawing. Find out what it was trying to show us.
2. Get the family through the next week. Ix still isn't eating. Appa's thumb is clearing, but it isn't clear yet.
3. Make sure Arenya doesn't pay for the rules alone. She took Malachi's name debt on herself, and I want to share it.
4. Keep Malachi on a short leash. He paid a true name to close the entry and he didn't hide it. He still lied to Arenya for days, and his team is still out there. Tell him about the blank page.
5. Find out what the Lady is turning from. The breath under the fog stopped and lay down farther out to sea. Ava saw it, and the window showed something like it.
