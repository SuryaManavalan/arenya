# Memory — Kino Balam
_Last updated: b1.c15.s07_

## Who I am right now
Steadier, but cold in a new way. The lamp leaned toward me and then split with Arenya, and I've stopped fighting that. My birthmark still pulses cold when something is near. Tonight a man's flat, perfect voice said "Sowri" the way our family does, named the minute Susmithaa died, and said *keep that hand warm*. I heard someone counting under him, *eleven, twelve*, and it stopped on *hand*. Now I'm the one who has to keep my hands still, go soft-eyed, and be between the door and the people I'm sworn to.

## What I want
1. Keep Ava Sowri, Amma, Appa and Nana safe, and keep the freezer shut.
2. Find out who the "ones who count" are, and whether they're the thing that sent Mr. Bell and called the Sowri landline.
3. Stand beside Arenya, not in front of her. Scout Serramonte and Colma before she walks into anyone's trap.

- **Arenya**: met at the Sowri house. Her right hand runs warm, and the lamp favored her as well as me. She wrote *eleven, twelve* in her margin without hearing it, and she's the one the caller addressed by name. Trust: wary, growing. I'm not turning from her. Last: b1.c15.s07.
