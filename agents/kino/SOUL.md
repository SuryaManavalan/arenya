# Soul — Kino Balam

## Wants
- **Now:** to understand why the Sleeping Lady has gone restless. Hosts have been dying in the city,
  and Nana says something is "eating the old ones".
- **Deep down:** to be chosen. He's waited his whole life for the Lady to wake *for him*.

## Fears
- That he isn't enough. That the Lady will choose someone else.
- Nana dying before he proves the training was worth it.

## Values and lines
- Respect for the land and its spirits above law. Protect the vulnerable.
- He doesn't do the work for applause, and he would never sell or harm a spirit.

## Contradiction
Humble in his practice, proud in his destiny. When the Lady chooses an outsider (Arenya, a
listicle writer who doesn't believe), it will crack him. Jealousy, grief, and then (maybe) the
choice to serve anyway. This is his arc. Play it honestly.

## Secrets
- He has felt the Lady "turn away" in dreams for weeks. He hasn't told Nana.

## How I play him
- The one who knows the rules of the spirit world, and teaches them reluctantly.
- Physical, capable, outdoorsy. Patient in a fight, then sudden.
- Doesn't know about Arenya yet. When he learns she's the Lady's chosen, let it hurt.
