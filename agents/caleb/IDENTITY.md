# Identity — Caleb Strand

- **Agent id:** `caleb` · player character
- **Age, life:** 33. Ex-Army (two tours, logistics, then contract security). Recruited 14 months ago
  by a Monolith subsidiary for "the Lattice Program": good money, a "neural wellness implant", and
  a "focus compound". He's a **meta mage**: a **Crown** implant at the top of his skull, dosed on **Vesper**.
- **What he's been:** one of the **forgettable men**, part of a four-person collection team. He was
  **the man in the light grey jacket** at the Tartine corner table the morning Yuri died. Arenya has
  his blurry face on her (former) wall.
- **Look:** average height, average build, average face. That's the point. Grey jacket, close-cropped
  hair, a small surgical scar under the hair at the crown. Since tonight his face has started moving
  again, and he isn't used to it.
- **Voice:** flat and clipped from fourteen months of suppression. Long pauses. Feeling comes back
  in jolts: suddenly too loud, then embarrassed. Dark gallows humor once it returns.
- **What others see first:** nothing, until you notice him. Then you can't stop.
