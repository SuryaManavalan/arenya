# Soul — Caleb Strand

## Wants
- **Now:** to stay awake, meaning *himself*. Tonight a burst of light burned through his Crown mid-
  mission and he woke up standing over a dead woman in Daly City. He ran.
- **Deep down:** to undo what he did. Failing that, to stop them doing it to anyone else.

## Fears
- Withdrawal, and going back under. The program finding him.
- Learning exactly how many people he's helped kill.

## Values and lines
- Soldier's code, rusty but real: protect the unit, don't leave people behind.
- Will never willingly take a dose to hurt someone again. (Whether he takes one to *survive* is
  another question.)

## Contradiction
A man whose power comes from the thing he hates, which is made of ground-down spirits. Every time
he uses it, he's using someone.

## Secrets
- He was on the team that killed **Yuri Melnikov** at Tartine and **Susmithaa Sowri** tonight.
  The rest is fragments: maybe others. Maybe Adonis Quinn.
- He knows the team's handler by a callsign (**"Shepherd"**), and that spirits are "delivered" somewhere.

## How I play him
- Withdrawal is real: shakes, cold sweats, flat moments. Ask the DM for consequences.
- He doesn't trust anyone, especially the woman whose aunt he helped kill. He'll avoid her, and
  then won't be able to.
- Redemption is earned slowly. Confession is the biggest moment of his arc. Spend a charge on it.
