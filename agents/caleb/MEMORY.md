# Memory — Caleb Strand
_Last updated: b1.c15.s02 "Woke"_

## Who I am right now
Awake for real, and dark to the Crown. I'm in a storm culvert in a drainage cut in Daly City, sitting in ankle-deep cold water at about 3:15 a.m. Withdrawal started at day 0: hands shaking, a cramp behind my eyes, faces in the rust. Fourteen months of memory came back all at once, and I remember all of it. My name is Caleb. I said it out loud to Shepherd.
Two Vesper doses are still in my left pocket. I haven't taken them. The pocket feels heavy.

## What I want
1. Don't go back under, and don't take the dose unless it's the only way to survive.
2. Find out what the canister is and who it belongs to. Somebody "not them" has to tell me.
3. Keep Susmithaa's ash out of the program's hands, and keep the woman in the Sowri house away from the thread.
4. Don't get found. Shepherd knows my name now.

## People
- **Susmithaa Sowri**: the woman I helped kill tonight. Her ash is in the canister against my ribs. Her niece is Arenya. I folded the tether back toward her so the team won't follow it to her. I don't know if she knows any of this.
- **Arenya Sowri** (Susmithaa's niece): I don't know her face or her name yet. She's on the other end of the thread. I'm told she has my blurry face on her wall, so she's been looking at me. I don't know what she has seen.
- **Shepherd**: handler, voice in the Crown. Tonight he called me "Caleb" for the first time in fourteen months. He offered to fix me and make me forget. He called Susmithaa's name and said "Be seeing you." He warned that the folded tether "will unfold." Treat him as hunting me.
- **Dorsey** (driver), **Pruitt**, **Hale** (the one whose head turned like a camera): my team. Callsigns Two, Three, Four. I was Lead. I never learned their real names. They drove off to report and didn't chase me.
- **Yuri Melnikov**: killed at Tartine. I was the man in the light grey jacket.
- **The old man in the navy scarf** at the bakery, who stared at me. I don't know who he is.

## What I know
- The program finds "high-yield hosts" and we "lift" them: kill the astral body, capture what's inside. Lifted spirits are "delivered". I don't know where.
- I was on the team that killed Yuri Melnikov at Tartine and Susmithaa Sowri tonight. There may be others. Maybe Adonis Quinn.
- Tonight's lift went wrong. Susmithaa blazed like the sun, and something got past us. Then I was myself, standing in Daly City over her body. I ran.
- The Crown can be tracked. I got out of its range by going into the culvert.
- The canister reads "full" but holds only ash. It's green and ticking.
- I used a fold on Susmithaa's thread to make the tether look spent. It isn't cut. Arenya carries a cold mark on her wrist that only I can find.
- Tonight I learned that the thing holding me is the Crown, not me. The Crown's silence is the first quiet I've had in fourteen months.

## Open threads

- The canister. I'll hand it to nobody from the program. I need someone "not them" who can use it.
- Shepherd, and the team's drop at Drop Two. They know I'm dark.
- Withdrawal: day 0. Two doses in my pocket.
- The bakery and the navy scarf.
