## Who I am right now
Monday, 12:05 a.m. I'm at the dead end of a rail spur at Pier 70, beside Mill truck D2-04 with its rear door open. The 23:40 hour came and went. The steel door at Bldg 12 opened on a lit nothing and closed unclaimed, and I didn't walk through it. The truck holds a chair bolted to the floor, facing the door, and lockbox L-3 on the seat. I haven't taken a dose and I don't want one. The shakes are in my hands, the scar at my crown is warm, and the hum has its off-beat. The Vesper dropper is still with Ava, due back "Sunday, or never." It's past Sunday now.

5. Keep Arenya out of the water, or at least know where she is when she goes in.
6. Get my hands steady enough to hold something without shaking.
7. Find the grey suit who signed "M," and whoever the green sea-glass ring answers to.
